# Section 1 — Intuisi: Conditioning Mengubah Sample Space

Misalkan random experiment awal mempunyai sample space $S$.

Sekarang kita diberi informasi bahwa event $B$ **sudah terjadi**.

Artinya outcome sebenarnya tidak lagi dapat berada di seluruh $S$; outcome harus berada di dalam $B$.

Secara efektif:

$$
\boxed{
\text{new sample space}=B
}
$$

Jika kita ingin mengetahui probability event $A$ setelah mengetahui $B$ terjadi, yang relevan hanyalah bagian $A$ yang masih berada dalam reduced sample space tersebut:

$$
A\cap B.
$$

Karena total probability pada reduced sample space harus kembali dinormalisasi menjadi 1, kita membagi probability mass dalam intersection dengan probability mass seluruh $B$:

$$
\boxed{
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
}
$$

provided

$$
P(B)>0.
$$

Mental model:

```text
Original sample space S
        ↓
information: B occurred
        ↓
restrict attention to B
        ↓
relevant part of A = A ∩ B
        ↓
normalize relative to P(B)
        ↓
P(A | B)
```

> [!TIP] Feynman Version
> Conditional probability bukan “formula baru yang misterius.”  
> Ia hanya menjawab:
>
> **“Di antara seluruh outcomes yang sudah diketahui berada di $B$, berapa proporsi yang juga berada di $A$?”**

# Section 2 — Textbook Motivation: Tulip Bulbs

Hogg memperkenalkan conditional probability melalui contoh 20 tulip bulbs.

Diketahui:

| | Early $E$ | Late $L$ | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Red $R$ | 5 | 8 | 13 |
| Yellow $Y$ | 3 | 4 | 7 |
| Total | 8 | 12 | 20 |

Tanpa informasi tambahan,

$$
P(R)=\frac{13}{20}.
$$

Tetapi jika diketahui bulb yang terpilih akan bloom early, hanya 8 early bulbs yang relevan.

Dari 8 tersebut, 5 berwarna red.

Maka

$$
P(R\mid E)=\frac58.
$$

Melalui counting:

$$
P(R\mid E)
=
\frac{N(R\cap E)}{N(E)}
=
\frac58.
$$

Karena seluruh 20 bulbs equiprobable,

$$
\frac{N(R\cap E)}{N(E)}
=
\frac{N(R\cap E)/N(S)}{N(E)/N(S)}
=
\frac{P(R\cap E)}{P(E)}.
$$

Sehingga kita memperoleh definisi umum.

# Section 3 — Definisi Formal

## 3.1 Conditional Probability

Untuk events $A$ dan $B$ dengan

$$
P(B)>0,
$$

conditional probability event $A$ given $B$ adalah

$$
\boxed{
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
}.
$$

Dibaca:

> “probability of $A$ given $B$.”

atau:

> “probability bahwa $A$ terjadi, dengan informasi bahwa $B$ telah terjadi.”

---

## 3.2 Reverse Conditioning

Sebaliknya, jika

$$
P(A)>0,
$$

maka

$$
\boxed{
P(B\mid A)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}
}.
$$

Perhatikan bahwa numerator sama:

$$
P(A\cap B),
$$

tetapi denominator berbeda.

Karena itu, secara umum:

$$
\boxed{
P(A\mid B)\ne P(B\mid A)
}
$$

---

## 3.3 Simple Numerical Example

Jika

$$
P(A)=0.4,\qquad
P(B)=0.5,\qquad
P(A\cap B)=0.3,
$$

maka

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{0.3}{0.5}
=
0.6.
$$

Sedangkan

$$
P(B\mid A)
=
\frac{0.3}{0.4}
=
0.75.
$$

Satu intersection, dua conditioning directions, dua denominator berbeda.

> [!BUG] Conditioning Direction Trap
> Kata di sebelah kanan vertical bar menentukan denominator:
>
> $$
> P(A\mid B)
> \Rightarrow
> \text{divide by }P(B).
> $$

# Section 4 — Conditional Probability sebagai Reduced Sample Space

Interpretasi geometris / Venn sangat penting.

Suppose:

$$
P(A)=0.7,\qquad
P(B)=0.3,\qquad
P(A\cap B)=0.2.
$$

Setelah diketahui $B$ terjadi, seluruh probability mass $0.3$ pada $B$ dianggap sebagai “100%” dari universe baru.

Bagian dari $B$ yang juga termasuk $A$ adalah $0.2$.

Maka:

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{0.2}{0.3}
=
\frac23.
$$

### Renormalization View

Original:

$$
P(B)=0.3.
$$

Conditional universe:

$$
P(B\mid B)=1.
$$

Scale factor:

$$
\frac{1}{P(B)}.
$$

Jadi semua mass di dalam $B$ diperbesar secara proporsional oleh factor

$$
\frac1{P(B)}.
$$

In particular:

$$
P(A\cap B)
\longrightarrow
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
$$

---

# Section 5 — Conditional Probability dari Equally Likely Counts

Jika original outcomes equally likely, maka

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{N(A\cap B)}{N(B)}.
$$

Perhatikan denominator:

$$
\boxed{N(B)}
$$

bukan $N(S)$.

### Example

Sebuah fair die dilempar sekali.

Let:

$$
A=\{\text{even}\}=\{2,4,6\},
$$

$$
B=\{X>3\}=\{4,5,6\}.
$$

Given $B$, reduced sample space adalah:

$$
\{4,5,6\}.
$$

Event $A\cap B$:

$$
\{4,6\}.
$$

Maka

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{2}{3}.
$$

Equivalent formula:

$$
P(A\cap B)=\frac26=\frac13,
$$

$$
P(B)=\frac36=\frac12,
$$

jadi

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{1/3}{1/2}
=
\frac23.
$$

> [!DANGER] Conditional Sample-Space Trap
> Setelah conditioning, denominator probability/count harus berasal dari **conditioning event**, bukan original sample space.

---

# Section 6 — Conditioning Tidak Membutuhkan Equally Likely Outcomes

Miller secara eksplisit menekankan bahwa definisi

$$
P(B\mid A)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}
$$

tidak bergantung pada equiprobability.

Equiprobability hanya membantu pada contoh counting awal.

### Textbook-style Loaded Die Example

Suppose loaded die probabilities:

$$
P(1)=\frac29,\quad
P(2)=\frac19,\quad
P(3)=\frac29,\quad
P(4)=\frac19,\quad
P(5)=\frac29,\quad
P(6)=\frac19.
$$

Let:

$$
A=\{4,5,6\},
$$

event “greater than 3”, dan

$$
B=\{1,4\},
$$

event “perfect square”.

Then:

$$
A\cap B=\{4\}.
$$

So:

$$
P(A\cap B)=\frac19,
$$

and

$$
P(A)
=
\frac19+\frac29+\frac19
=
\frac49.
$$

Hence:

$$
P(B\mid A)
=
\frac{1/9}{4/9}
=
\frac14.
$$

Poin utamanya:

> Reduced sample space dapat memiliki outcomes dengan probability weights yang tidak sama; kita tetap melakukan **renormalization**, bukan raw counting.

---

# Section 7 — Conditional Probability Memenuhi Probability Axioms

Hogg menunjukkan bahwa, untuk fixed conditioning event $B$ dengan $P(B)>0$, fungsi

$$
A\mapsto P(A\mid B)
$$

sendiri bertindak sebagai probability function.

### Non-negativity

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
\ge0.
$$

### Normalization pada reduced sample space

$$
P(B\mid B)
=
\frac{P(B\cap B)}{P(B)}
=
1.
$$

### Additivity

Jika $A_1,A_2,\ldots$ mutually exclusive, maka

$$
P\left(\bigcup_i A_i\mid B\right)
=
\sum_i P(A_i\mid B).
$$

Ini memperkuat interpretation bahwa conditioning pada $B$ menghasilkan **probability model baru di dalam $B$**.

---

# Section 8 — Multiplication Rule

Mulai dari definisi:

$$
P(B\mid A)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}.
$$

Kalikan kedua sisi dengan $P(A)$:

$$
\boxed{
P(A\cap B)
=
P(A)P(B\mid A)
}.
$$

By symmetry of intersection,

$$
A\cap B=B\cap A,
$$

sehingga juga:

$$
\boxed{
P(A\cap B)
=
P(B)P(A\mid B)
}.
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
P(A)P(B\mid A)
=
P(B)P(A\mid B)
=
P(A\cap B)
}.
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] Interpretation
> Untuk menghitung probability “$A$ **dan** $B$”, kita dapat:
>
> 1. masuk ke $A$ dulu, lalu menghitung chance $B$ setelah $A$;
> 2. atau masuk ke $B$ dulu, lalu menghitung chance $A$ setelah $B$.

---

# Section 9 — Sequential Sampling

Conditional probability muncul secara natural ketika outcomes sebelumnya mengubah probabilities berikutnya.

## 9.1 Without Replacement

Sebuah shipment berisi 240 TVs, 15 defective.

Dua sets dipilih successively without replacement.

Let:

- $A$: first set defective;
- $B$: second set defective.

Then:

$$
P(A)=\frac{15}{240}.
$$

Given first defective, tersisa:

- $239$ TVs;
- $14$ defective.

Therefore:

$$
P(B\mid A)=\frac{14}{239}.
$$

By multiplication rule:

$$
P(A\cap B)
=
\frac{15}{240}\cdot\frac{14}{239}.
$$

Textbook result:

$$
P(A\cap B)=\frac{7}{1912}.
$$

---

## 9.2 With Replacement vs Without Replacement

Draw two aces successively from a standard deck.

### Without replacement

$$
P(\text{two aces})
=
\frac4{52}\cdot\frac3{51}
=
\frac1{221}.
$$

### With replacement

After first card is returned, composition kembali seperti awal:

$$
P(\text{two aces})
=
\frac4{52}\cdot\frac4{52}
=
\frac1{169}.
$$

> [!BUG] Replacement Trap
> Jangan mempertahankan denominator/numerator yang sama setelah draw without replacement.
>
> State changes:
>
> $$
> 52\to51,\qquad
> 4\to3.
> $$

---

# Section 10 — Chain Rule untuk Tiga Events

Miller memperluas multiplication rule menjadi:

Jika

$$
P(A\cap B)>0,
$$

maka

$$
\boxed{
P(A\cap B\cap C)
=
P(A)
P(B\mid A)
P(C\mid A\cap B)
}.
$$

Derivation:

$$
P(A\cap B\cap C)
=
P((A\cap B)\cap C)
$$

$$
=
P(A\cap B)P(C\mid A\cap B)
$$

dan

$$
P(A\cap B)
=
P(A)P(B\mid A).
$$

Substitute:

$$
P(A\cap B\cap C)
=
P(A)P(B\mid A)P(C\mid A\cap B).
$$

### Example — Three Defective Fuses

Box contains 20 fuses, 5 defective.

Select 3 without replacement.

Probability all 3 defective:

$$
P
=
\frac5{20}
\cdot
\frac4{19}
\cdot
\frac3{18}.
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
P=\frac1{114}
}.
$$

---

# Section 11 — General Sequential Logic

Untuk successive events $A_1,\ldots,A_k$, multiplication structure berkembang sebagai:

$$
P(A_1\cap A_2\cap\cdots\cap A_k)
$$

$$
=
P(A_1)
P(A_2\mid A_1)
P(A_3\mid A_1\cap A_2)
\cdots
P(A_k\mid A_1\cap\cdots\cap A_{k-1}).
$$

Miller menyatakan bahwa extension dari three-event rule ke $k$ events straightforward.

> [!TIP] Exam Method
> Untuk sampling without replacement, sering lebih cepat berpikir langsung sebagai sequence of conditional probabilities daripada membangun full sample space.

---

# Section 12 — Temporal Order vs Logical Conditioning

Conditional probability tidak harus berarti “event sebelumnya menyebabkan event berikutnya”.

$P(A\mid B)$ hanya berarti:

> probability $A$ ketika informasi $B$ diketahui.

Textbook Miller menekankan bahwa temporal order tidak wajib.

Contoh:

> “Probability bahwa first card adalah ace **given** second card adalah ace.”

Conditioning event dapat merujuk pada informasi tentang outcome yang secara waktu datang kemudian.

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
\text{conditioning} \ne \text{causation}
}
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
\text{conditioning} \ne \text{necessarily temporal order}
}
$$

# Section 13 — Worked Examples

## Example 1 — Direct Formula

Given:

$$
P(A)=0.60,
\qquad
P(B)=0.50,
\qquad
P(A\cap B)=0.30.
$$

Find $P(A\mid B)$.

### Setup

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
$$

### Calculation

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{0.30}{0.50}
=
0.60.
$$

### Verification

Conditional probability lies in $[0,1]$.

---

## Example 2 — Recover Joint Probability

Given:

$$
P(A)=0.40,
\qquad
P(B\mid A)=0.75.
$$

Find $P(A\cap B)$.

Use multiplication rule:

$$
P(A\cap B)
=
P(A)P(B\mid A).
$$

Thus:

$$
P(A\cap B)
=
0.40(0.75)
=
0.30.
$$

---

## Example 3 — Reverse Direction Is Not Automatic

Given:

$$
P(A)=0.80,
\qquad
P(A\cap B)=0.72.
$$

Then:

$$
P(B\mid A)
=
\frac{0.72}{0.80}
=
0.90.
$$

But $P(A\mid B)$ cannot be computed unless $P(B)$ is also known:

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{0.72}{P(B)}.
$$

> [!BUG] Reverse-Conditional Trap
> Knowing $P(B\mid A)$ does **not** determine $P(A\mid B)$ by simply reversing the notation.

---

## Example 4 — Reduced Sample Space from Counting

Suppose a population contains:

| | Event $A$ | Not $A$ | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $B$ | 18 | 12 | 30 |
| $B^c$ | 20 | 50 | 70 |
| Total | 38 | 62 | 100 |

Given that $B$ occurred:

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{18}{30}
=
0.6.
$$

Do **not** use:

$$
\frac{18}{100}.
$$

The denominator must be the conditioning universe.

# Section 14 — CF2 Calculation Workflow

Gunakan urutan ini hampir selalu:

## Step 1 — Define events

Misalnya:

$$
A=\{\text{claim is fraudulent}\},
$$

$$
B=\{\text{claim receives manual review}\}.
$$

## Step 2 — Identify conditioning direction

If question asks:

> “probability fraudulent **given manual review**”

target adalah:

$$
P(A\mid B),
$$

bukan $P(B\mid A)$.

## Step 3 — Check conditioning probability

Need:

$$
P(B)>0.
$$

## Step 4 — Find joint/intersection

Determine:

$$
P(A\cap B).
$$

Ini bisa berasal dari:

- table;
- counting;
- multiplication rule;
- later, total probability/Bayes structure.

## Step 5 — Normalize

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
$$

## Step 6 — Verify

Check:

$$
0\le P(A\mid B)\le1.
$$

Also:

$$
P(A\cap B)\le P(B),
$$

so numerator tidak boleh melebihi denominator.

# Section 15 — Exam Traps

## 15.1 Denominator Trap

For

$$
P(A\mid B),
$$

denominator is

$$
P(B),
$$

not $P(A)$.

Memory cue:

> **Right side of the bar = new universe.**

---

## 15.2 Intersection Trap

Numerator is:

$$
P(A\cap B),
$$

not $P(A)$.

Only the portion of $A$ that survives inside $B$ matters.

---

## 15.3 Reverse Conditional Trap

Generally:

$$
P(A\mid B)\ne P(B\mid A).
$$

Never reverse without calculating denominator appropriately.

---

## 15.4 Original Sample-Space Trap

For equally likely outcomes:

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{N(A\cap B)}{N(B)},
$$

not

$$
\frac{N(A\cap B)}{N(S)}.
$$

---

## 15.5 Equiprobability Trap

Raw counting only works if relevant outcomes have equal probability weights.

For loaded dice or unequal-risk categories, use probability masses:

$$
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
$$

---

## 15.6 Replacement Trap

Without replacement:

probabilities generally change after every selection.

With replacement:

state may reset.

Do not assume constant factors unless the experiment supports it.

---

## 15.7 Causation Trap

$P(A\mid B)$ measures conditional probability, not causal effect.

“Given” means information restriction, not necessarily “caused by”.

---

## 15.8 Impossible Conditioning Event

Definition requires:

$$
P(B)>0.
$$

Jika $P(B)=0$, expression

$$
\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}
$$

tidak terdefinisi dalam elementary conditional probability framework yang digunakan textbook pada bagian ini.

---

# Section 16 — Formula Map

| Concept | Formula | Condition / Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional probability | $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}$ | $P(B)>0$ |
| Reverse conditional | $P(B\mid A)=\dfrac{P(A\cap B)}{P(A)}$ | $P(A)>0$ |
| Counting form | $P(A\mid B)=\dfrac{N(A\cap B)}{N(B)}$ | relevant elementary outcomes equally likely |
| Multiplication rule | $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B\mid A)$ | direct rearrangement |
| Alternative multiplication | $P(A\cap B)=P(B)P(A\mid B)$ | direct rearrangement |
| Three-event chain | $P(A\cap B\cap C)=P(A)P(B\mid A)P(C\mid A\cap B)$ | valid conditioning events |
| Normalization | $P(B\mid B)=1$ | $P(B)>0$ |
| Bound | $0\le P(A\mid B)\le1$ | conditional probability function |

# Section 17 — Relationship Map

## 17.1 Connection to [[1.3 Metode Enumerasi]]

For finite equally likely outcomes:

$$
P(A\mid B)
=
\frac{N(A\cap B)}{N(B)}.
$$

Counting determines numerator and denominator.

---

## 17.2 Connection to [[1.5 Kejadian Independen]]

Jika knowing $B$ does not change probability of $A$, then intuitively:

$$
P(A\mid B)=P(A).
$$

Formal independence criterion akan dibahas pada Topik 1.5.

Jangan menggunakan equality tersebut sekarang kecuali independence memang justified.

---

## 17.3 Connection to [[1.6 Teorema Bayes dan Hukum Probabilitas Total]]

Bayes akan membalik conditioning direction:

$$
P(A\mid B)
\leftrightarrow
P(B\mid A),
$$

dengan base probability / partition structure yang tepat.

Topik 1.4 harus dikuasai dulu karena Bayes hanyalah organized use dari:

- conditional probability;
- multiplication rule;
- total probability normalization.

| joint + marginal given | conditional probability | normalization | joint / conditioning marginal | reverse direction | label numerator & denominator |
| marginal + conditional given | joint probability | multiplication | $P(A)P(B\mid A)$ | multiply wrong direction | reconstruct intersection |
| successive no-replacement draws | chain conditional | state updates | product of changing fractions | keep probabilities constant | update counts each draw |
| successive with replacement | repeated condition | state resets | product of reset probabilities | decrease denominator unnecessarily | restore original composition |
| three successive events | chain rule | nested conditioning | $P(A)P(B\mid A)P(C\mid A\cap B)$ | condition only on immediate prior event | condition on full relevant history |
| compare $P(A)$ and $P(A\mid B)$ | interpretation | information changes universe | calculate both | infer causation | think reweighting, not causality |
| ask reverse conditional | $P(A\mid B)$ from $P(B\mid A)$ | incomplete unless more info | need joint/base probability | flip numbers | Bayes only when inputs available |
