## Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Conditional distribution menjawab pertanyaan:

> **Bagaimana distribusi satu random variable berubah ketika nilai random variable lain diketahui?**

Misalkan joint distribution menggambarkan seluruh pasangan $(X,Y)$. Jika diberi informasi bahwa $Y=y$, kita tidak lagi melihat seluruh joint support. Kita hanya melihat **slice** yang compatible dengan nilai tersebut.

Mental picture:

```text
Joint distribution
      ↓
Observe / fix Y = y
      ↓
Keep only points or density slice compatible with Y = y
      ↓
Renormalize that slice so total probability/density = 1
      ↓
Conditional distribution of X given Y = y
```

Secara simbolik:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Conditional}=rac{\text{Joint slice}}{\text{Marginal size of conditioning slice}}
}
$$

Untuk kasus diskrit, ini langsung berasal dari conditional probability:

$$
P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.
$$

Dengan

$$
A=\{X=x\},\qquad B=\{Y=y\},
$$

kita memperoleh

$$
P(X=x\mid Y=y)
=
\frac{P(X=x,Y=y)}{P(Y=y)}.
$$

Untuk kasus kontinu, event $Y=y$ mempunyai probability nol. Karena itu textbook mendefinisikan **conditional density** melalui ratio joint density terhadap marginal density, bukan melalui pembagian point probabilities.

> [!IMPORTANT] Discrete vs Continuous Conditioning
> - **Diskrit:** $P(Y=y)$ dapat positif, sehingga conditional probability literal dapat digunakan.
> - **Kontinu:** biasanya $P(Y=y)=0$, sehingga jangan menulis $P(X=x,Y=y)/P(Y=y)$. Gunakan density ratio.

### Conditional vs Marginal

Marginal distribution dan conditional distribution melakukan operasi yang berbeda:

| Operation | Apa yang dilakukan? | Hasil |
|---|---|---|
| Marginalize | Hilangkan variabel lain dengan sum/integral | Distribution tanpa informasi variabel lain |
| Condition | Fix nilai variabel lain lalu normalize slice | Distribution setelah informasi diberikan |

Ringkasnya:

$$
\text{Marginal: eliminate}
\qquad\text{vs}\qquad
\text{Conditional: fix + renormalize}.
$$

## Section 2 — Definisi, Support & Core Formulas

### 2.1 Conditional PMF — Kasus Diskrit

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ discrete dengan joint PMF

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X=x,Y=y),
$$

dan marginal PMF $Y$ adalah

$$
p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y),
$$

maka untuk $p_Y(y)>0$,

$$
\boxed{
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}
}.
$$

Demikian pula, untuk $p_X(x)>0$,

$$
\boxed{
p_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)
=
\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_X(x)}
}.
$$

### 2.2 Conditional Support — Kasus Diskrit

Jika conditioning value $Y=y$ fixed, conditional support $X$ adalah

$$
S_{X\mid Y=y}
=
\{x:(x,y)\in S_{X,Y}\}.
$$

Jadi conditional support adalah **slice joint support** pada nilai conditioning variable.

Contoh:

$$
S_{X,Y}=\{(1,1),(1,2),(2,2)\}.
$$

Jika $Y=1$:

$$
S_{X\mid Y=1}=\{1\}.
$$

Jika $Y=2$:

$$
S_{X\mid Y=2}=\{1,2\}.
$$

> [!WARNING] Support Trap
> Marginal support $S_X$ tidak otomatis sama dengan conditional support $S_{X\mid Y=y}$. Conditioning dapat mempersempit support.

### 2.3 Normalization Conditional PMF

Untuk fixed $y$ dengan $p_Y(y)>0$,

$$
\sum_x p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\sum_x\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}
=
\frac{p_Y(y)}{p_Y(y)}
=1.
$$

Ini adalah alasan denominator harus marginal probability dari **conditioning variable**.

---

### 2.4 Conditional PDF — Kasus Kontinu

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ jointly continuous dengan joint PDF $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$ dan marginal PDF

$$
f_Y(y)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx,
$$

maka untuk $f_Y(y)>0$,

$$
\boxed{
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}
}.
$$

Demikian pula,

$$
\boxed{
f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_X(x)}
},
\qquad f_X(x)>0.
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] Density, Bukan Point Probability
> Untuk continuous random variables,
> $f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)$ adalah **density height**, bukan $P(X=x\mid Y=y)$.

### 2.5 Conditional Support — Kasus Kontinu

Jika joint support adalah

$$
0<x<y<1,
$$

maka setelah conditioning pada $Y=y$ dengan $0<y<1$:

$$
0<x<y.
$$

Jadi

$$
S_{X\mid Y=y}=(0,y).
$$

Sebaliknya, conditioning pada $X=x$ menghasilkan

$$
x<y<1.
$$

Conditional support selalu berasal dari **joint support setelah conditioning value disubstitusikan**.

### 2.6 Normalization Conditional PDF

Untuk fixed $y$ dengan $f_Y(y)>0$,

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx
=
\frac{1}{f_Y(y)}
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx
=1.
$$

Dalam actual support, integral sebaiknya ditulis langsung dengan bounds yang benar.

---

### 2.7 Conditional CDF

Untuk fixed conditioning value $y$, conditional CDF $X$ didefinisikan sebagai

$$
\boxed{
F_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=P(X\le x\mid Y=y)
}.
$$

#### Diskrit

$$
F_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\sum_{u\le x}p_{X\mid Y}(u\mid y).
$$

#### Kontinu

$$
F_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\int_{-\infty}^{x}f_{X\mid Y}(u\mid y)\,du,
$$

dengan effective lower bound mengikuti conditional support.

Jika differentiable terhadap $x$,

$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}F_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y).
$$

### 2.8 Conditional Probability dari Conditional Distribution

Setelah conditional PMF/PDF diperoleh, probability dihitung seperti univariate distribution biasa.

Diskrit:

$$
P(X\in A\mid Y=y)
=
\sum_{x\in A}p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y).
$$

Kontinu:

$$
P(a<X<b\mid Y=y)
=
\int_a^b f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx,
$$

dengan interval di-intersect terlebih dahulu dengan conditional support.

### 2.9 Reverse Reconstruction

Definisi conditional juga memberikan factorization:

$$
\boxed{
p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)p_Y(y)}
$$

untuk discrete model, dan

$$
\boxed{
f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)f_Y(y)}
$$

untuk continuous model.

Similarly,

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)p_X(x),
$$

atau

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)f_X(x).
$$

Hubungan ini sangat penting untuk Topik 3.7 compound distributions dan Topik 3.4 conditional expectation.

## Section 3 — How It Works

### 3.1 General Workflow — Diskrit

Jika joint PMF diberikan dan target adalah distribution $X\mid Y=y_0$:

1. **Check conditioning value:** pastikan $p_Y(y_0)>0$.
2. **Slice joint support:** tentukan semua $x$ yang compatible dengan $y_0$.
3. **Find denominator:**
   $$
   p_Y(y_0)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y_0).
   $$
4. **Normalize each joint mass:**
   $$
   p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y_0)
   =\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y_0)}{p_Y(y_0)}.
   $$
5. **Check total mass:**
   $$
   \sum_x p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y_0)=1.
   $$
6. **Use conditional PMF** untuk probability/CDF target.

> [!TIP] Fast Method — Ratio Within Slice
> Bila joint PMF hanya diketahui up to common factor pada fixed $y$, conditional probabilities dapat diperoleh dari **relative masses pada slice** lalu dinormalisasi. Common factor akan cancel.

### 3.2 General Workflow — Kontinu

Jika joint PDF diberikan dan target adalah $X\mid Y=y_0$:

1. **Read joint support.**
2. **Fix $y=y_0$** dan tentukan conditional support untuk $x$.
3. **Compute marginal density at $y_0$:**
   $$
   f_Y(y_0)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y_0)\,dx
   $$
   dengan bounds dari slice support.
4. **Divide:**
   $$
   f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y_0)
   =\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y_0)}{f_Y(y_0)}.
   $$
5. **Check normalization** over conditional support.
6. **Integrate conditional density** untuk target probability atau build conditional CDF.

### 3.3 Geometry of Conditioning

Marginalization mengintegralkan seluruh slice untuk menghasilkan **ukuran slice**. Conditioning kemudian membagi density pada slice dengan ukuran tersebut.

Untuk fixed $y$:

$$
f_Y(y)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx.
$$

Kemudian

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{\int f_{X,Y}(u,y)\,du}.
$$

Interpretasi:

> conditional density adalah **shape dari joint density sepanjang slice**, dinormalisasi agar area slice menjadi $1$.

### 3.4 Conditional Distribution dan Independence

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent, joint distribution factorizes:

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y).
$$

Maka

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{f_X(x)f_Y(y)}{f_Y(y)}
=f_X(x).
$$

Jadi independence berarti conditioning pada $Y$ **tidak mengubah distribution $X$**.

Untuk discrete case:

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=p_X(x).
$$

> [!NOTE] Exam Diagnostic
> Jika conditional distribution masih bergantung pada conditioning value $y$, itu merupakan evidence bahwa distribusi $X$ berubah ketika $Y$ diketahui. Jangan menyimpulkan independence. Jika conditional distribution sama dengan marginal untuk seluruh conditioning values yang valid, itu konsisten dengan independence criterion yang dibahas lebih lanjut di [[3.5 Independensi dan Korelasi]].

### 3.5 Higher-Dimensional Conditional Distribution

Source juga memperluas conditioning ke lebih dari dua random variables.

Misalkan joint PDF adalah

$$
f(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n).
$$

Jika conditioning pada $X_1=x_1$ dan marginal density $f_1(x_1)>0$, maka joint conditional PDF dari $X_2,\ldots,X_n$ adalah

$$
\boxed{
f_{2,\ldots,n\mid 1}(x_2,\ldots,x_n\mid x_1)
=
\frac{f(x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n)}{f_1(x_1)}
}.
$$

Lebih umum, bila beberapa variables dikondisikan, denominator adalah **joint marginal distribution dari seluruh conditioning variables**.

> [!IMPORTANT] Denominator Rule
> Denominator harus persis distribution dari informasi yang diberikan setelah tanda conditioning bar.

## Section 4 — Worked Examples & Exam Cases

### Case A — Fundamental Discrete Conditional PMF

Diberikan joint PMF textbook:

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)=\frac{x+y}{21},
\qquad x=1,2,3,\quad y=1,2.
$$

Cari:

1. $p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid 2)$;
2. $P(X\ge2\mid Y=2)$.

#### Step 1 — Support

Untuk $Y=2$:

$$
x\in\{1,2,3\}.
$$

#### Step 2 — Marginal Denominator

Dari marginalization:

$$
p_Y(y)=\frac{y+2}{7}.
$$

Jadi

$$
p_Y(2)=\frac47.
$$

#### Step 3 — Conditional PMF

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid2)
=
\frac{(x+2)/21}{4/7}
=
\frac{x+2}{12},
\qquad x=1,2,3.
$$

Sehingga

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(1\mid2)=\frac3{12}=\frac14,
$$

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(2\mid2)=\frac4{12}=\frac13,
$$

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(3\mid2)=\frac5{12}.
$$

#### Step 4 — Target Probability

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(X\ge2\mid Y=2)
&=p_{X\mid Y}(2\mid2)+p_{X\mid Y}(3\mid2)\\
&=\frac4{12}+\frac5{12}\\
&=\boxed{\frac34}.
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Verification

$$
\frac3{12}+\frac4{12}+\frac5{12}=1.
$$

> [!TIP] Fast Method
> Pada slice $Y=2$, joint masses proportional terhadap
> $$
> 3:4:5.
> $$
> Jadi probability $X\ge2$ langsung
> $$
> \frac{4+5}{3+4+5}=\frac34.
> $$
> Tidak perlu menghitung denominator $21$ karena common factor cancel.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case A
> - Denominator salah memakai $p_X(x)$ padahal conditioning adalah $Y=2$.
> - Menjumlahkan seluruh joint PMF, bukan hanya slice $Y=2$.
> - Menganggap conditional PMF sama dengan joint PMF tanpa renormalization.

### Case B — Continuous Conditional Density pada Rectangular Support

Diberikan joint density textbook Miller:

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
\frac23(x+2y),
\qquad 0<x<1,\quad 0<y<1.
$$

Cari

$$
P\left(X\le\frac12\mid Y=\frac12\right).
$$

#### Step 1 — Conditional Support

Karena support rectangular,

$$
0<x<1
$$

untuk setiap fixed $0<y<1$.

#### Step 2 — Marginal Density of $Y$

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_Y(y)
&=\int_0^1 \frac23(x+2y)\,dx\\
&=\frac23\left[\frac{x^2}{2}+2yx\right]_0^1\\
&=\frac13(1+4y).
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Step 3 — Conditional PDF

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
&=\frac{\frac23(x+2y)}{\frac13(1+4y)}\\
&=\frac{2x+4y}{1+4y},
\qquad 0<x<1.
\end{aligned}
$$

Set $y=1/2$:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}\left(x\mid\frac12\right)
=
\frac{2x+2}{3},
\qquad 0<x<1.
$$

#### Step 4 — Conditional Probability

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\left(X\le\frac12\mid Y=\frac12\right)
&=\int_0^{1/2}\frac{2x+2}{3}\,dx\\
&=\frac13[x^2+2x]_0^{1/2}\\
&=\frac13\left(\frac14+1\right)\\
&=\boxed{\frac5{12}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Verification

Conditional PDF integrates to $1$:

$$
\int_0^1\frac{2x+2}{3}\,dx
=
\frac13(1+2)
=1.
$$

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case B
> Untuk continuous $Y$, jangan menulis
> $$
> \frac{P(X\le1/2,Y=1/2)}{P(Y=1/2)}.
> $$
> Kedua point-type probabilities melibatkan event $Y=1/2$ yang probability-nya $0$. Gunakan conditional density.

### Case C — Challenging Support-Dependent Conditioning

Diberikan joint PDF textbook Hogg, McKean & Craig:

$$
f_{X_1,X_2}(x_1,x_2)
=
\begin{cases}
2,&0<x_1<x_2<1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

Cari:

1. $f_{X_1\mid X_2}(x_1\mid x_2)$;
2. $P(X_1<1/2\mid X_2=3/4)$;
3. bandingkan dengan $P(X_1<1/2)$.

#### Step 1 — Marginal Density of $X_2$

Untuk fixed $x_2$:

$$
0<x_1<x_2.
$$

Maka

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{X_2}(x_2)
&=\int_0^{x_2}2\,dx_1\\
&=2x_2,
\qquad 0<x_2<1.
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Step 2 — Conditional PDF

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{X_1\mid X_2}(x_1\mid x_2)
&=\frac{2}{2x_2}\\
&=\frac1{x_2},
\qquad 0<x_1<x_2<1.
\end{aligned}
$$

Ini adalah uniform density pada interval conditional $(0,x_2)$.

#### Step 3 — Conditional Probability

Untuk $x_2=3/4$:

$$
f_{X_1\mid X_2}\left(x_1\mid\frac34\right)
=\frac43,
\qquad 0<x_1<\frac34.
$$

Karena $1/2<3/4$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\left(X_1<\frac12\mid X_2=\frac34\right)
&=\int_0^{1/2}\frac43\,dx_1\\
&=\boxed{\frac23}.
\end{aligned}
$$

#### Step 4 — Marginal Probability for Comparison

Marginal density $X_1$:

$$
f_{X_1}(x_1)=\int_{x_1}^1 2\,dx_2=2(1-x_1),
\qquad 0<x_1<1.
$$

Thus,

$$
\begin{aligned}
P\left(X_1<\frac12\right)
&=\int_0^{1/2}2(1-x_1)\,dx_1\\
&=\boxed{\frac34}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Jadi

$$
P\left(X_1<\frac12\mid X_2=\frac34\right)
=\frac23
\neq
\frac34
=P\left(X_1<\frac12\right).
$$

Informasi bahwa $X_2=3/4$ mengubah distribution $X_1$.

> [!IMPORTANT] Structural Lesson
> Joint density constant **tidak** berarti conditional support constant. Di triangular support, conditioning value menentukan panjang interval conditional.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case C
> Jika salah memakai support $0<x_1<1$, maka conditional density $1/x_2$ akan diintegralkan pada interval yang terlalu lebar dan totalnya bukan $1$.

### Case D — Independence Recognition melalui Conditional Density

Diberikan joint PDF textbook Miller:

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)=4xy,
\qquad 0<x<1,\quad0<y<1.
$$

Marginal densities:

$$
f_X(x)=2x,
\qquad
f_Y(y)=2y.
$$

Conditional density:

$$
\begin{aligned}
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
&=\frac{4xy}{2y}\\
&=2x,
\qquad0<x<1.
\end{aligned}
$$

Tetapi

$$
f_X(x)=2x.
$$

Sehingga

$$
\boxed{f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=f_X(x)}
$$

untuk setiap valid $y$.

Ini konsisten dengan factorization

$$
4xy=(2x)(2y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y),
$$

sehingga $X$ dan $Y$ independent.

> [!TIP] Fast Recognition
> Jika conditional density setelah simplification kehilangan seluruh dependence pada $y$ dan sama dengan $f_X(x)$ pada support yang compatible, cek factorization independence.

## Section 5 — Verification & Sanity Check

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check
> 1. **Conditioning value valid:** discrete memerlukan $p_Y(y)>0$; continuous density ratio memerlukan $f_Y(y)>0$ sesuai definition source.
> 2. **Denominator variable:** conditioning pada $Y=y$ → denominator adalah $p_Y(y)$ atau $f_Y(y)$.
> 3. **Support:** conditional support adalah slice dari joint support, bukan otomatis marginal support penuh.
> 4. **Normalization:** conditional PMF harus sum ke $1$; conditional PDF harus integrate ke $1$ untuk each fixed valid conditioning value.
> 5. **Non-negativity:** conditional PMF/PDF tidak boleh negatif.
> 6. **Probability range:** semua conditional probabilities harus di $[0,1]$.
> 7. **CDF check:** conditional CDF harus nondecreasing dalam target variable dan bergerak dari $0$ ke $1$ di conditional support.
> 8. **Independence check:** jika independent, conditional distribution harus reduce ke corresponding marginal distribution.
> 9. **Continuous check:** jangan interpretasikan density value sebagai point probability.
> 10. **Slice check:** bila hasil conditional density masih nonzero di luar joint support slice, support kemungkinan salah.

### Metode Alternatif

#### Full Formula vs Ratio Cancellation — Diskrit

Full method:

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}.
$$

Fast method jika fixed $y$ dan seluruh slice memiliki common factor:

1. tulis relative joint masses;
2. jumlahkan weights;
3. normalize weights.

Ini sangat efisien pada tabel PMF.

#### Compute Conditional PDF Symbolically vs Substitute First

Jika diminta $X\mid Y=y_0$, dua cara valid:

**Cara 1 — General first**

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)},
$$

kemudian substitute $y=y_0$.

**Cara 2 — Slice first**

Substitute $y_0$ pada joint density lalu normalize langsung:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y_0)
=
\frac{f(x,y_0)}{\int f(u,y_0)\,du}.
$$

Cara 2 sering lebih cepat jika hanya satu conditioning value diminta.

#### Conditional CDF vs Direct Integration

Jika target probability berupa banyak threshold pada conditional distribution, bangun conditional CDF sekali:

$$
F_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y).
$$

Jika hanya satu interval sederhana, direct integral conditional PDF biasanya lebih cepat.

## Section 6 — Connections & Mental Model

### 6.1 Joint → Marginal → Conditional

Urutan calculation yang paling aman:

```text
Joint distribution
      ↓
Read support
      ↓
Find marginal of conditioning variable
      ↓
Divide joint by that marginal
      ↓
Conditional distribution
      ↓
Probability / CDF / expectation / variance
```

### 6.2 Conditioning sebagai Renormalized Slice

Untuk continuous model:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}.
$$

Denominator

$$
f_Y(y)=\int f_{X,Y}(u,y)\,du
$$

adalah total density mass of the slice sebelum normalisasi.

### 6.3 Connection ke Conditional Expectation

Setelah $f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)$ diperoleh, conditional expectation di [[3.4 Nilai Harapan dan Variansi Bersyarat]] dihitung seperti expectation univariate terhadap conditional distribution:

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]
=
\int x f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx
$$

untuk continuous case, atau corresponding summation untuk discrete case.

### 6.4 Connection ke Independence

Independence dapat dipahami secara conditional:

> mengetahui $Y=y$ tidak mengubah distribution $X$.

Secara formula:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=f_X(x).
$$

### 6.5 Connection ke Compound Distribution

Compound models sering ditulis melalui conditional structure:

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)f_Y(y).
$$

Kita condition pada variable yang membuat model sederhana, kemudian aggregate kembali. Ini menjadi dasar Topik [[3.7 Distribusi Majemuk (Compound Distribution)]].

### 6.6 Visual ↔ Formula

| Visual / Geometry | Conditional Meaning |
|---|---|
| Column/row pada joint PMF table | slice ketika one variable fixed |
| Sum of slice | marginal probability conditioning event |
| Normalize entries in slice | conditional PMF |
| Horizontal/vertical cross-section joint PDF | conditional density shape before normalization |
| Area under cross-section | marginal density at conditioning value |
| Cross-section divided by its area | normalized conditional PDF |
| Triangular support | conditional interval length depends on conditioning value |
| Same conditional and marginal shape | signal consistent with independence |

## Section 7 — Exam Traps & Misconceptions

### Wrong Denominator Trap

> [!BUG] Salah
> Jika mencari $p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)$, membagi joint PMF dengan $p_X(x)$.
>
> **Benar**
> $$
> p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
> =\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}.
> $$
>
> Mental rule: **denominator = distribution of what comes after the conditioning bar.**

### Marginal vs Conditional Trap

> [!BUG] Marginal
> $$
> p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y).
> $$
>
> [!BUG] Conditional
> $$
> p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}.
> $$
>
> Sum/integrate out ≠ divide/normalize.

### Continuous Point-Probability Trap

> [!BUG] Salah
> $$
> f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
> =\frac{P(X=x,Y=y)}{P(Y=y)}
> $$
> untuk jointly continuous variables.
>
> Karena point probabilities bernilai $0$, textbook menggunakan density ratio.

### Conditional Support Trap

Untuk

$$
0<x<y<1,
$$

conditioning pada $Y=y$ memberi

$$
0<x<y,
$$

bukan $0<x<1$.

### Substitute-Before-Support Trap

Jika langsung memasukkan $y=y_0$ ke formula density tanpa membaca support, conditional bounds mudah salah.

Mental sequence:

> **joint support → fix conditioning value → read slice → normalize**.

### Zero / Invalid Conditioning Value Trap

Conditional PMF formula memerlukan

$$
p_Y(y)>0.
$$

Conditional PDF definition source digunakan ketika

$$
f_Y(y)>0.
$$

Jangan divide by zero.

### Normalization Trap

Jika conditional density diperoleh sebagai

$$
g(x)=\frac{f(x,y_0)}{f_Y(y_0)},
$$

tetapi

$$
\int g(x)\,dx\ne1,
$$

paling sering penyebabnya:

- wrong denominator;
- wrong conditional support;
- wrong marginal integration bounds.

### Conditioning Direction Trap

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
$$

dan

$$
f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)
$$

secara umum berbeda. Jangan menukar numerator-denominator orientation.

### Independence Trap

Jangan berasumsi conditional = marginal kecuali independence sudah dibuktikan/didukung.

### Density Height Trap

Conditional PDF boleh lebih besar dari $1$ pada suatu point. Yang harus valid adalah:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\ge0
$$

dan total area $1$ pada conditional support.

### Red Flags

Jika hasil akhir:

- denominator memakai marginal variable yang salah;
- conditional support tidak berubah walau joint support triangular;
- conditional PMF sum bukan $1$;
- conditional PDF integral bukan $1$;
- continuous conditioning memakai $P(Y=y)$ secara literal;
- probability conditional $<0$ atau $>1$;
- $f_{X\mid Y}$ masih nonzero pada pasangan $(x,y)$ di luar joint support;

maka calculation hampir pasti salah.

## Section 8 — Executive Summary

### Must Remember

> [!SUMMARY] Must Remember
> 1. Conditional distribution = **fix one variable + normalize compatible joint slice**.
> 2. Discrete:
>    $$
>    p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)},\qquad p_Y(y)>0.
>    $$
> 3. Continuous:
>    $$
>    f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)},\qquad f_Y(y)>0.
>    $$
> 4. Denominator selalu distribution dari **conditioning variable**.
> 5. Conditional support = slice dari joint support setelah conditioning value fixed.
> 6. Conditional PMF/PDF harus normalize ke $1$.
> 7. Conditional CDF diperoleh dengan cumulative sum/integral dari conditional distribution.
> 8. Continuous point probability bukan density: $P(Y=y)=0$ tidak mencegah penggunaan conditional density ratio.
> 9. Reverse factorization:
>    $$
>    f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)f_Y(y).
>    $$
> 10. Jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent, conditioning tidak mengubah distribution:
>     $$
>     f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=f_X(x).
>     $$
> 11. Pada non-rectangular support, conditioning value dapat mengubah interval/range target variable.
> 12. Setelah conditional distribution diperoleh, probability/expectation mechanics sama seperti univariate case pada conditional support.

### Formula Map / Comparison Table

| Target | Diskrit | Kontinu | Critical Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional object | $p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=p_{X,Y}(x,y)/p_Y(y)$ | $f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=f_{X,Y}(x,y)/f_Y(y)$ | denominator > 0 |
| Denominator | $p_Y(y)=\sum_xp_{X,Y}(x,y)$ | $f_Y(y)=\int f_{X,Y}(x,y)dx$ | use conditioning variable |
| Conditional support | valid points with fixed $y$ | valid slice with fixed $y$ | derive from joint support |
| Normalization | $\sum_x p_{X\mid Y}=1$ | $\int f_{X\mid Y}dx=1$ | over conditional support |
| Conditional CDF | cumulative conditional mass | integral conditional density | nondecreasing, limits 0→1 |
| Conditional probability | sum conditional PMF | integrate conditional PDF | intersect event with conditional support |
| Reverse joint formula | $p_{X,Y}=p_{X\mid Y}p_Y$ | $f_{X,Y}=f_{X\mid Y}f_Y$ | valid support |
| Independence signal | $p_{X\mid Y}=p_X$ | $f_{X\mid Y}=f_X$ | for all valid conditioning values |

### Trigger Keywords

| Jika soal mengatakan... | Pikirkan... |
|---|---|
| “given that $Y=y$” | conditional distribution |
| “distribution of $X$ given $Y$” | joint ÷ marginal of $Y$ |
| “given $Y=y$, find probability...” | derive/use conditional PMF/PDF |
| “conditional density” | density ratio, not point probabilities |
| “conditional CDF” | cumulative conditional PMF/PDF |
| “given one measurement/value” | slice joint support |
| “does information about $Y$ change $X$?” | compare conditional vs marginal |
| triangular support + conditioning | recalculate slice bounds |
| multiple variables conditioned | denominator = joint marginal of conditioning set |

### Kapan Digunakan

Gunakan conditional distribution ketika soal memberikan **informasi mengenai nilai variable lain** dan meminta distribution/probability target variable setelah informasi tersebut diketahui.

### Kapan TIDAK Boleh Digunakan

Jangan memakai conditional distribution jika target hanya meminta unconditional distribution satu variable; gunakan [[3.2 Distribusi Marginal]]. Jangan langsung memakai conditional expectation formula jika conditional distribution belum jelas; lihat [[3.4 Nilai Harapan dan Variansi Bersyarat]]. Jangan menyimpulkan independence hanya dari zero covariance; lihat [[3.5 Independensi dan Korelasi]].

### Quick Decision Tree

```text
Diberikan joint distribution + informasi Y = y
│
├─ Discrete?
│   ├─ Find pY(y) by summing joint slice
│   ├─ Check pY(y) > 0
│   └─ pX|Y(x|y) = pXY(x,y) / pY(y)
│
└─ Continuous?
    ├─ Read conditional support from joint region
    ├─ Find fY(y) by integrating joint slice
    ├─ Check fY(y) > 0
    └─ fX|Y(x|y) = fXY(x,y) / fY(y)

Then:
conditional PMF/PDF
      ↓
CDF / probability
      ↓
verify normalization + support
```
