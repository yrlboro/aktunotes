## Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Conditional expectation menjawab:

> **Jika saya sudah mengetahui $Y=y$, berapa rata-rata baru untuk $X$?**

Conditional variance menjawab:

> **Setelah informasi $Y=y$ diketahui, seberapa besar variasi $X$ yang masih tersisa?**

Mental model:

```text
Joint distribution
      ↓
Fix / observe Y = y
      ↓
Build conditional distribution X | Y = y
      ↓
Compute mean under that conditional distribution
      ↓
E[X | Y = y]
      ↓
Compute second conditional moment
      ↓
Var(X | Y = y)
```

Jadi tidak ada formula “misterius” yang benar-benar baru. Setelah conditioning dilakukan, kita memperlakukan

$$
X\mid Y=y
$$

seperti random variable univariat biasa.

> [!TIP] Mental Rule
> **Condition first, then calculate expectation exactly as in the univariate case.**

## Section 2 — Core Concepts & Formula Map

### 2.1 Conditional Expectation — Discrete

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ diskrit dan

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
$$

adalah conditional PMF dari $X$ given $Y=y$, maka untuk fungsi $u(X)$:

$$
\boxed{
E[u(X)\mid Y=y]
=
\sum_x u(x)p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
}
$$

dengan summation hanya pada **conditional support** yang valid.

Khusus untuk $u(X)=X$:

$$
\boxed{
E[X\mid Y=y]
=
\sum_x x\,p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
}
$$

Untuk second moment:

$$
\boxed{
E[X^2\mid Y=y]
=
\sum_x x^2p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
}
$$

---

### 2.2 Conditional Expectation — Continuous

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ kontinu dan

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
$$

adalah conditional PDF, maka:

$$
\boxed{
E[u(X)\mid Y=y]
=
\int u(x)f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx
}
$$

dengan integration bounds berasal dari conditional support.

Khususnya:

$$
\boxed{
E[X\mid Y=y]
=
\int x f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx
}
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
E[X^2\mid Y=y]
=
\int x^2f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx.
}
$$

---

### 2.3 Conditional Variance

Definisi:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y)
=
E\left[
\left(
X-E[X\mid Y=y]
\right)^2
\middle|Y=y
\right].
$$

Dalam pengerjaan ujian, identity berikut hampir selalu lebih cepat:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y)
=
E[X^2\mid Y=y]
-
\left(E[X\mid Y=y]\right)^2
}
$$

> [!WARNING] Expectation–Variance Trap
> Jangan menulis
>
> $$
> E[X^2\mid Y=y]
> =
> \left(E[X\mid Y=y]\right)^2.
> $$
>
> Keduanya hanya sama pada kasus degenerate tertentu.

---

### 2.4 $E[X\mid Y=y]$ vs $E[X\mid Y]$

Ini distinction penting.

Untuk nilai tertentu $y$:

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]
$$

adalah suatu nilai atau fungsi yang dievaluasi pada $y$.

Jika hasil perhitungan memberi

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]=g(y),
$$

maka secara random-variable notation:

$$
\boxed{
E[X\mid Y]=g(Y).
}
$$

Contoh:

Jika

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]=\frac y2,
$$

maka

$$
E[X\mid Y]=\frac Y2.
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] Important Distinction
> $E[X\mid Y]$ **bukan konstanta secara umum**. Ia adalah fungsi dari random variable $Y$.

---

### 2.5 Law of Total Expectation

Silabus CF2 secara eksplisit memasukkan hukum ekspektasi total.

Jika expectation terkait ada, maka:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
E\{E[X\mid Y]\}
}
$$

Mental interpretation:

> **Average the conditional averages over the distribution of the conditioning variable.**

#### Jika $Y$ diskrit

Jika

$$
m(y)=E[X\mid Y=y],
$$

maka:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
\sum_y m(y)p_Y(y).
}
$$

#### Jika $Y$ kontinu

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
\int E[X\mid Y=y]\,f_Y(y)\,dy.
}
$$

Ini adalah versi expectation dari prinsip:

```text
Condition → solve easier problem → average back over Y
```

## Section 3 — Structure Recognition & Calculation Pathways

### 3.1 Jika Diberi Joint PMF/PDF

Gunakan pathway:

```text
Joint distribution
      ↓
Find marginal of Y
      ↓
Form X | Y = y
      ↓
Find conditional support
      ↓
Compute E[X | Y = y]
      ↓
Compute E[X² | Y = y]
      ↓
Var(X | Y = y)
```

Formula conditioning:

Diskrit:

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}.
$$

Kontinu:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}.
$$

---

### 3.2 Jika Diberi Conditional Distribution Langsung

Tidak perlu kembali ke joint distribution.

Langsung:

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]
=
\sum_x x p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
$$

atau

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]
=
\int x f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx.
$$

Untuk variance:

$$
E[X^2\mid Y=y]
\rightarrow
E[X^2\mid Y=y]
-
\{E[X\mid Y=y]\}^2.
$$

> [!TIP] Exam Shortcut
> Jika conditional distribution sudah diberikan, **jangan membangun joint distribution yang tidak diperlukan**.

---

### 3.3 Conditional Support Bisa Bergantung pada $y$

Jika joint support:

$$
0<x<y<1,
$$

maka untuk fixed $Y=y$:

$$
0<x<y.
$$

Jadi bounds expectation adalah:

$$
\int_0^y (\cdots)\,dx,
$$

bukan otomatis $\int_0^1$.

> [!DANGER] Support Trap
> Sebagian besar error pada conditional expectation kontinu bukan berasal dari integrand, tetapi dari **batas integral yang salah**.

---

### 3.4 Conditional Variance: Direct vs Shortcut

Direct:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y)
=
\int
\left(x-E[X\mid Y=y]\right)^2
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx.
$$

Shortcut:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y)
=
E[X^2\mid Y=y]
-
\{E[X\mid Y=y]\}^2.
}
$$

Untuk exam, shortcut second moment biasanya lebih cepat dan mengurangi expansion error.

## Section 4 — Worked Cases

### Case A — Fundamental: Conditional Mean & Variance pada Triangular Support

**Soal**

Diberikan joint PDF

$$
f_{X_1,X_2}(x_1,x_2)
=
2,
\qquad
0<x_1<x_2<1.
$$

Tentukan:

1. $E[X_1\mid X_2=x_2]$;
2. $\operatorname{Var}(X_1\mid X_2=x_2)$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **1. Parse Narasi & Target**  
> Joint density diberikan pada triangular region. Target adalah conditional mean dan variance dari $X_1$ given $X_2=x_2$.
>
> **2. Identify Structure / Distribution**  
> Kita perlu menemukan conditional density $f_{X_1\mid X_2}(x_1\mid x_2)$ terlebih dahulu.
>
> **3. Support / Domain / Region**  
> Dari
>
> $$
> 0<x_1<x_2<1,
> $$
>
> untuk fixed $x_2$:
>
> $$
> 0<x_1<x_2.
> $$
>
> Marginal $X_2$:
>
> $$
> f_{X_2}(x_2)
> =
> \int_0^{x_2}2\,dx_1
> =
> 2x_2,
> \qquad 0<x_2<1.
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> f_{X_1\mid X_2}(x_1\mid x_2)
> =
> \frac{2}{2x_2}
> =
> \frac1{x_2},
> \qquad 0<x_1<x_2.
> $$
>
> **4. Setup**
>
> Conditional mean:
>
> $$
> E[X_1\mid X_2=x_2]
> =
> \int_0^{x_2}
> x_1\frac1{x_2}\,dx_1.
> $$
>
> Conditional second moment:
>
> $$
> E[X_1^2\mid X_2=x_2]
> =
> \int_0^{x_2}
> x_1^2\frac1{x_2}\,dx_1.
> $$
>
> **5. Calculation**
>
> Mean:
>
> $$
> E[X_1\mid X_2=x_2]
> =
> \frac1{x_2}
> \left[\frac{x_1^2}{2}\right]_0^{x_2}
> =
> \frac{x_2}{2}.
> $$
>
> Second moment:
>
> $$
> E[X_1^2\mid X_2=x_2]
> =
> \frac1{x_2}
> \left[\frac{x_1^3}{3}\right]_0^{x_2}
> =
> \frac{x_2^2}{3}.
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X_1\mid X_2=x_2)
> =
> \frac{x_2^2}{3}
> -
> \left(\frac{x_2}{2}\right)^2
> =
> \boxed{\frac{x_2^2}{12}}.
> $$
>
> Dan:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> E[X_1\mid X_2=x_2]=\frac{x_2}{2}
> }.
> $$
>
> **6. Interpretation**  
> Conditional distribution $X_1\mid X_2=x_2$ uniform pada interval $(0,x_2)$. Karena upper endpoint berubah bersama $x_2$, conditional mean dan variance juga berubah sebagai fungsi $x_2$.
>
> **7. Verification**
>
> - Mean harus berada antara $0$ dan $x_2$: $x_2/2$ memenuhi.
> - Variance $\ge0$: $x_2^2/12>0$ untuk $x_2>0$.
> - Conditional density normalize:
>
> $$
> \int_0^{x_2}\frac1{x_2}dx_1=1.
> $$

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case A
> - **Trap:** memakai bounds $0<x_1<1$ setelah conditioning.
> - **Mengapa distractor terlihat benar:** marginal support $X_1$ memang berada dalam $(0,1)$.
> - **Cara menghindari:** setelah fix $X_2=x_2$, baca ulang joint inequality.
> - **Shortcut aman:** kenali langsung bahwa $X_1\mid X_2=x_2$ uniform pada $(0,x_2)$ setelah conditional PDF terbentuk.
> - **Target waktu:** sekitar 2–3 menit.

### Case B — Exam-Typical: Conditional Mean dan Variance dari Joint Density pada Rectangular Support

**Soal**

Diberikan joint PDF

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
\frac23(x+2y),
\qquad
0<x<1,\quad 0<y<1.
$$

Hitung

$$
E[X\mid Y=\tfrac12]
$$

dan

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=\tfrac12).
$$

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **1. Parse Narasi & Target**  
> Kita membutuhkan conditional distribution $X\mid Y=1/2$, lalu conditional first dan second moments.
>
> **2. Identify Structure / Distribution**  
> Continuous joint density dengan rectangular support.
>
> **3. Support / Domain / Region**  
> Karena support rectangular:
>
> $$
> 0<x<1
> $$
>
> tetap berlaku setelah $Y=1/2$.
>
> **4. Setup**
>
> Hitung marginal $Y$:
>
> $$
> f_Y(y)
> =
> \int_0^1
> \frac23(x+2y)\,dx.
> $$
>
> Pada $y=1/2$:
>
> $$
> f_Y\left(\frac12\right)
> =
> \int_0^1
> \frac23(x+1)\,dx.
> $$
>
> **5. Calculation**
>
> Denominator:
>
> $$
> f_Y\left(\frac12\right)
> =
> \frac23
> \left[
> \frac{x^2}{2}+x
> \right]_0^1
> =
> \frac23\left(\frac12+1\right)
> =
> 1.
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> f_{X\mid Y}\left(x\mid\frac12\right)
> =
> \frac23(x+1),
> \qquad 0<x<1.
> $$
>
> Conditional mean:
>
> $$
> E\left[X\mid Y=\frac12\right]
> =
> \int_0^1
> x\frac23(x+1)\,dx
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \frac23
> \int_0^1(x^2+x)\,dx
> =
> \frac23
> \left(\frac13+\frac12\right)
> =
> \boxed{\frac59}.
> $$
>
> Conditional second moment:
>
> $$
> E\left[X^2\mid Y=\frac12\right]
> =
> \frac23
> \int_0^1(x^3+x^2)\,dx
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \frac23
> \left(\frac14+\frac13\right)
> =
> \frac{7}{18}.
> $$
>
> Therefore:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}\left(X\mid Y=\frac12\right)
> =
> \frac{7}{18}
> -
> \left(\frac59\right)^2
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \frac{63}{162}
> -
> \frac{50}{162}
> =
> \boxed{\frac{13}{162}}.
> $$
>
> **6. Interpretation**  
> Informasi $Y=1/2$ menghasilkan conditional density yang lebih berat pada $x$ besar karena density proportional terhadap $x+1$.
>
> **7. Verification**
>
> $$
> \int_0^1\frac23(x+1)\,dx=1.
> $$
>
> Selain itu:
>
> $$
> 0<\frac59<1
> $$
>
> dan
>
> $$
> \frac{13}{162}>0.
> $$

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case B
> - **Trap:** menghitung expectation langsung terhadap joint PDF tanpa normalize.
> - **Mengapa distractor terlihat benar:** joint integrand terlihat seperti density terhadap $x$.
> - **Cara menghindari:** selalu tanyakan: “density ini joint atau conditional?”
> - **Shortcut aman:** untuk satu nilai $y_0$, gunakan slice-and-normalize:
>
> $$
> f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y_0)
> =
> \frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y_0)}
> {\int f_{X,Y}(u,y_0)\,du}.
> $$
>
> - **Target waktu:** sekitar 3 menit.

### Case C — Challenging / Integrated: Conditional Expectation + Law of Total Expectation

**Soal**

Misalkan joint PMF $(X,Y)$ diberikan oleh:

| $Y$ | $X=0$ | $X=2$ | $X=4$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $0$ | $0.30$ | $0.20$ | $0.10$ |
| $1$ | $0.05$ | $0.15$ | $0.20$ |

Tentukan:

1. $E[X\mid Y=0]$;
2. $E[X\mid Y=1]$;
3. $E[X]$ menggunakan law of total expectation;
4. verifikasi $E[X]$ langsung dari marginal $X$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **1. Parse Narasi & Target**  
> Soal meminta conditional means untuk dua state $Y$, lalu meng-average conditional means untuk memperoleh unconditional expectation.
>
> **2. Identify Structure / Distribution**  
> Joint PMF diskrit.
>
> **3. Support / Domain / Region**  
>
> $$
> X\in\{0,2,4\},
> \qquad
> Y\in\{0,1\}.
> $$
>
> Marginal $Y$:
>
> $$
> P(Y=0)=0.30+0.20+0.10=0.60,
> $$
>
> $$
> P(Y=1)=0.05+0.15+0.20=0.40.
> $$
>
> **4. Setup**
>
> Untuk $Y=0$:
>
> $$
> p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid0)
> =
> \frac{p_{X,Y}(x,0)}{0.60}.
> $$
>
> Untuk $Y=1$:
>
> $$
> p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid1)
> =
> \frac{p_{X,Y}(x,1)}{0.40}.
> $$
>
> **5. Calculation**
>
> Conditional mean ketika $Y=0$:
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y=0]
> =
> 0\left(\frac{0.30}{0.60}\right)
> +
> 2\left(\frac{0.20}{0.60}\right)
> +
> 4\left(\frac{0.10}{0.60}\right)
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \frac23+\frac23
> =
> \boxed{\frac43}.
> $$
>
> Conditional mean ketika $Y=1$:
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y=1]
> =
> 0\left(\frac{0.05}{0.40}\right)
> +
> 2\left(\frac{0.15}{0.40}\right)
> +
> 4\left(\frac{0.20}{0.40}\right)
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> 0.75+2
> =
> \boxed{\frac{11}{4}}.
> $$
>
> Maka sebagai fungsi random variable:
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y]
> =
> \begin{cases}
> \frac43, & Y=0,\\[4pt]
> \frac{11}{4}, & Y=1.
> \end{cases}
> $$
>
> Law of total expectation:
>
> $$
> E[X]
> =
> E\{E[X\mid Y]\}
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \frac43(0.60)
> +
> \frac{11}{4}(0.40)
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> 0.8+1.1
> =
> \boxed{1.9}.
> $$
>
> **Direct verification**
>
> Marginal $X$:
>
> $$
> P(X=0)=0.35,
> $$
>
> $$
> P(X=2)=0.35,
> $$
>
> $$
> P(X=4)=0.30.
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> E[X]
> =
> 0(0.35)+2(0.35)+4(0.30)
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> 0.70+1.20
> =
> \boxed{1.90}.
> $$
>
> Kedua metode cocok.
>
> **6. Interpretation**  
> $E[X\mid Y]$ berubah sesuai state $Y$. Unconditional mean diperoleh dengan memberi weight setiap conditional mean berdasarkan probabilitas state $Y$.
>
> **7. Verification**
>
> - $P(Y=0)+P(Y=1)=1$.
> - Setiap conditional PMF normalize ke $1$.
> - $E[X]=1.9$ berada dalam range support $[0,4]$.
> - Direct expectation dan total expectation menghasilkan nilai sama.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case C
> - **Trap:** menjumlahkan conditional means tanpa weighting.
> - **Distractor:** $\frac43+\frac{11}{4}$.
> - **Mengapa salah:** law of total expectation memerlukan expectation terhadap distribution $Y$.
> - **Shortcut aman:** buat tabel kecil `state Y | conditional mean | P(Y=y) | product`.
> - **Target waktu:** sekitar 2–3 menit.

## Section 5 — Verification & Sanity Check

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check
> 1. **Conditional normalization:** pastikan conditional PMF/PDF totalnya $1$.
> 2. **Support check:** conditional mean harus compatible dengan conditional support. Jika $a<X<b$ conditional, maka expectation harus berada di antara $a$ dan $b$ bila finite.
> 3. **Variance check:** $\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y)\ge0$.
> 4. **Second-moment check:** harus berlaku
>
> $$
> E[X^2\mid Y=y]
> \ge
> \{E[X\mid Y=y]\}^2.
> $$
>
> 5. **Total expectation check:** hasil
>
> $$
> E\{E[X\mid Y]\}
> $$
>
> harus sama dengan direct $E[X]$ bila keduanya dihitung benar.
> 6. **Conditioning-value check:** denominator conditioning harus valid:
>    - diskrit: $P(Y=y)>0$;
>    - kontinu: conditional density formulation digunakan untuk $f_Y(y)>0$.
> 7. **Function check:** bila $E[X\mid Y=y]=g(y)$, jangan lupa
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y]=g(Y).
> $$

### Metode Alternatif

#### Method 1 — Full Conditional Distribution

Gunakan:

$$
p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}
$$

atau

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}.
$$

Lalu hitung expectation.

**Best when:**
- conditional distribution juga ditanyakan;
- beberapa conditional probabilities/moments dibutuhkan.

---

#### Method 2 — Slice and Normalize

Untuk continuous case pada fixed $y_0$:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y_0)
=
\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y_0)}
{\int f_{X,Y}(u,y_0)\,du}.
$$

Ini menghindari derivasi full marginal $f_Y(y)$ bila hanya satu conditioning value diperlukan.

**Best when:** hanya diminta $E[X\mid Y=y_0]$ atau variance pada satu nilai tertentu.

---

#### Method 3 — Direct Variance vs Second-Moment Shortcut

Direct:

$$
E[(X-\mu_{X\mid y})^2\mid Y=y].
$$

Shortcut:

$$
E[X^2\mid Y=y]-E[X\mid Y=y]^2.
$$

**Exam preference:** biasanya second-moment shortcut lebih cepat.

---

#### Method 4 — Direct Expectation vs Total Expectation

Direct:

$$
E[X]
=
\sum_x xp_X(x)
$$

atau

$$
E[X]
=
\int xf_X(x)\,dx.
$$

Conditioning:

$$
E[X]
=
E\{E[X\mid Y]\}.
$$

**Conditioning lebih unggul** jika $X\mid Y$ jauh lebih sederhana dibanding unconditional distribution $X$.

## Section 6 — Connections & Mental Model

### 6.1 Dari Conditional Distribution ke Conditional Moment

```text
Joint PMF/PDF
      ↓
Marginal conditioning variable
      ↓
Conditional PMF/PDF
      ↓
Conditional first moment
      ↓
Conditional second moment
      ↓
Conditional variance
```

Karena itu [[3.3 Distribusi Bersyarat (Conditional Distribution)]] adalah prerequisite langsung.

---

### 6.2 Conditioning sebagai Information Update

Tanpa informasi $Y$:

$$
E[X]
$$

adalah global average.

Setelah $Y=y$ diketahui:

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]
$$

adalah average dalam subpopulation/slice yang compatible dengan informasi tersebut.

Jika nilai ini berubah kuat terhadap $y$, maka informasi $Y$ sangat mempengaruhi lokasi conditional distribution $X$.

---

### 6.3 $E[X\mid Y]$ sebagai Random Variable

Misalnya:

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]=2+3y.
$$

Maka:

$$
E[X\mid Y]=2+3Y.
$$

Karena $Y$ random, quantity tersebut juga random.

Lalu:

$$
E[X]
=
E[2+3Y]
=
2+3E[Y].
$$

Ini adalah pola hitung yang sangat penting untuk compound models di [[3.7 Distribusi Majemuk (Compound Distribution)]].

---

### 6.4 Connection ke Independence

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent dan conditional expectation finite, mengetahui $Y=y$ tidak mengubah distribution $X$. Maka conditional mean tidak bergantung pada $y$:

$$
E[X\mid Y=y]=E[X].
$$

Gunakan ini sebagai **diagnostic connection**, bukan sebagai pengganti criterion independence yang dibahas di [[3.5 Independensi dan Korelasi]].

---

### 6.5 Connection ke Compound Distribution

Pada model compound/random sum, conditioning variable sering berupa **count**.

Mental strategy:

```text
Condition on count N
      ↓
Compute expected aggregate given N
      ↓
Obtain a simple function of N
      ↓
Average over N
      ↓
Unconditional expected aggregate
```

Ini adalah aplikasi natural dari:

$$
E[X]=E\{E[X\mid Y]\}.
$$

## Section 7 — Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

| Trap | Kesalahan | Mengapa Terjadi | Pencegahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional-support trap | memakai marginal support, bukan slice support | lupa bahwa fixing $Y=y$ mengubah range $X$ | tulis inequality support setelah substitute $y$ |
| Joint-vs-conditional trap | integrasi langsung terhadap joint PDF | joint density belum normalize pada slice | divide by marginal / normalize slice |
| Wrong denominator | divide by $f_X(x)$ saat mencari $X\mid Y$ | arah conditioning tertukar | variable kanan bar menentukan denominator |
| First-vs-second moment trap | $E[X^2\mid Y]$ dianggap square mean | notation terlihat mirip | hitung kedua quantities terpisah |
| Variance negativity | algebra memberi variance negatif | second moment/square salah | variance harus $\ge0$ |
| Random-variable trap | $E[X\mid Y]$ dianggap angka | lupa hasil bergantung pada random $Y$ | derive $g(y)$ lalu replace $y$ dengan $Y$ |
| Total expectation trap | conditional means dijumlah tanpa weight | lupa outer expectation | weight by $p_Y(y)$ / integrate with $f_Y(y)$ |
| Independence shortcut trap | conditional mean constant ⇒ otomatis independent | mean saja tidak menentukan full distribution | independence membutuhkan criterion distributional |
| Bounds trap | integral $-\infty$ ke $\infty$ tanpa membaca support | formula generic dihafal | use actual conditional support |
| Continuous point trap | memakai $P(Y=y)$ di denominator | point probability zero untuk continuous | use conditional density ratio |

## Section 8 — Executive Summary

> [!SUMMARY] Must Remember
> 1. Conditional expectation dihitung terhadap **conditional PMF/PDF**, bukan joint density secara langsung.
> 2. Diskrit:
>
> $$
> E[u(X)\mid Y=y]
> =
> \sum_xu(x)p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y).
> $$
>
> 3. Kontinu:
>
> $$
> E[u(X)\mid Y=y]
> =
> \int u(x)f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx.
> $$
>
> 4. Conditional mean:
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y=y].
> $$
>
> 5. Conditional variance:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y=y)
> =
> E[X^2\mid Y=y]-E[X\mid Y=y]^2.
> $$
>
> 6. Conditional support berasal dari **joint support setelah conditioning value difix**.
> 7. Jika
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y=y]=g(y),
> $$
>
> maka
>
> $$
> E[X\mid Y]=g(Y).
> $$
>
> 8. Law of total expectation:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> E[X]=E\{E[X\mid Y]\}.
> }
> $$
>
> 9. Untuk discrete $Y$:
>
> $$
> E[X]
> =
> \sum_yE[X\mid Y=y]p_Y(y).
> $$
>
> 10. Conditioning sangat berguna ketika conditional problem lebih sederhana daripada unconditional problem.

### Formula Map

| Target | Formula | Key Requirement | Common Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conditional mean, discrete | $\sum_x x p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)$ | valid conditional support | use joint PMF |
| Conditional mean, continuous | $\int x f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)dx$ | correct bounds | integrate over marginal support |
| Conditional function expectation | $\sum/\int u(x)\,f_{X\mid Y}$ | expectation exists | use $u(E[X\mid Y])$ |
| Conditional second moment | $E[X^2\mid Y=y]$ | square $x$ before averaging | square conditional mean |
| Conditional variance | $E[X^2\mid Y=y]-E[X\mid Y=y]^2$ | finite second moment | sign/algebra error |
| Random-variable form | $E[X\mid Y]=g(Y)$ | first obtain $g(y)$ | treat as constant |
| Total expectation | $E[X]=E\{E[X\mid Y]\}$ | expectation exists | omit weighting over $Y$ |

### Trigger Keywords

| Jika soal mengatakan... | Pikirkan... |
|---|---|
| “expected value of $X$ given $Y=y$” | conditional PMF/PDF → first moment |
| “conditional mean” | same as $E[X\mid Y=y]$ |
| “conditional variance” | conditional first + second moments |
| “given $Y=y$” + triangular support | slice support first |
| “find $E[X]$ using conditioning” | law of total expectation |
| “$E[X\mid Y=y]=g(y)$” | replace $y$ by $Y$ for $E[X\mid Y]$ |
| “random sum / count” | condition on count; see 3.7 |
| conditional density already given | skip joint/marginal derivation |
