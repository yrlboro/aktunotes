> [!IMPORTANT] Core Exam Principle
> Jangan memilih distribusi dari “bentuk formula yang terasa familiar”. Mulai dari mekanisme:
>
> $$
> \text{narasi}
> \rightarrow
> \text{support}
> \rightarrow
> \text{stochastic mechanism}
> \rightarrow
> \text{parameterization}
> \rightarrow
> \text{target quantity}
> \rightarrow
> \text{calculation}.
> $$

> [!IMPORTANT] Parameterization Discipline
> Untuk Gamma dan Exponential, **scale vs rate** dapat mengubah seluruh formula.  
> Convention utama note ini mengikuti Hogg–Tanis/Miller:
>
> $$
> \Gamma(\alpha,\theta)
> $$
>
> dengan $\alpha$ = shape dan $\theta$ = **scale**, sehingga
>
> $$
> E[X]=\alpha\theta,\qquad
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=\alpha\theta^2.
> $$
>
> Untuk Exponential, $\theta$ adalah mean/scale:
>
> $$
> E[X]=\theta,\qquad
> f_X(x)=\frac1\theta e^{-x/\theta}.
> $$
>
> Hogg–McKean–Craig juga menggunakan Gamma$(\alpha,\beta)$ dengan $\beta$ sebagai scale. Bila rate $\lambda$ digunakan, mapping-nya adalah
>
> $$
> \lambda=\frac1\theta.
> $$

## Section 1 — Master Recognition Map

| Distribusi | Mechanism / Interpretation | Support | Mean | Variance |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Uniform$(a,b)$ | seluruh lokasi dalam interval equally dense | $a<x<b$ | $\frac{a+b}{2}$ | $\frac{(b-a)^2}{12}$ |
| Exponential$(\theta)$ | waiting time ke occurrence pertama / constant hazard | $x>0$ | $\theta$ | $\theta^2$ |
| Gamma$(\alpha,\theta)$ | positive waiting-time/aggregate family; waiting time ke occurrence ke-$\alpha$ untuk integer shape dalam Poisson-process setting | $x>0$ | $\alpha\theta$ | $\alpha\theta^2$ |
| Normal$(\mu,\sigma^2)$ | symmetric bell-shaped location-scale family | $-\infty<x<\infty$ | $\mu$ | $\sigma^2$ |

Mental split:

```text
Constant density on finite interval
→ Uniform

Positive waiting time, first event, memoryless
→ Exponential

Positive waiting time / sum of iid exponentials / kth-event waiting time
→ Gamma

Symmetric bell shape with location μ and spread σ
→ Normal
```

## Section 2 — Uniform Distribution

### 2.1 Definition & Support

Hogg–Tanis dan Miller mendefinisikan continuous Uniform pada interval $[a,b]$ sebagai density constant:

$$
\boxed{
f_X(x)=
\frac1{b-a},
\qquad a<x<b,
}
$$

dan $0$ elsewhere, dengan

$$
a<b.
$$

Notation:

$$
\boxed{X\sim U(a,b).}
$$

Karena density constant, probability hanya bergantung pada **panjang interval**.

Untuk

$$
a\le c<d\le b,
$$

$$
\boxed{
P(c<X<d)
=
\frac{d-c}{b-a}.
}
$$

Ini adalah ratio:

$$
\frac{\text{favorable length}}
{\text{total support length}}.
$$

---

### 2.2 CDF

Untuk $X\sim U(a,b)$,

$$
\boxed{
F_X(x)=
\begin{cases}
0,&x<a,\\[4pt]
\dfrac{x-a}{b-a},&a\le x<b,\\[8pt]
1,&x\ge b.
\end{cases}
}
$$

Pada interior support, CDF linear karena density konstan.

---

### 2.3 Mean & Variance

Miller memberikan:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\frac{a+b}{2}
}
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
\frac{(b-a)^2}{12}.
}
$$

Interpretation:

- mean = midpoint support;
- variance hanya bergantung pada width $b-a$;
- shifting interval tanpa mengubah width tidak mengubah variance.

---

### 2.4 MGF

Untuk $t\ne0$,

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_X(t)
&=
\int_a^b e^{tx}\frac1{b-a}\,dx\\
&=
\frac{e^{bt}-e^{at}}{t(b-a)}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Maka

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)=
\frac{e^{bt}-e^{at}}{t(b-a)},
\qquad t\ne0,
}
$$

dengan

$$
M_X(0)=1.
$$

> [!TIP] Recognition by Geometry
> Jika density constant pada interval finite, jangan integralkan dari awal bila target probability hanya panjang subinterval.

---

### 2.5 Linear Transformation Property

Jika

$$
Y\sim U(0,1)
$$

dan

$$
W=a+(b-a)Y,
$$

maka Hogg–Tanis memberi exercise yang menunjukkan:

$$
\boxed{
W\sim U(a,b).
}
$$

Connection ke [[2.4 Transformasi Variabel Acak Univariat]]:

$$
U(0,1)
\xrightarrow{\;a+(b-a)y\;}
U(a,b).
$$

---

### 2.6 Uniform Exam Traps

> [!BUG] Support Trap
> Formula
>
> $$
> \frac{d-c}{b-a}
> $$
>
> hanya dapat langsung digunakan jika interval target berada di dalam support. Jika event melewati $a$ atau $b$, intersect event dengan support dulu.

> [!BUG] Density-vs-Probability Trap
> $1/(b-a)$ adalah **density**, bukan probability satu titik.

## Section 3 — Exponential Distribution

### 3.1 Parameterization — Scale/Mean Convention

Main convention:

$$
X\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\theta),
\qquad \theta>0,
$$

dengan

$$
\boxed{
f_X(x)=\frac1\theta e^{-x/\theta},
\qquad x>0.
}
$$

Di sini:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\theta.
}
$$

Jika memakai rate notation

$$
\lambda=\frac1\theta,
$$

maka equivalent density:

$$
f_X(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x},
\qquad x>0.
$$

> [!DANGER] Parameterization Trap
> Jangan memasukkan $\theta$ ke formula rate:
>
> $$
> e^{-\theta x}
> $$
>
> kecuali source memang mendefinisikan $\theta$ sebagai rate.  
> Pada convention note ini, exponent adalah
>
> $$
> -x/\theta.
> $$

---

### 3.2 CDF & Survival Function

Integrate density:

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_X(x)
&=
\int_0^x
\frac1\theta e^{-u/\theta}\,du\\
&=
1-e^{-x/\theta}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
F_X(x)=
\begin{cases}
0,&x<0,\\
1-e^{-x/\theta},&x\ge0.
\end{cases}
}
$$

Upper tail / survival:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>x)=e^{-x/\theta},
\qquad x>0.
}
$$

Ini salah satu shortcut paling penting dalam Topik 2.

---

### 3.3 Mean, Variance, MGF

Sebagai Gamma dengan shape $1$:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\theta
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\theta^2
}
$$

MGF:

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)=\frac1{1-\theta t},
\qquad t<\frac1\theta.
}
$$

---

### 3.4 Quantile / Median

Hogg–Tanis menurunkan median dari

$$
F(m)=0.5.
$$

Karena

$$
1-e^{-m/\theta}=0.5,
$$

maka

$$
e^{-m/\theta}=0.5
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
m=\theta\ln2.
}
$$

Lebih umum, untuk quantile probability $p$:

$$
F(x_p)=p
$$

memberikan

$$
1-e^{-x_p/\theta}=p,
$$

sehingga

$$
\boxed{
x_p=-\theta\ln(1-p).
}
$$

---

### 3.5 Poisson–Exponential Connection

Miller dan Hogg–Tanis membangun Exponential dari Poisson process.

Jika occurrences mengikuti Poisson process dengan rate

$$
\lambda
$$

per unit time, maka waiting time hingga **first occurrence** mempunyai

$$
\theta=\frac1\lambda
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
X\sim\operatorname{Exp}\left(\frac1\lambda\right).
}
$$

Contoh structure:

$$
P(X>t)
=
P(\text{zero occurrences during }[0,t])
=
e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

Dari sini:

$$
f_X(t)=\lambda e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

> [!TIP] Recognition Pair
> - **Poisson** → number of events in interval.
> - **Exponential** → waiting time to next/first event.
>
> Jangan tertukar antara count dan time.

---

### 3.6 Memoryless Property

Hogg–Tanis menekankan property:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>x+y\mid X>x)
=
P(X>y).
}
$$

Proof:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(X>x+y\mid X>x)
&=
\frac{P(X>x+y)}{P(X>x)}\\
&=
\frac{e^{-(x+y)/\theta}}
{e^{-x/\theta}}\\
&=
e^{-y/\theta}\\
&=
P(X>y).
\end{aligned}
$$

Interpretation:

> Setelah object sudah survive $x$ units, probabilistic waiting time tambahan tidak “mengingat” usia yang sudah berlalu.

Hogg–Tanis juga menyatakan Exponential adalah continuous distribution pada $(0,\infty)$ dengan forgetfulness property ini dalam discussion mereka.

---

### 3.7 Constant Hazard

Hogg–McKean–Craig membahas hazard:

$$
r(x)=\frac{f_X(x)}{1-F_X(x)}.
$$

Untuk Exponential:

$$
r(x)
=
\frac{\theta^{-1}e^{-x/\theta}}
{e^{-x/\theta}}
=
\boxed{\frac1\theta}.
$$

Jadi hazard constant.

Ini konsisten dengan memorylessness.

## Section 4 — Gamma Distribution

### 4.1 Parameterization — Shape/Scale

Main convention:

$$
X\sim\Gamma(\alpha,\theta)
$$

dengan:

- $\alpha>0$ = **shape**;
- $\theta>0$ = **scale**.

PDF:

$$
\boxed{
f_X(x)
=
\frac{
x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\theta}
}{
\Gamma(\alpha)\theta^\alpha
},
\qquad x>0.
}
$$

dan $0$ elsewhere.

Gamma function:

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma(\alpha)
=
\int_0^\infty
u^{\alpha-1}e^{-u}\,du,
\qquad \alpha>0.
}
$$

Recursion:

$$
\Gamma(\alpha)
=
(\alpha-1)\Gamma(\alpha-1).
$$

Untuk positive integer $n$:

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!.
}
$$

---

### 4.2 Mean, Variance, MGF

Untuk shape–scale convention:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\alpha\theta
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\alpha\theta^2
}
$$

MGF:

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)
=
(1-\theta t)^{-\alpha},
\qquad
t<\frac1\theta.
}
$$

> [!DANGER] Rate-vs-Scale Trap
> Jika source memakai rate $\lambda$, density ditulis
>
> $$
> \frac{\lambda^\alpha}{\Gamma(\alpha)}
> x^{\alpha-1}e^{-\lambda x},
> $$
>
> dengan
>
> $$
> E[X]=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda},
> \qquad
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda^2}.
> $$
>
> Mapping:
>
> $$
> \lambda=\frac1\theta.
> $$

---

### 4.3 Exponential sebagai Special Case

Set

$$
\alpha=1.
$$

Karena

$$
\Gamma(1)=1,
$$

Gamma density menjadi

$$
f_X(x)
=
\frac1\theta e^{-x/\theta}.
$$

Maka:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Exp}(\theta)
=
\Gamma(1,\theta).
}
$$

---

### 4.4 Shape & Mode

Miller meminta menunjukkan bahwa jika

$$
\alpha>1,
$$

Gamma density memiliki mode pada

$$
\boxed{
x_{\text{mode}}=\theta(\alpha-1).
}
$$

Jika

$$
\alpha=1,
$$

density menurun dari boundary $0$ seperti Exponential.

Jika

$$
0<\alpha<1,
$$

density sangat tinggi dekat $0$.

Mental shape map:

```text
0 < α < 1 → strong right skew, spike near 0
α = 1     → Exponential
α > 1     → interior mode at θ(α−1)
larger α  → less skewed / more concentrated relatively
```

---

### 4.5 Additive Property

Hogg–McKean–Craig menyatakan:

Jika

$$
X_i\sim\Gamma(\alpha_i,\theta),
$$

independent dan **memiliki scale $\theta$ yang sama**, maka

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^nX_i
\sim
\Gamma\left(
\sum_{i=1}^n\alpha_i,
\theta
\right).
}
$$

Proof via MGF:

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{\sum X_i}(t)
&=
\prod_i(1-\theta t)^{-\alpha_i}\\
&=
(1-\theta t)^{-\sum_i\alpha_i}.
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!WARNING] Common-Scale Requirement
> Jangan menambahkan shape secara otomatis jika scales berbeda.

---

### 4.6 Sum of iid Exponentials

Jika

$$
X_1,\ldots,X_n
\stackrel{\text{iid}}{\sim}
\operatorname{Exp}(\theta),
$$

maka setiap $X_i$ adalah

$$
\Gamma(1,\theta).
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^nX_i
\sim
\Gamma(n,\theta).
}
$$

Hogg–Tanis dan Miller menunjukkan relationship ini dengan MGF.

Mean sum:

$$
n\theta.
$$

Variance sum:

$$
n\theta^2.
$$

---

### 4.7 Waiting Time to the $\alpha$th Poisson Event

Hogg–Tanis menurunkan distribution waiting time $W$ hingga occurrence ke-$\alpha$ dalam Poisson process dengan rate $\lambda$.

Untuk integer $\alpha$:

$$
P(W\le w)
=
1-P(\text{fewer than }\alpha\text{ events by }w).
$$

Dengan Poisson mean $\lambda w$:

$$
F_W(w)
=
1-
\sum_{k=0}^{\alpha-1}
\frac{(\lambda w)^ke^{-\lambda w}}{k!}.
$$

Differentiating menghasilkan Gamma density dengan:

$$
\theta=\frac1\lambda.
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
W\sim\Gamma\left(\alpha,\frac1\lambda\right).
}
$$

Ini memberi relationship penting:

```text
Poisson process count
↓
waiting time to 1st event → Exponential
↓
waiting time to kth event → Gamma
```

---

### 4.8 Gamma–Poisson CDF Relationship

Hogg–McKean–Craig mencatat relationship untuk integer shape $k$ antara upper Gamma tail dan cumulative Poisson probability. Ini membantu memahami bahwa waiting-time event dan event-count statement adalah dua representasi process yang sama.

Untuk exam, gunakan connection ini hanya jika setup Poisson process memang jelas; jangan memaksakan relationship bila soal hanya memberi generic Gamma variable.

## Section 5 — Normal Distribution

### 5.1 Definition & Parameterization

Gunakan notation:

$$
X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2),
\qquad \sigma>0.
$$

PDF:

$$
\boxed{
f_X(x)
=
\frac1{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}
\exp\left[
-\frac12
\left(
\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}
\right)^2
\right],
\qquad -\infty<x<\infty.
}
$$

Parameter:

- $\mu$ = location = mean;
- $\sigma^2$ = variance;
- $\sigma$ = standard deviation.

> [!IMPORTANT] Notation Trap
> Miller menulis density sebagai $n(x;\mu,\sigma)$, tetapi parameter kedua di formula density adalah **standard deviation $\sigma$**.  
> Dalam note CF2 kita menulis distribution sebagai
>
> $$
> N(\mu,\sigma^2)
> $$
>
> agar variance eksplisit.

---

### 5.2 Mean, Variance, Symmetry

Miller membuktikan melalui MGF bahwa

$$
\boxed{E[X]=\mu}
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{\operatorname{Var}(X)=\sigma^2.}
$$

Normal density symmetric terhadap

$$
x=\mu.
$$

Karena symmetry:

$$
P(X<\mu)=P(X>\mu)=\frac12.
$$

Mode dan median juga berada di $\mu$ untuk Normal.

---

### 5.3 MGF

Miller memberikan:

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)
=
\exp\left(
\mu t+\frac12\sigma^2t^2
\right),
\qquad -\infty<t<\infty.
}
$$

Differentiating:

$$
M_X'(0)=\mu,
$$

$$
M_X''(0)=\mu^2+\sigma^2,
$$

sehingga:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
M_X''(0)-[M_X'(0)]^2
=
\sigma^2.
$$

---

### 5.4 Standard Normal

Jika

$$
X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2),
$$

define:

$$
\boxed{
Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}.
}
$$

Maka:

$$
\boxed{
Z\sim N(0,1).
}
$$

CDF standard Normal ditulis:

$$
\boxed{
\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z).
}
$$

Karena standard Normal symmetric:

$$
\boxed{
\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z).
}
$$

Equivalent upper tail:

$$
\boxed{
P(Z>z)=1-\Phi(z)=\Phi(-z).
}
$$

---

### 5.5 Probability via Standardization

Target:

$$
P(a<X<b).
$$

Standardize both bounds:

$$
z_a=\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma},
\qquad
z_b=\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}.
$$

Then:

$$
\boxed{
P(a<X<b)
=
\Phi(z_b)-\Phi(z_a).
}
$$

Upper tail:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>x)
=
1-
\Phi\left(
\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}
\right).
}
$$

Lower tail:

$$
\boxed{
P(X<x)
=
\Phi\left(
\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}
\right).
}
$$

---

### 5.6 Quantile Transformation

Jika

$$
P(X\le x_p)=p,
$$

dan $z_p$ adalah standard Normal quantile:

$$
\Phi(z_p)=p,
$$

maka

$$
\boxed{
x_p=\mu+\sigma z_p.
}
$$

Conversely:

$$
\boxed{
z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}.
}
$$

---

### 5.7 Linear Transformation

Dari MGF:

Jika

$$
X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)
$$

dan

$$
Y=aX+b,
$$

maka

$$
\boxed{
Y\sim
N(a\mu+b,a^2\sigma^2).
}
$$

Special case standardization:

$$
a=\frac1\sigma,
\qquad
b=-\frac\mu\sigma,
$$

memberikan $N(0,1)$.

---

### 5.8 Sum of Independent Normal Variables

Jika

$$
X_i\sim N(\mu_i,\sigma_i^2)
$$

independent, maka berdasarkan product MGF:

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^nX_i
\sim
N\left(
\sum_i\mu_i,
\sum_i\sigma_i^2
\right).
}
$$

Untuk independent linear combination:

$$
Y=\sum_i a_iX_i,
$$

$$
\boxed{
Y\sim
N\left(
\sum_i a_i\mu_i,
\sum_i a_i^2\sigma_i^2
\right).
}
$$

> [!DANGER] Variance Sign Trap
> Jika $Y=X_1-X_2$ dan independent,
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(Y)
> =
> \sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2,
> $$
>
> karena $(-1)^2=1$.

## Section 6 — Uniform vs Exponential vs Gamma vs Normal

| Feature | Uniform | Exponential | Gamma | Normal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Support | finite interval | $(0,\infty)$ | $(0,\infty)$ | $\mathbb R$ |
| Shape | flat | decreasing | flexible right-skew | symmetric bell |
| Main parameter clue | endpoints | mean/scale or rate | shape + scale | mean + variance |
| Memoryless | No | **Yes** | generally No | No |
| Count/waiting connection | None central | first Poisson waiting time | kth Poisson waiting time | sampling/measurement model |
| Standardization | affine to $U(0,1)$ | scale by $\theta$ | scale by $\theta$ | $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$ |
| MGF | $\frac{e^{bt}-e^{at}}{t(b-a)}$ | $(1-\theta t)^{-1}$ | $(1-\theta t)^{-\alpha}$ | $e^{\mu t+\sigma^2t^2/2}$ |

---

## Section 7 — Distribution Recognition Decision Tree

```text
Continuous random variable
│
├─ Finite interval and constant density?
│   └─ Uniform
│
├─ Positive support and waiting time to first event?
│   └─ Exponential
│
├─ Positive support and waiting time to kth event /
│  sum of exponentials / shape parameter?
│   └─ Gamma
│
└─ Symmetric bell-shaped location-scale model?
    └─ Normal
```

Second-level check:

```text
If Gamma/Exponential:
Write parameterization first.

If Normal:
Write μ and σ², then standardize.

If Uniform:
Probability = interval length ratio.

If Exponential:
Try survival function e^(−x/θ) before integrating.

If Gamma:
Look for special shape or additive property.
```

## Section 8 — High-Yield Relationships

### 8.1 Exponential is Gamma with Shape 1

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Exp}(\theta)
=
\Gamma(1,\theta).
}
$$

---

### 8.2 Sum of iid Exponentials

$$
X_i\stackrel{iid}{\sim}\operatorname{Exp}(\theta)
$$

implies

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^nX_i
\sim\Gamma(n,\theta).
}
$$

---

### 8.3 Poisson Count vs Exponential/Gamma Waiting Time

If Poisson process rate is $\lambda$:

$$
N(t)\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda t),
$$

waiting time to first event:

$$
T_1\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1/\lambda),
$$

waiting time to kth event:

$$
\boxed{
T_k\sim\Gamma(k,1/\lambda).
}
$$

### 8.4 Normal Standardization

$$
X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)
$$

iff

$$
\boxed{
\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1).
}
$$

From [[2.4 Transformasi Variabel Acak Univariat]], for continuous strictly increasing $F_X$:

$$
F_X(X)\sim U(0,1).
$$

This is a useful conceptual bridge:

$$
\text{any suitable continuous distribution}
\rightarrow
U(0,1).

## Section 9 — Worked Exam Cases

### Case A — Uniform

Suppose

$$
X\sim U(2,10).
$$

Find:

$$
P(4<X<7).
$$

Support width:

$$
10-2=8.
$$

Target width:

$$
7-4=3.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
P(4<X<7)=\frac38.
}
$$

**Sanity check:** target interval is less than half total width, so probability below $0.5$ is reasonable.

---

### Case B — Exponential Tail

Suppose lifetime $X$ has mean

$$
\theta=500
$$

hours.

Find:

$$
P(X>800).
$$

Use survival directly:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>800)
=
e^{-800/500}
=
e^{-1.6}.
}
$$

No integral is needed.

---

### Case C — Exponential Conditional Probability

Suppose $X\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\theta)$.

Find:

$$
P(X>900\mid X>300).
$$

By memorylessness:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>900\mid X>300)
=
P(X>600)
=
e^{-600/\theta}.
}
$$

Alternative verification:

$$
\frac{P(X>900)}{P(X>300)}
=
\frac{e^{-900/\theta}}{e^{-300/\theta}}
=
e^{-600/\theta}.
$$

---

### Case D — Gamma Sum

Let

$$
X_1,X_2,X_3
\stackrel{iid}{\sim}
\operatorname{Exp}(\theta).
$$

For

$$
Y=X_1+X_2+X_3,
$$

MGF:

$$
M_Y(t)
=
[(1-\theta t)^{-1}]^3
=
(1-\theta t)^{-3}.
$$

Hence:

$$
\boxed{
Y\sim\Gamma(3,\theta).
}
$$

Then:

$$
E[Y]=3\theta,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=3\theta^2.
$$

---

### Case E — Normal Probability

Suppose

$$
X\sim N(100,15^2).
$$

Find:

$$
P(X>130).
$$

Standardize:

$$
z=
\frac{130-100}{15}
=
2.
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>130)
=
1-\Phi(2).
}
$$

Using standard Normal table:

$$
\Phi(2)\approx0.9772,
$$

thus

$$
\boxed{
P(X>130)\approx0.0228.
}
$$

---

### Case F — Normal Interval

For the same distribution,

$$
P(85<X<115)
$$

has bounds

$$
z_1=\frac{85-100}{15}=-1,
$$

$$
z_2=\frac{115-100}{15}=1.
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
P(85<X<115)
=
\Phi(1)-\Phi(-1).
}
$$

Using symmetry:

$$
\Phi(-1)=1-\Phi(1),
$$

so

$$
P(-1<Z<1)
=
2\Phi(1)-1.
$$

## Section 10 — Common Exam Traps

### Trap 1 — Rate vs Scale

For Exponential:

Scale convention:

$$
f(x)=\frac1\theta e^{-x/\theta},
\qquad E[X]=\theta.
$$

Rate convention:

$$
f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x},
\qquad E[X]=\frac1\lambda.
$$

Do not mix:

$$
\boxed{\lambda=1/\theta.}
$$

---

### Trap 2 — Gamma Parameterization

Shape–scale:

$$
E[X]=\alpha\theta,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\alpha\theta^2.
$$

Shape–rate:

$$
E[X]=\frac\alpha\lambda,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac\alpha{\lambda^2}.
$$

Always write PDF/support before using memorized moments.

---

### Trap 3 — Poisson vs Exponential

“Number of arrivals in 2 hours” → Poisson.

“Time until next arrival” → Exponential.

“Time until 5th arrival” → Gamma.

---

### Trap 4 — Normal Variance vs Standard Deviation

If:

$$
X\sim N(100,225),
$$

then:

$$
\mu=100,
\qquad
\sigma^2=225,
\qquad
\sigma=15.
$$

Standardization uses **$\sigma=15$**, not $225$.

---

### Trap 5 — Standardization Direction

Correct:

$$
Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}.
$$

For threshold $x$:

$$
z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}.
$$

Not:

$$
\frac{\mu-x}{\sigma}.
$$

unless event is intentionally algebraically reversed.

---

### Trap 6 — Normal Exact vs CLT Approximation

A Normal population gives Normal linear combinations/sample means under the relevant independence assumptions **exactly**.

CLT gives an **approximation/limiting result** for many non-Normal settings.

Do not label an exact Normal result as “by CLT” when underlying variables are already Normal and source result is exact.

---

### Trap 7 — Exponential Endpoint

Because $X$ is continuous:

$$
P(X>x)=P(X\ge x).
$$

Do not waste time distinguishing strict/inclusive endpoints.

---

### Trap 8 — Uniform Probability Outside Support

If $X\sim U(0,5)$,

$$
P(-2<X<3)
=
P(0<X<3)
=
\frac35,
$$

not

$$
\frac{3-(-2)}5=1.
$$

Intersect with support first.

---

### Trap 9 — Gamma Additivity without Common Scale

Only use

$$
\sum X_i
\sim
\Gamma\left(\sum\alpha_i,\theta\right)
$$

when independent Gamma variables share the same scale $\theta$.

## Section 11 — Fast Calculation Templates

### Template A — Uniform

Given

$$
X\sim U(a,b).
$$

For interval target:

1. intersect target with $[a,b]$;
2. compute favorable length;
3. divide by
   $$
   b-a.
   $$

Formula:

$$
\boxed{
P(c<X<d)=\frac{d-c}{b-a}
}
$$

when $a\le c<d\le b$.

---

### Template B — Exponential Probability

Given scale/mean $\theta$:

$$
F(x)=1-e^{-x/\theta}.
$$

Use:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>x)=e^{-x/\theta}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
P(X\le x)=1-e^{-x/\theta}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
P(a<X<b)=e^{-a/\theta}-e^{-b/\theta}.
}
$$

---

### Template C — Exponential Memorylessness

For $s,t>0$:

$$
\boxed{
P(X>s+t\mid X>s)=P(X>t).
}
$$

---

### Template D — Gamma Recognition

1. support positive?
2. density resembles
   $$
   x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\theta}?
   $$
3. identify shape $\alpha$ and scale $\theta$;
4. immediately write:
   $$
   E[X]=\alpha\theta,
   $$
   $$
   \operatorname{Var}(X)=\alpha\theta^2.
   $$

If sum of independent same-scale Gammas:

$$
\alpha_{\text{sum}}
=
\sum\alpha_i.
$$

---

### Template E — Normal Probability

Given:

$$
X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2).
$$

1. compute:
   $$
   \sigma=\sqrt{\sigma^2};
   $$
2. standardize:
   $$
   z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma};
   $$
3. rewrite event in $Z$;
4. use $\Phi$;
5. use complement/symmetry if shorter.

---

### Template F — Normal Quantile

Given probability $p$:

1. find $z_p$ such that
   $$
   \Phi(z_p)=p;
   $$
2. transform:
   $$
   \boxed{
   x_p=\mu+\sigma z_p.
   }
   $$

## Section 12 — Formula Sheet

### Uniform$(a,b)$

$$
f_X(x)
=
\frac1{b-a},
\qquad a<x<b.
$$

$$
F_X(x)
=
\begin{cases}
0,&x<a,\\
\dfrac{x-a}{b-a},&a\le x<b,\\
1,&x\ge b.
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\frac{a+b}{2}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{(b-a)^2}{12}
}
$$

$$
M_X(t)
=
\frac{e^{bt}-e^{at}}{t(b-a)},
\qquad t\ne0.
$$

---

### Exponential$(\theta)$ — Scale/Mean

$$
f_X(x)
=
\frac1\theta e^{-x/\theta},
\qquad x>0.
$$

$$
F_X(x)
=
1-e^{-x/\theta},
\qquad x\ge0.
$$

$$
\boxed{
P(X>x)=e^{-x/\theta}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\theta
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\theta^2
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)=(1-\theta t)^{-1},
\qquad t<1/\theta.
}
$$

---

### Gamma$(\alpha,\theta)$ — Shape/Scale

$$
f_X(x)
=
\frac{x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\theta}}
{\Gamma(\alpha)\theta^\alpha},
\qquad x>0.
$$

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\alpha\theta
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\alpha\theta^2
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)=(1-\theta t)^{-\alpha},
\qquad t<1/\theta.
}
$$

Mode for $\alpha>1$:

$$
\boxed{
x_{\text{mode}}=\theta(\alpha-1).
}
$$

---

### Normal$(\mu,\sigma^2)$

$$
f_X(x)
=
\frac1{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}
e^{-\frac12((x-\mu)/\sigma)^2},
\qquad x\in\mathbb R.
$$

$$
\boxed{
E[X]=\mu,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\sigma^2.
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t)
=
e^{\mu t+\frac12\sigma^2t^2}.
}
$$

Standardization:

$$
\boxed{
Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1).
}
$$

Probability:

$$
\boxed{
P(a<X<b)
=
\Phi\left(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\right)
-
\Phi\left(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\right).
}
$$

## Section 16 — Reverse Engineering Parameters

### 16.1 Gamma from Mean & Variance

Under shape–scale convention:

$$
\mu=\alpha\theta,
$$

$$
\sigma^2=\alpha\theta^2.
$$

Divide:

$$
\frac{\sigma^2}{\mu}
=
\theta.
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
\theta=\frac{\sigma^2}{\mu}.
}
$$

Then:

$$
\boxed{
\alpha
=
\frac{\mu}{\theta}
=
\frac{\mu^2}{\sigma^2}.
}
$$

This is a useful exam shortcut.

---

### 16.2 Exponential from Mean

If

$$
E[X]=m,
$$

then scale convention gives immediately:

$$
\boxed{\theta=m.}
$$

Rate:

$$
\boxed{\lambda=1/m.}
$$

---

### 16.3 Uniform from Mean & Width

If width:

$$
w=b-a
$$

and mean:

$$
m=\frac{a+b}{2},
$$

then:

$$
\boxed{
a=m-\frac w2,
\qquad
b=m+\frac w2.
}
$$

---

### 16.4 Normal from Variance

If variance given:

$$
\sigma^2=v,
$$

always compute:

$$
\boxed{
\sigma=\sqrt v
}
$$

before standardization.

## Section 17 — Verification Checklist

> [!CHECK] Setelah calculation, cek:
> 1. Probability berada di $[0,1]$.
> 2. Support distribution sesuai narrative.
> 3. Untuk Uniform, result proporsional terhadap interval length.
> 4. Untuk Exponential/Gamma, support tidak boleh negatif.
> 5. Exponential tail mengecil saat threshold naik.
> 6. Gamma mean dan variance consistent dengan declared scale/rate.
> 7. Normal $z$ sign sesuai posisi threshold terhadap $\mu$.
> 8. Upper-tail Normal di atas mean harus $<0.5$.
> 9. Normal variance memakai squared scale; standardization memakai standard deviation.
> 10. Jika memakai MGF, cek $M(0)=1$.
> 11. Jika distribution identified from sum, independence assumptions dan common parameters yang dibutuhkan sudah terpenuhi.
> 12. Jika result berupa approximation, jangan menyebutnya exact.
