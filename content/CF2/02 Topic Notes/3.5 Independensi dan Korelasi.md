## Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Pada joint distribution, dua random variables dapat berhubungan dalam dua level yang berbeda:

1. **Distributional dependence**  
   Apakah mengetahui nilai satu random variable mengubah distribution random variable lainnya?

2. **Linear association**  
   Apakah keduanya cenderung bergerak bersama secara linear?

**Independence** menjawab pertanyaan pertama.  
**Covariance/correlation** mengukur aspek kedua.

Mental model:

```text
Joint distribution
      ↓
Check factorization
      ↓
Independent or dependent?
      ↓
Compute joint moments
      ↓
Cov(X,Y)
      ↓
Standardize
      ↓
rho(X,Y)
```

Hubungan yang sangat penting:

```text
Independent
    ↓
E[XY] = E[X]E[Y]
    ↓
Cov(X,Y) = 0
    ↓
rho = 0   (if variances > 0)
```

Tetapi arah sebaliknya **tidak valid secara umum**:

```text
Cov(X,Y) = 0
     ✗
does NOT imply independence
```

## Section 2 — Independence of Random Variables

### 2.1 Criterion dari Joint PMF/PDF

Untuk random variables diskrit:

$$
\boxed{
p_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
p_X(x)p_Y(y)
}
$$

untuk semua pasangan $(x,y)$ yang relevan jika dan hanya jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent.

Untuk continuous random variables:

$$
\boxed{
f_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
f_X(x)f_Y(y)
}
$$

pada support yang relevan jika dan hanya jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent.

Practical workflow:

```text
Joint PMF/PDF
      ↓
Find marginals
      ↓
Multiply marginals
      ↓
Compare with joint
      ↓
Factorization holds everywhere?
```

> [!TIP] Fast Recognition
> Jika support merupakan **product set**
>
> $$
> S_{X,Y}=S_X\times S_Y
> $$
>
> dan joint density/mass dapat difaktorkan menjadi fungsi $x$ saja dikali fungsi $y$ saja, independence sering dapat dikenali sangat cepat.
>
> Namun rectangular support saja **tidak cukup**. Formula joint juga harus factorize dengan benar.

---

### 2.2 Support sebagai Independence Diagnostic

Jika joint support **tidak** berbentuk product set, misalnya

$$
0<x<y<1,
$$

maka $X$ dan $Y$ tidak dapat independent.

Mengapa?

Untuk independent variables, setiap kombinasi nilai marginal yang valid seharusnya dapat muncul pada joint support:

$$
S_{X,Y}=S_X\times S_Y.
$$

Pada triangular support, misalnya $x=0.8$ dan $y=0.2$ masing-masing mungkin berada pada marginal support, tetapi pasangan $(0.8,0.2)$ tidak diizinkan oleh $x<y$.

> [!WARNING] Independence Trap
> **Non-product support ⇒ dependent.**  
> Tetapi **product support ⇏ independent** tanpa factorization density/mass.

---

### 2.3 Criterion dari Joint CDF

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberi criterion:

$$
\boxed{
F_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
F_X(x)F_Y(y)
}
$$

untuk semua $(x,y)$ jika dan hanya jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent.

Ini berguna jika soal memberi joint CDF secara langsung.

---

### 2.4 Probability-Rectangle Criterion

Untuk independent $X,Y$:

$$
P(a<X\le b,\ c<Y\le d)
=
P(a<X\le b)\,
P(c<Y\le d).
$$

Hogg, McKean & Craig menunjukkan bahwa jika relationship ini berlaku untuk setiap rectangle, relationship tersebut juga merupakan criterion independence.

Pada exam, satu counterexample sudah cukup untuk membuktikan **dependence**.

---

### 2.5 Independence melalui Conditional Distribution

Jika denominator valid, independence ekuivalen dengan conditional distribution yang sama dengan marginal:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=f_X(x).
$$

Artinya:

> knowing $Y=y$ does not update the distribution of $X$.

Ini menghubungkan topik ini dengan [[3.3 Distribusi Bersyarat (Conditional Distribution)]].

## Section 3 — Joint Moment Generating Function

### 3.1 Definisi Joint MGF

Untuk random vector $(X,Y)$:

$$
\boxed{
M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
=
E\left[e^{t_1X+t_2Y}\right]
}
$$

jika expectation tersebut ada pada neighborhood dari $(0,0)$.

Marginal MGFs diperoleh dengan membuat parameter random variable lain sama dengan nol:

$$
\boxed{
M_X(t_1)=M_{X,Y}(t_1,0)
}
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
M_Y(t_2)=M_{X,Y}(0,t_2).
}
$$

Mental rule:

```text
joint MGF → set other t = 0 → marginal MGF
```

---

### 3.2 Joint MGF dan Independence

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberikan theorem:

Jika joint MGF exists, maka $X$ dan $Y$ independent jika dan hanya jika

$$
\boxed{
M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
=
M_X(t_1)M_Y(t_2)
}
$$

atau ekuivalen:

$$
M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
=
M_{X,Y}(t_1,0)
M_{X,Y}(0,t_2).
$$

Derivasi arah independence $\Rightarrow$ factorization:

$$
\begin{aligned}
M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
&=
E[e^{t_1X+t_2Y}]\\
&=
E[e^{t_1X}e^{t_2Y}]\\
&=
E[e^{t_1X}]E[e^{t_2Y}]\\
&=
M_X(t_1)M_Y(t_2),
\end{aligned}
$$

karena untuk independent variables:

$$
E[u(X)v(Y)]
=
E[u(X)]E[v(Y)].
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] MGF Existence
> Criterion factorization via MGF digunakan ketika joint MGF memang ada pada neighborhood yang sesuai. Jangan menggunakan “MGF factorization test” tanpa existence condition.

---

### 3.3 Joint Moments dari Joint MGF

Mixed derivatives joint MGF menghasilkan joint raw moments:

$$
\boxed{
\left.
\frac{\partial^{r+s}}
{\partial t_1^r\partial t_2^s}
M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
\right|_{(0,0)}
=
E[X^rY^s].
}
$$

Khusus:

$$
\left.
\frac{\partial M}{\partial t_1}
\right|_{(0,0)}
=
E[X],
$$

$$
\left.
\frac{\partial M}{\partial t_2}
\right|_{(0,0)}
=
E[Y],
$$

$$
\left.
\frac{\partial^2 M}
{\partial t_1\partial t_2}
\right|_{(0,0)}
=
E[XY].
$$

Maka covariance dapat diperoleh dari:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
=
\left.
\frac{\partial^2M}
{\partial t_1\partial t_2}
\right|_{(0,0)}
-
E[X]E[Y].
$$

> [!TIP] Exam Recognition
> Jika soal memberikan joint MGF eksplisit, derivatives biasanya lebih cepat daripada mencoba recover joint PDF/PMF terlebih dahulu.

---

## Section 4 — Covariance

### 4.1 Definisi

Jika means dan product expectation terkait finite:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
=
E[(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)].
}
$$

Expanding:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
&=
E[XY-\mu_XY-\mu_YX+\mu_X\mu_Y]\\
&=
E[XY]
-\mu_XE[Y]
-\mu_YE[X]
+\mu_X\mu_Y\\
&=
E[XY]-\mu_X\mu_Y.
\end{aligned}
$$

Jadi formula hitung utama:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
=
E[XY]-E[X]E[Y].
}
$$

---

### 4.2 Joint Product Moment $E[XY]$

Diskrit:

$$
\boxed{
E[XY]
=
\sum_x\sum_y
xy\,p_{X,Y}(x,y).
}
$$

Kontinu:

$$
\boxed{
E[XY]
=
\int\!\!\int
xy\,f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx\,dy.
}
$$

Perhatikan:

- $E[X]$ dan $E[Y]$ dapat dihitung dari marginal distributions.
- $E[XY]$ memerlukan **joint information**.

Ini karena covariance mengukur bagaimana dua variables bergerak **bersama**, bukan bagaimana masing-masing berperilaku sendirian.

---

### 4.3 Equivalent Identity

Dari covariance:

$$
\boxed{
E[XY]
=
E[X]E[Y]+\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y).
}
$$

Jika correlation tersedia:

$$
\boxed{
E[XY]
=
\mu_X\mu_Y+\rho_{X,Y}\sigma_X\sigma_Y.
}
$$

Formula ini sering berguna saat soal memberi means, standard deviations, dan correlation tanpa joint distribution eksplisit.

---

### 4.4 Independence Implies Zero Covariance

Jika $X$ dan $Y$ independent:

$$
E[XY]
=
E[X]E[Y].
$$

Maka:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0.
}
$$

Jika variances positif:

$$
\boxed{
\rho_{X,Y}=0.
}
$$

Tetapi:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0
\not\Rightarrow
X,Y\text{ independent}
}
$$

secara umum.

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberikan counterexample discrete dengan probability mass $1/4$ pada

$$
(-1,0),\ (0,-1),\ (1,0),\ (0,1).
$$

Di sini:

$$
E[X]=E[Y]=0,
$$

dan karena setiap support point memiliki $XY=0$,

$$
E[XY]=0.
$$

Jadi:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0.
$$

Namun joint support bukan product set dari marginal supports, sehingga variables dependent.

> [!DANGER] Exam-Writing Trap
> **Zero correlation means no linear association, not automatically no dependence.**

---

## Section 5 — Correlation Coefficient

### 5.1 Definisi

Jika $\sigma_X>0$ dan $\sigma_Y>0$:

$$
\boxed{
\rho_{X,Y}
=
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}
{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}.
}
$$

Correlation adalah covariance yang sudah distandardisasi.

Karena dibagi oleh standard deviations, correlation tidak membawa unit.

---

### 5.2 Range Correlation

Hogg, McKean & Craig menunjukkan:

$$
\boxed{
-1\le\rho_{X,Y}\le1.
}
$$

Jadi setiap hasil numerik correlation harus berada pada interval tersebut.

> [!CHECK] Immediate Sanity Check
> Jika perhitungan menghasilkan
>
> $$
> |\rho|>1,
> $$
>
> maka pasti ada kesalahan pada covariance atau variance/standard deviation.

---

### 5.3 Interpretasi Sign

Dari definition:

$$
\rho
=
\frac{
E[(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)]
}
{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}.
$$

- Jika $X$ dan $Y$ cenderung berada **di atas mean bersama-sama** atau **di bawah mean bersama-sama**, product deviations cenderung positif → $\rho>0$.
- Jika satu cenderung berada di atas mean ketika lainnya di bawah mean → product deviations cenderung negatif → $\rho<0$.
- Jika positive dan negative products saling menyeimbangkan → $\rho$ dapat dekat nol.

Correlation mengukur **linear association**, bukan seluruh bentuk dependence.

---

### 5.4 $\rho=\pm1$

Source Hogg, McKean & Craig menghubungkan equality case dengan linear relationship hampir pasti.

Secara exam intuition:

- $\rho=1$: perfect positive linear relationship.
- $\rho=-1$: perfect negative linear relationship.

Jangan menginterpretasikan nilai correlation di antara $-1$ dan $1$ sebagai probability.

---

## Section 6 — Core Calculation Pathways

### 6.1 Jika Diberi Joint PMF/PDF dan Diminta Independence

```text
1. Identify joint support
2. Find marginal p_X/f_X
3. Find marginal p_Y/f_Y
4. Compare joint with product marginals
5. State independent/dependent
```

Quick stop:

- non-product support → dependent immediately.
- one point violating factorization → dependent immediately.

---

### 6.2 Jika Diberi Joint CDF

Check:

$$
F_{X,Y}(x,y)
\stackrel{?}{=}
F_X(x)F_Y(y).
$$

Marginals dapat diperoleh dari limits joint CDF:

$$
F_X(x)
=
\lim_{y\to\infty}F_{X,Y}(x,y),
$$

$$
F_Y(y)
=
\lim_{x\to\infty}F_{X,Y}(x,y).
$$

---

### 6.3 Jika Diberi Joint MGF

1. Compute:

$$
M_X(t_1)=M(t_1,0),
$$

$$
M_Y(t_2)=M(0,t_2).
$$

2. Compare:

$$
M(t_1,t_2)
\stackrel{?}{=}
M_X(t_1)M_Y(t_2).
$$

3. Jika ya dan MGF exists appropriately → independent.

---

### 6.4 Jika Diminta Covariance dan Correlation

Gunakan calculation ladder:

```text
E[X]
E[Y]
E[X²]
E[Y²]
E[XY]
   ↓
Var(X), Var(Y)
   ↓
Cov(X,Y)
   ↓
rho
```

Formulas:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
E[X^2]-E[X]^2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y)
=
E[Y^2]-E[Y]^2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
=
E[XY]-E[X]E[Y],
$$

$$
\rho
=
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}
{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)\operatorname{Var}(Y)}}.
$$

## Section 7 — Worked Cases

### Case A — Fundamental: Independence dari Joint PMF Factorization

Diberikan joint PMF:

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
\frac{xy^2}{30},
\qquad
x=1,2,3,\quad y=1,2.
$$

Tentukan apakah $X$ dan $Y$ independent.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **1. Parse & Support**
>
> Support:
>
> $$
> S_X=\{1,2,3\},
> \qquad
> S_Y=\{1,2\},
> $$
>
> sehingga joint support rectangular.
>
> **2. Marginal $X$**
>
> $$
> p_X(x)
> =
> \sum_{y=1}^{2}\frac{xy^2}{30}
> =
> \frac{x(1+4)}{30}
> =
> \frac{x}{6}.
> $$
>
> **3. Marginal $Y$**
>
> $$
> p_Y(y)
> =
> \sum_{x=1}^{3}\frac{xy^2}{30}
> =
> \frac{(1+2+3)y^2}{30}
> =
> \frac{y^2}{5}.
> $$
>
> **4. Product**
>
> $$
> p_X(x)p_Y(y)
> =
> \frac{x}{6}\frac{y^2}{5}
> =
> \frac{xy^2}{30}
> =
> p_{X,Y}(x,y).
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \boxed{X\text{ dan }Y\text{ independent}.}
> $$
>
> **5. Verification**
>
> Factorization berlaku pada seluruh product support.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case A
> Rectangular support **hanya memungkinkan** independence; yang membuktikan independence adalah equality joint = product marginals.

---

### Case B — Exam-Typical: Covariance dan Correlation dari Joint PMF

Gunakan textbook-style joint PMF:

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
\frac{x+2y}{18},
\qquad
x=1,2,\quad y=1,2.
$$

Tentukan covariance dan correlation.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Marginals:
>
> $$
> p_X(x)
> =
> \frac{x+3}{9},
> \qquad x=1,2,
> $$
>
> $$
> p_Y(y)
> =
> \frac{3+4y}{18},
> \qquad y=1,2.
> $$
>
> Means:
>
> $$
> E[X]
> =
> 1\left(\frac49\right)
> +
> 2\left(\frac59\right)
> =
> \frac{14}{9},
> $$
>
> $$
> E[Y]
> =
> 1\left(\frac7{18}\right)
> +
> 2\left(\frac{11}{18}\right)
> =
> \frac{29}{18}.
> $$
>
> Second moments:
>
> $$
> E[X^2]
> =
> 1^2\frac49+2^2\frac59
> =
> \frac{24}{9},
> $$
>
> sehingga
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)
> =
> \frac{24}{9}
> -
> \left(\frac{14}{9}\right)^2
> =
> \frac{20}{81}.
> $$
>
> Untuk $Y$:
>
> $$
> E[Y^2]
> =
> 1^2\frac7{18}
> +
> 2^2\frac{11}{18}
> =
> \frac{51}{18},
> $$
>
> sehingga
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(Y)
> =
> \frac{51}{18}
> -
> \left(\frac{29}{18}\right)^2
> =
> \frac{77}{324}.
> $$
>
> Joint product moment:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> E[XY]
> &=
> (1)(1)\frac3{18}
> +(2)(1)\frac4{18}\\
> &\quad
> +(1)(2)\frac5{18}
> +(2)(2)\frac6{18}\\
> &=
> \frac{45}{18}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Covariance:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> &=
> E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]\\
> &=
> \frac{45}{18}
> -
> \left(\frac{14}{9}\right)
> \left(\frac{29}{18}\right)\\
> &=
> -\frac1{162}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> =
> -\frac1{162}
> }.
> $$
>
> Correlation:
>
> $$
> \rho
> =
> \frac{-1/162}
> {\sqrt{(20/81)(77/324)}}
> =
> -\frac1{\sqrt{1540}}
> \approx
> \boxed{-0.025}.
> $$
>
> **Interpretation**
>
> Linear association sangat lemah dan sedikit negatif. Tetapi jangan mengganti statement tersebut menjadi “almost independent”; independence adalah criterion distributional yang terpisah.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case B
> - Jangan menggunakan $E[X]E[Y]$ sebagai $E[XY]$ kecuali independence sudah terbukti.
> - Jangan membagi covariance dengan variances; denominator correlation adalah **standard deviations**:
>
> $$
> \sigma_X\sigma_Y.
> $$

### Case C — Integrated: Continuous Joint PDF + Correlation

Diberikan:

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)
=
x+y,
\qquad
0<x<1,\quad 0<y<1.
$$

Tentukan $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$ dan $\rho$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **1. Support**
>
> Unit square:
>
> $$
> 0<x<1,\qquad0<y<1.
> $$
>
> **2. Means**
>
> Karena symmetry:
>
> $$
> E[X]=E[Y].
> $$
>
> Hitung:
>
> $$
> E[X]
> =
> \int_0^1\int_0^1
> x(x+y)\,dy\,dx
> =
> \frac7{12}.
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \mu_X=\mu_Y=\frac7{12}.
> $$
>
> **3. Variances**
>
> $$
> E[X^2]
> =
> \int_0^1\int_0^1
> x^2(x+y)\,dy\,dx.
> $$
>
> Evaluasi menghasilkan:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)
> =
> \operatorname{Var}(Y)
> =
> \frac{11}{144}.
> $$
>
> **4. Joint Product Moment**
>
> $$
> E[XY]
> =
> \int_0^1\int_0^1
> xy(x+y)\,dy\,dx.
> $$
>
> Hasil covariance textbook:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> =
> -\frac1{144}.
> $$
>
> **5. Correlation**
>
> Karena kedua variances sama:
>
> $$
> \rho
> =
> \frac{-1/144}
> {\sqrt{(11/144)(11/144)}}
> =
> \boxed{-\frac1{11}}.
> $$
>
> **6. Sanity Check**
>
> $$
> -1<-\frac1{11}<1.
> $$

> [!TIP] Symmetry Shortcut
> Jika joint density symmetric terhadap pertukaran $x$ dan $y$ dan support juga symmetric, maka sering:
>
> $$
> E[X]=E[Y],
> \qquad
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=\operatorname{Var}(Y),
> $$
>
> sehingga computation dapat dipotong hampir setengah.

---

### Case D — Conceptual Trap: Zero Correlation tetapi Dependent

Misalkan probability mass masing-masing $1/4$ berada pada:

$$
(-1,0),\quad(0,-1),\quad(1,0),\quad(0,1).
$$

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Marginal distributions symmetric, sehingga:
>
> $$
> E[X]=E[Y]=0.
> $$
>
> Pada setiap support point:
>
> $$
> XY=0.
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> E[XY]=0.
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> =
> 0-0(0)
> =
> 0.
> $$
>
> Dengan nonzero variances:
>
> $$
> \rho=0.
> $$
>
> Tetapi $X$ dan $Y$ **tidak independent**. Misalnya marginal supports adalah
>
> $$
> S_X=S_Y=\{-1,0,1\},
> $$
>
> namun banyak pasangan dalam product set tidak muncul pada joint support.
>
> Contoh:
>
> $$
> P(X=1,Y=1)=0,
> $$
>
> sedangkan
>
> $$
> P(X=1)P(Y=1)
> =
> \frac14\cdot\frac14
> =
> \frac1{16}.
> $$
>
> Karena:
>
> $$
> 0\neq\frac1{16},
> $$
>
> variables dependent.
>
> **Lesson**
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \rho=0
> \not\Rightarrow
> \text{independence}
> }.
> $$

### Case E — Joint MGF Factorization

Diberikan:

$$
M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
=
\frac{1}{1-t_1}
\cdot
\frac{1}{(1-t_2)^2},
$$

untuk parameter values yang membuat MGF finite.

Tentukan apakah $X$ dan $Y$ independent.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Marginal MGF $X$:
>
> $$
> M_X(t_1)
> =
> M(t_1,0)
> =
> \frac{1}{1-t_1}.
> $$
>
> Marginal MGF $Y$:
>
> $$
> M_Y(t_2)
> =
> M(0,t_2)
> =
> \frac{1}{(1-t_2)^2}.
> $$
>
> Product:
>
> $$
> M_X(t_1)M_Y(t_2)
> =
> \frac{1}{1-t_1}
> \frac{1}{(1-t_2)^2},
> $$
>
> yang sama dengan joint MGF.
>
> Jadi, dengan existence condition:
>
> $$
> \boxed{X\text{ dan }Y\text{ independent}.}
> $$

> [!WARNING] MGF Trap
> Jangan hanya memeriksa bahwa expression “terlihat seperti dua faktor.” Pastikan satu faktor benar-benar hanya bergantung pada $t_1$ dan satu lagi hanya pada $t_2$, serta masing-masing sama dengan marginal MGF.

## Section 8 — Verification & Sanity Checks

> [!CHECK] Sanity Checks
>
> **Independence**
>
> - Joint support harus compatible dengan product marginal supports.
> - Jika factorization gagal pada satu valid point → dependent.
> - Jika conditional distribution berubah dengan conditioning value → dependent.
>
> **Covariance**
>
> - Pastikan $E[XY]$ dihitung dari joint distribution.
> - Jika independence telah terbukti, covariance harus $0$.
>
> **Correlation**
>
> - Denominator:
>
> $$
> \sigma_X\sigma_Y
> =
> \sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)\operatorname{Var}(Y)}.
> $$
>
> - Harus:
>
> $$
> -1\le\rho\le1.
> $$
>
> - Jika covariance $0$ dan variances positive → $\rho=0$.
> - $\rho=0$ tidak cukup untuk independence.
>
> **MGF**
>
> - Check:
>
> $$
> M(0,0)=1.
> $$
>
> - Marginals:
>
> $$
> M_X(t)=M(t,0),
> \qquad
> M_Y(t)=M(0,t).
> $$

## Section 9 — Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

| Trap | Kesalahan | Mengapa Salah | Pencegahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rectangular-support trap | langsung menyatakan independent | product support hanya necessary-looking geometry, belum membuktikan factorization | check joint = product marginals |
| Non-product support ignored | mencoba factorization panjang pada triangular support | dependence sudah terlihat dari support | inspect support first |
| Zero-covariance trap | $\operatorname{Cov}=0$ ⇒ independent | converse false generally | distinguish uncorrelated vs independent |
| Joint moment trap | $E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]$ tanpa proof independence | equality tidak berlaku umum | compute joint moment |
| Correlation denominator trap | divide by variances | correlation uses SDs | take square roots |
| Correlation range trap | menerima $|\rho|>1$ | mathematically impossible | immediate sanity check |
| Joint-MGF trap | salah mengambil marginal MGF | lupa set parameter lain $=0$ | $M_X(t_1)=M(t_1,0)$ |
| MGF-independence trap | product MGF tanpa existence condition | theorem assumes MGF exists | state condition |
| Conditional-vs-marginal trap | confuse $f_{X|Y}$ with $f_X$ | equality only under independence | compare explicitly |
| One-point proof trap | satu equality dipakai untuk prove independence | independence requires equality everywhere | one counterexample disproves; proof requires full criterion |
| Correlation interpretation trap | $\rho=0.8$ disebut “80% dependent” | correlation not probability | interpret as standardized linear association |
| Sign confusion | negative covariance dianggap negative probability | covariance can be negative | separate probability bounds from moment measures |

## Section 10 — Formula Map

| Target | Formula | Requirement | Fast Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| PMF independence | $p_{X,Y}=p_Xp_Y$ | all valid support points | support + factorization |
| PDF independence | $f_{X,Y}=f_Xf_Y$ | all relevant points | product support |
| CDF independence | $F_{X,Y}=F_XF_Y$ | all $x,y$ | marginals via limits |
| Joint MGF | $M(t_1,t_2)=E[e^{t_1X+t_2Y}]$ | exists locally | $M(0,0)=1$ |
| MGF independence | $M(t_1,t_2)=M_X(t_1)M_Y(t_2)$ | joint MGF exists | set one $t=0$ |
| Joint moment | $E[X^rY^s]$ | finite moment | mixed derivative / direct integration |
| Covariance | $E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]$ | finite second-related moments | independence ⇒ $0$ |
| Correlation | $\operatorname{Cov}/(\sigma_X\sigma_Y)$ | $\sigma_X,\sigma_Y>0$ | $|\rho|\le1$ |
| Product expectation | $E[XY]=\mu_X\mu_Y+\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$ | expectations exist | useful if $\rho$ given |
| Independent product expectation | $E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]$ | independence | yields zero covariance |

## Section 11 — Connections & Mental Model

### 11.1 Independence vs Conditional Distribution

Jika independent:

$$
f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)
=
f_X(x).
$$

Jadi conditioning tidak membawa information update.

---

### 11.2 Independence vs Sampling

Random sample dari infinite population biasanya dimodelkan sebagai iid:

$$
X_1,\ldots,X_n.
$$

Huruf pertama pada iid — **independent** — memungkinkan joint distribution factorization:

$$
f(x_1,\ldots,x_n)
=
\prod_{i=1}^n f(x_i).
$$

Ini menjadi fondasi untuk likelihood dan sampling distributions di Topik 4.

---

### 11.3 Covariance ke Variance-Covariance Matrix

Untuk dua variables:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X),
\quad
\operatorname{Var}(Y),
\quad
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
$$

dapat disusun menjadi matrix. Generalization ini adalah inti [[3.6 Matriks Variansi-Kovariansi]].

---

### 11.4 Correlation sebagai Standardized Covariance

Covariance bergantung pada units.

Jika:

- $X$ dalam rupiah,
- $Y$ dalam tahun,

maka covariance membawa product unit tersebut.

Correlation menghilangkan scale dengan membagi standard deviations:

$$
\rho
=
\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}
{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}.
$$

Karena itu correlation unitless.

---

### 11.5 Joint MGF sebagai Jembatan

Joint MGF menggabungkan beberapa fungsi sekaligus:

```text
joint distribution
      ↓
joint MGF
      ├── marginal MGF
      ├── moments of X
      ├── moments of Y
      ├── joint moments E[X^rY^s]
      └── independence factorization
```

Untuk CF2, ini membuat joint MGF bukan sekadar alat transformasi, tetapi juga alat diagnosis dependence dan perhitungan moments.

## Section 12 — Executive Summary

> [!SUMMARY] Must Remember
>
> 1. Independence adalah property **seluruh joint distribution**, bukan sekadar covariance.
>
> 2. Diskrit:
>
> $$
> p_{X,Y}(x,y)
> =
> p_X(x)p_Y(y).
> $$
>
> 3. Kontinu:
>
> $$
> f_{X,Y}(x,y)
> =
> f_X(x)f_Y(y).
> $$
>
> 4. Joint CDF:
>
> $$
> F_{X,Y}(x,y)
> =
> F_X(x)F_Y(y).
> $$
>
> 5. Joint MGF:
>
> $$
> M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)
> =
> E[e^{t_1X+t_2Y}].
> $$
>
> 6. Jika joint MGF exists:
>
> $$
> X\perp Y
> \iff
> M_{X,Y}(t_1,t_2)=M_X(t_1)M_Y(t_2).
> $$
>
> 7. Mixed derivative:
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial^2M}{\partial t_1\partial t_2}(0,0)
> =
> E[XY].
> $$
>
> 8. Covariance:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> =
> E[XY]-E[X]E[Y].
> }
> $$
>
> 9. Correlation:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \rho
> =
> \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}
> {\sigma_X\sigma_Y}.
> }
> $$
>
> 10. Always:
>
> $$
> -1\le\rho\le1.
> $$
>
> 11. Independence implies:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0.
> $$
>
> 12. Tetapi:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0
> \not\Rightarrow
> X\perp Y.
> $$
