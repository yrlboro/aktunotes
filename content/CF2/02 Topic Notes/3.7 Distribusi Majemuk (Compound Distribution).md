# Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Misalkan ada beberapa model kemungkinan untuk random variable $X$.

Contoh sederhana:

- dengan probabilitas $p_1$, $X$ berasal dari distribution $f_1$;
- dengan probabilitas $p_2$, $X$ berasal dari distribution $f_2$;
- dan seterusnya.

Kita tidak mengobservasi langsung “model mana yang dipilih”. Distribution akhir $X$ adalah **weighted mixture** dari seluruh component distributions.

Mental model:

```text
Choose component / parameter Θ
          ↓
X | Θ follows a simpler conditional distribution
          ↓
Θ itself has a mixing distribution
          ↓
Average / integrate over Θ
          ↓
Unconditional (compound / mixture) distribution of X
```

Secara probabilistik:

$$
\boxed{
\text{conditional model} + \text{mixing model}
\longrightarrow
\text{marginal compound model}
}
$$

Ini adalah aplikasi langsung dari ide joint → marginal:

$$
f_{X,\Theta}(x,\theta)
=
f_{X\mid\Theta}(x\mid\theta)g_\Theta(\theta),
$$

lalu integrate/sum out $\Theta$.

# Section 2 — Finite Mixture Distribution

## 2.1 Setup

Misalkan terdapat $k$ distributions dengan:

$$
f_1(x),f_2(x),\ldots,f_k(x),
$$

support masing-masing $S_1,\ldots,S_k$, mean:

$$
\mu_1,\ldots,\mu_k,
$$

variance:

$$
\sigma_1^2,\ldots,\sigma_k^2,
$$

serta mixing probabilities:

$$
p_i>0,
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i=1.
$$

Mixture PDF:

$$
\boxed{
f(x)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i f_i(x)
}
$$

pada:

$$
S=\bigcup_{i=1}^{k}S_i.
$$

Untuk discrete distributions, formula identik dengan PMF:

$$
\boxed{
p_X(x)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i p_i^{(component)}(x)
}
$$

(dengan notation component PMF harus dibedakan dari mixing weights).

> [!TIP] Mental Rule
> **Mixture probability = weighted average of component probabilities/densities.**

---

## 2.2 Mixture CDF

Karena integration bersifat linear:

$$
\boxed{
F(x)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_iF_i(x)
}
$$

Jadi probability interval juga dapat diperoleh sebagai weighted average component probabilities:

$$
P(X\in A)
=
\sum_i p_iP_i(X\in A).
$$

---

# Section 3 — Mean dan Variance Finite Mixture

## 3.1 Mean

Hogg, McKean & Craig memperoleh:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i\mu_i
}
$$

Jadi mixture mean hanyalah **weighted mean of component means**.

Ini juga dapat dipahami sebagai total expectation.

Jika $C$ adalah indicator/component label:

$$
E[X]
=
E\{E[X\mid C]\}.
$$

---

## 3.2 Variance

Variance mixture **bukan** sekadar weighted average component variances.

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberikan:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i\sigma_i^2
+
\sum_{i=1}^{k}p_i(\mu_i-\mu)^2
}
$$

where:

$$
\mu=\sum_i p_i\mu_i.
$$

Interpretasi:

$$
\boxed{
\text{total variance}
=
\text{within-component variance}
+
\text{between-component variance}
}
$$

Term pertama:

$$
\sum_i p_i\sigma_i^2
$$

= weighted average dari variability **di dalam** tiap component.

Term kedua:

$$
\sum_i p_i(\mu_i-\mu)^2
$$

= variability **antar-component means**.

> [!DANGER] Major Exam Trap
> Salah:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)
> =
> \sum_i p_i\sigma_i^2.
> $$
>
> Itu hanya benar jika semua component means sama, sehingga between-component term bernilai nol.

---

## 3.3 Connection ke Law of Total Variance

Dengan component indicator $C$:

$$
E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid C)]
=
\sum_i p_i\sigma_i^2,
$$

sedangkan:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid C])
=
\sum_i p_i(\mu_i-\mu)^2.
$$

Sehingga formula source sejalan dengan:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
E\{\operatorname{Var}(X\mid C)\}
+
\operatorname{Var}\{E[X\mid C]\}.
}
$$

---

# Section 4 — Mixture ≠ Linear Combination

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberikan warning eksplisit bahwa mixture dari distributions **bukan** hal yang sama dengan linear combination random variables.

Mixture:

$$
f_X(x)=p f_1(x)+(1-p)f_2(x).
$$

Artinya:

> satu component distribution dipilih secara acak, lalu $X$ berasal dari component tersebut.

Linear combination:

$$
Y=aX_1+bX_2.
$$

Artinya:

> kedua random variables muncul bersamaan dan nilai akhirnya adalah algebraic combination.

> [!IMPORTANT] Important Distinction
> Jangan menggunakan formula variance linear combination dari [[3.6 Matriks Variansi-Kovariansi]] untuk mixture distribution.

# Section 5 — Continuous Mixing / Parameter Compounding

Finite mixture memakai weights $p_1,\dots,p_k$.

Hogg, McKean & Craig memperluas konsep ini: mixing variable dapat kontinu.

Misalkan:

$$
X\mid\Theta=\theta
\sim
f(x\mid\theta),
$$

sedangkan:

$$
\Theta\sim g(\theta).
$$

Joint distribution:

$$
\boxed{
f_{X,\Theta}(x,\theta)
=
f(x\mid\theta)g(\theta)
}
$$

Unconditional / compound distribution:

$$
\boxed{
h(x)
=
\int g(\theta)f(x\mid\theta)\,d\theta
}
$$

Jika mixing variable diskrit:

$$
\boxed{
h(x)
=
\sum_\theta g(\theta)f(x\mid\theta).
}
$$

Mental model:

```text
Parameter Θ is random
      ↓
Conditional distribution X | Θ
      ↓
Multiply by distribution of Θ
      ↓
Integrate Θ out
      ↓
Marginal / compound distribution of X
```

> [!TIP] Recognition Clue
> Narasi seperti **“parameter $\theta$ sendiri dipilih secara acak / memiliki distribution”** hampir selalu meminta conditioning + marginalization.

# Section 6 — Core Example: Poisson–Gamma Compounding

Ini contoh paling penting dalam §3.7 karena hasil compound-nya menjadi distribution familiar.

## 6.1 Conditional Model

Misalkan:

$$
X\mid\Theta=\theta
\sim
\operatorname{Poisson}(\theta).
$$

Conditional PMF:

$$
P(X=x\mid\Theta=\theta)
=
\frac{\theta^xe^{-\theta}}{x!},
\qquad x=0,1,2,\ldots
$$

Mixing distribution:

$$
\Theta\sim\Gamma(\alpha,\beta),
$$

mengikuti **shape–scale** convention source:

$$
g(\theta)
=
\frac{1}{\beta^\alpha\Gamma(\alpha)}
\theta^{\alpha-1}e^{-\theta/\beta},
\qquad \theta>0.
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] Parameterization
> Di sini $\beta$ adalah **scale**, sehingga:
>
> $$
> E[\Theta]=\alpha\beta.
> $$

---

## 6.2 Marginalize Mixing Parameter

Compound PMF:

$$
P(X=x)
=
\int_0^\infty
P(X=x\mid\Theta=\theta)
g(\theta)\,d\theta.
$$

Substitusi:

$$
P(X=x)
=
\int_0^\infty
\frac{\theta^xe^{-\theta}}{x!}
\frac{\theta^{\alpha-1}e^{-\theta/\beta}}
{\beta^\alpha\Gamma(\alpha)}
\,d\theta.
$$

Gabungkan terms:

$$
=
\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)\beta^\alpha x!}
\int_0^\infty
\theta^{\alpha+x-1}
\exp\left[-\theta\frac{1+\beta}{\beta}\right]
\,d\theta.
$$

Dengan Gamma-integral/change of variable, source memperoleh:

$$
\boxed{
P(X=x)
=
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+x)\beta^x}
{\Gamma(\alpha)x!(1+\beta)^{\alpha+x}},
\qquad x=0,1,2,\ldots
}
$$

---

## 6.3 Negative Binomial Special Case

Ambil:

$$
\alpha=r,
$$

positive integer, dan:

$$
\beta=\frac{1-p}{p}.
$$

Karena:

$$
1+\beta
=
\frac1p,
$$

compound PMF menjadi:

$$
\boxed{
P(X=x)
=
\frac{(r+x-1)!}{(r-1)!x!}
p^r(1-p)^x,
\qquad x=0,1,2,\ldots
}
$$

atau:

$$
\boxed{
P(X=x)
=
\binom{r+x-1}{x}p^r(1-p)^x.
}
$$

Ini adalah Negative Binomial dalam **failure/excess-trials convention**:

$$
X=
\text{number of failures before the }r\text{th success}.
$$

> [!DANGER] Parameterization Trap
> Jangan tertukar dengan convention Hogg–Tanis/Miller yang dapat menggunakan **trial number of the $r$th success**, dengan support $r,r+1,\ldots$.
>
> Pada Poisson–Gamma mixture ini support adalah:
>
> $$
> x=0,1,2,\ldots
> $$
>
> sehingga yang muncul adalah **failure-count Negative Binomial**.

# Section 7 — Moments Poisson–Gamma via Conditioning

Walaupun §3.7 menurunkan PMF melalui integration, moments sering jauh lebih cepat dengan conditioning.

Diberikan:

$$
X\mid\Theta\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Theta).
$$

Karena Poisson memiliki:

$$
E[X\mid\Theta]=\Theta,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\Theta)=\Theta.
$$

Dan:

$$
E[\Theta]=\alpha\beta,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\Theta)=\alpha\beta^2.
$$

### Mean

$$
E[X]
=
E\{E[X\mid\Theta]\}
=
E[\Theta]
=
\boxed{\alpha\beta}.
$$

### Variance

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(X)
&=
E\{\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\Theta)\}
+
\operatorname{Var}\{E[X\mid\Theta]\}\\
&=
E[\Theta]+\operatorname{Var}(\Theta)\\
&=
\alpha\beta+\alpha\beta^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
\alpha\beta(1+\beta).
}
$$

Interpretation:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)>E[X]
$$

untuk $\beta>0$.

Random heterogeneity pada Poisson rate menambah variability di atas ordinary Poisson variability.

> [!TIP] Exam Shortcut
> Jika target hanya mean/variance compound model, **jangan integrate full marginal PMF/PDF kecuali diminta**. Conditioning biasanya jauh lebih cepat.

# Section 8 — Beta–Binomial Compounding

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberi contoh:

$$
X\mid P=p
\sim
\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p),
$$

sementara success probability sendiri random:

$$
P\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta).
$$

Conditional PMF:

$$
P(X=x\mid p)
=
\binom nxp^x(1-p)^{n-x}.
$$

Beta mixing density:

$$
g(p)
=
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}
{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)}
 p^{\alpha-1}(1-p)^{\beta-1},
\qquad0<p<1.
$$

Marginalize $p$:

$$
P(X=x)
=
\int_0^1
P(X=x\mid p)g(p)\,dp.
$$

Source obtains:

$$
\boxed{
P(X=x)
=
\frac{n!\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)
\Gamma(x+\alpha)
\Gamma(n-x+\beta)}
{x!(n-x)!\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)
\Gamma(n+\alpha+\beta)}
}
$$

for:

$$
x=0,1,\ldots,n.
$$

### Mean via Conditioning

Karena:

$$
E[X\mid P]=nP,
$$

maka:

$$
E[X]
=
nE[P].
$$

Untuk Beta$(\alpha,\beta)$:

$$
E[P]=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}.
$$

Sehingga:

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
\frac{n\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}.
}
$$

> [!TIP] Structure Recognition
> **Binomial with uncertain/random $p$** → think Beta mixing → Beta-Binomial type marginal distribution.

# Section 9 — Normal with Random Precision → Student-t Connection

Source juga menunjukkan continuous compounding.

Misalkan conditional distribution:

$$
X\mid\Theta=\theta
\sim
N\left(0,\frac1\theta\right),
$$

dengan random precision:

$$
\Theta\sim\Gamma(\alpha,\beta).
$$

Marginal distribution diperoleh dari:

$$
h(x)
=
\int_0^\infty
f(x\mid\theta)g(\theta)\,d\theta.
$$

Source memperoleh density berbentuk:

$$
h(x)
=
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+1/2)}
{\beta^\alpha\sqrt{2\pi}\Gamma(\alpha)}
\left(
\frac{2\beta}{2+\beta x^2}
\right)^{\alpha+1/2}.
$$

Untuk special parameterization:

$$
\alpha=\frac r2,
\qquad
\beta=\frac2r,
$$

marginal $X$ menjadi Student's $t$ dengan $r$ degrees of freedom.

### Main Lesson

Mixing variance/precision dapat menghasilkan distribution dengan **heavier tails** dibanding conditional Normal awal.

> [!NOTE] Exam Relevance
> Fokus utama bukan menghafal integral panjangnya, tetapi memahami structure:
>
> $$
> \text{Normal conditional on random precision}
> \rightarrow
> \text{integrate precision}
> \rightarrow
> \text{heavy-tailed marginal}.
> $$

---

# Section 10 — Gamma–Gamma Compounding & Pareto Connection

Hogg, McKean & Craig juga membangun heavy-tailed distribution melalui:

$$
X\mid\Theta=\theta
$$

berdistribusi Gamma dengan shape $k$ dan rate-like parameter $\theta$, sementara $\Theta$ memiliki Gamma mixing distribution.

Setelah integrate out $\theta$, source memperoleh generalized Pareto-type density:

$$
\boxed{
h(x)
=
\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+k)\beta^k x^{k-1}}
{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(k)(1+\beta x)^{\alpha+k}},
\qquad x>0.
}
$$

Khusus $k=1$ (conditional Exponential):

$$
\boxed{
h(x)
=
\alpha\beta(1+\beta x)^{-(\alpha+1)},
\qquad x>0,
}
$$

yang merupakan Pareto form pada source.

CDF:

$$
\boxed{
H(x)
=
1-(1+\beta x)^{-\alpha}.
}
$$

### Main Lesson

Randomizing a scale/rate parameter dapat menghasilkan unconditional distribution dengan **tail lebih tebal** daripada conditional distribution.

> [!NOTE] Depth Control
> Formula ini berguna sebagai illustration mechanism. Untuk exam, prioritaskan ability untuk **set up conditional × mixing density → marginalize**, bukan menghafal setiap special compound family jika soal tidak memberi structure-nya.

# Section 11 — General Calculation Framework

Untuk compound/mixture question:

```text
1. Identify what is random at level 1
2. Identify conditional distribution X | Θ
3. Identify mixing distribution of Θ
4. State supports for X and Θ
5. Form joint: f(x|θ)g(θ)
6. Marginalize θ
7. Simplify / recognize resulting family
8. If only moments needed, use conditioning instead
9. Verify PMF/PDF normalization or known-family form
```

### Generic Continuous Mixing

$$
\boxed{
h_X(x)
=
\int_{S_\Theta}
f_{X\mid\Theta}(x\mid\theta)
g_\Theta(\theta)
\,d\theta.
}
$$

### Generic Discrete Mixing

$$
\boxed{
p_X(x)
=
\sum_{\theta\in S_\Theta}
p_{X\mid\Theta}(x\mid\theta)
p_\Theta(\theta).
}
$$

### Mean

$$
\boxed{
E[X]
=
E\{E[X\mid\Theta]\}.
}
$$

### Variance

Jika supported/applicable:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
E\{\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\Theta)\}
+
\operatorname{Var}\{E[X\mid\Theta]\}.
}
$$

# Section 12 — Worked Cases

## Case A — Fundamental: Two-Component Mixture

Misalkan:

- dengan probability $0.7$, $X$ berasal dari distribution dengan mean $10$ dan variance $4$;
- dengan probability $0.3$, $X$ berasal dari distribution dengan mean $20$ dan variance $9$.

Tentukan mean dan variance mixture.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **Mean**
>
> $$
> \mu
> =
> 0.7(10)+0.3(20)
> =13.
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \boxed{E[X]=13}.
> $$
>
> **Variance**
>
> Within-component term:
>
> $$
> 0.7(4)+0.3(9)=5.5.
> $$
>
> Between-component term:
>
> $$
> 0.7(10-13)^2
> +0.3(20-13)^2
> $$
>
> $$
> =0.7(9)+0.3(49)=21.
> $$
>
> Hence:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \operatorname{Var}(X)=5.5+21=26.5.
> }
> $$
>
> **Interpretation**
>
> Variance total jauh lebih besar dari weighted average component variance karena component means berbeda cukup jauh.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap
> Distractor paling natural adalah $5.5$, yaitu hanya menghitung weighted average component variances dan lupa between-component variation.

---

## Case B — Exam-Typical: Direct Compound Probability

Misalkan:

$$
X\mid\Theta=\theta
\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\theta)
$$

dan:

$$
\Theta\sim\Gamma(2,1)
$$

(shape $=2$, scale $=1$).

Hitung $P(X=0)$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Conditional probability:
>
> $$
> P(X=0\mid\Theta=\theta)
> =e^{-\theta}.
> $$
>
> Mixing density:
>
> $$
> g(\theta)=\theta e^{-\theta},
> \qquad\theta>0.
> $$
>
> Marginalize:
>
> $$
> P(X=0)
> =
> \int_0^\infty
> e^{-\theta}\theta e^{-\theta}\,d\theta
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \int_0^\infty
> \theta e^{-2\theta}\,d\theta.
> $$
>
> Using Gamma integral:
>
> $$
> \int_0^\infty \theta e^{-2\theta}d\theta
> =\frac14.
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \boxed{P(X=0)=\frac14}.
> $$
>
> **Alternative recognition**
>
> Dari general Poisson–Gamma compound PMF:
>
> $$
> P(X=0)
> =
> \frac{\Gamma(2)}{\Gamma(2)}
> \frac{1}{(1+1)^2}
> =\frac14.
> $$

## Case C — Moments tanpa Derive Marginal Distribution

Misalkan:

$$
X\mid\Theta\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\Theta),
$$

serta:

$$
E[\Theta]=6,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\Theta)=10.
$$

Tentukan $E[X]$ dan $\operatorname{Var}(X)$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Conditional Poisson moments:
>
> $$
> E[X\mid\Theta]=\Theta,
> $$
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X\mid\Theta)=\Theta.
> $$
>
> Total expectation:
>
> $$
> E[X]
> =
> E[\Theta]
> =\boxed6.
> $$
>
> Total variance:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)
> =
> E[\Theta]
> +
> \operatorname{Var}(\Theta)
> $$
>
> $$
> =6+10
> =\boxed{16}.
> $$
>
> Tidak perlu menemukan marginal PMF $X$ terlebih dahulu.

> [!TIP] Exam Shortcut
> Jika target hanya first two moments, conditioning biasanya adalah fastest route.

$$

serta:

$$

hitung $E[X]$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>

>
> Maka:
>
> $$

> =20E[P].
> $$
>
> Untuk Beta$(2,3)$:
>
> $$
> E[P]
> =
> \frac2{2+3}
> =\frac25.
> $$
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \boxed{E[X]=8}.
> $$

# Section 13 — Verification & Sanity Checks

> [!CHECK] Sanity Checks
>
> **Mixture weights**
>
> $$
> p_i\ge0,
> \qquad
> \sum_i p_i=1.
> $$
>
> **Mixture PDF/PMF**
>
> Harus nonnegative dan normalize ke $1$.
>
> **Mixture mean**
>
> Jika component means finite, weighted mean harus berada di convex range component means:
>
> $$
> \min_i\mu_i
> \le
> \mu
> \le
> \max_i\mu_i.
> $$
>
> **Variance**
>
> Harus:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)
> \ge
> \sum_i p_i\sigma_i^2,
> $$
>
> karena between-component term nonnegative.
>
> **Compound marginalization**
>
> Pastikan integration/summation dilakukan atas **mixing variable**, bukan variable target.
>
> **Poisson–Gamma**
>
> Resulting support tetap:
>
> $$
> x=0,1,2,\ldots
> $$
>
> dan special Negative Binomial convention harus cocok dengan support tersebut.

# Section 14 — Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

| Trap | Kesalahan | Mengapa Salah | Pencegahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mixture vs linear combination | memperlakukan mixture sebagai $pX_1+(1-p)X_2$ | stochastic mechanism berbeda | tanyakan “pilih component” atau “jumlahkan variables”? |
| Weighted-variance trap | variance = weighted average variances saja | lupa between-component variation | gunakan source formula lengkap |
| Conditional vs marginal trap | berhenti pada $f(x\mid\theta)$ | target compound adalah unconditional $h(x)$ | integrate/sum over $\theta$ |
| Wrong integration variable | integrate $x$ instead of $\theta$ | menghilangkan target variable | marginalize mixing variable |
| Parameterization trap | Gamma rate/scale tertukar | exponent dan moments berubah | declare Gamma convention first |
| Negative-Binomial support trap | menggunakan support $r,r+1,\ldots$ pada Poisson–Gamma result | source result adalah failure-count form | inspect support $0,1,2,\ldots$ |
| Poisson variance trap | conclude compound variance = compound mean | random rate adds heterogeneity | total variance has extra term |
| Component normalization trap | component $f_i$ tidak valid / weights tidak sum 1 | mixture tidak normalize | check each component + weights |
| Overintegration trap | derive full marginal distribution untuk hanya mean | unnecessary | use total expectation |
| Total variance trap | langsung pakai formula tanpa conditional moments | assumptions/structure belum jelas | write $E[X|\Theta]$, $\operatorname{Var}(X|\Theta)$ first |
| “Compound = random sum” trap | otomatis membuat $S=\sum_{i=1}^N X_i$ | source §3.7 defines compounding as mixture | follow source terminology |
| Heavy-tail overclaim | semua mixtures dianggap heavy-tailed | tidak selalu | claim only when source/example supports it |

# Section 15 — Recognition Map

| Narasi | Structure | Setup Utama | Likely Route |
|---|---|---|---|
| “with probability $p$, from distribution A; otherwise B” | finite mixture | $pf_A+(1-p)f_B$ | weighted distribution |
| “parameter $\Theta$ is itself random” | continuous/discrete mixing | $f(x|\theta)g(\theta)$ | marginalize $\theta$ |
| Poisson with random rate Gamma | Poisson–Gamma compound | integrate Poisson × Gamma | Negative Binomial form |
| Binomial with random $p$ Beta | Beta–Binomial compound | integrate Binomial × Beta | Beta integral |
| Normal with random precision Gamma | Normal–Gamma mixing | integrate precision | Student-t special case |
| Exponential/Gamma with random rate Gamma | Gamma–Gamma mixing | integrate rate | Pareto/generalized Pareto form |
| “find unconditional mean” | iterated expectation | $E[E[X|\Theta]]$ | condition first |
| “find unconditional variance” | total variance | $E[Var(X|\Theta)]+Var(E[X|\Theta])$ | two-term decomposition |

# Section 17 — Executive Summary

> [!SUMMARY] Must Remember
>
> 1. Dalam Hogg, McKean & Craig §3.7, **mixture distributions disebut compounding**.
>
> 2. Finite mixture:
>
> $$
> \boxed{f(x)=\sum_i p_i f_i(x)}.
> $$
>
> 3. Mixture CDF:
>
> $$
> \boxed{F(x)=\sum_i p_iF_i(x)}.
> $$
>
> 4. Mean:
>
> $$
> \boxed{E[X]=\sum_i p_i\mu_i}.
> $$
>
> 5. Variance:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> Var(X)
> =
> \sum_i p_i\sigma_i^2
> +
> \sum_i p_i(\mu_i-\mu)^2.
> }
> $$
>
> 6. Mixture **bukan** linear combination.
>
> 7. Continuous parameter mixing:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
h(x)=\int f(x|\theta)g(\theta)d\theta}.
> $$
>
> 8. Compound distribution adalah marginal/unconditional distribution setelah mixing variable diintegrasikan/dijumlahkan keluar.
>
> 9. Poisson with Gamma-random rate menghasilkan Negative-Binomial-type marginal distribution.
>
> 10. Selalu periksa **Negative Binomial parameterization/support**.
>
> 11. Jika hanya moments diminta, gunakan:
>
> $$
> \boxed{E[X]=E[E[X|\Theta]]}
> $$
>
> dan bila applicable:
>
> $$
> \boxed{Var(X)=E[Var(X|\Theta)]+Var(E[X|\Theta])}.
> $$
>
> 12. Randomness pada parameter dapat menambah variability dan pada beberapa source examples menghasilkan heavier-tailed marginal distribution.
