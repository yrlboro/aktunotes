## Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Untuk satu random variable $X$, variasi diringkas oleh satu angka:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X).
$$

Untuk dua random variables $X$ dan $Y$, kita memerlukan:

- $\operatorname{Var}(X)$;
- $\operatorname{Var}(Y)$;
- $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$.

Untuk $n$ random variables, seluruh informasi second-order tersebut dapat disusun menjadi **satu matrix**.

Jika

$$
\mathbf X=
\begin{pmatrix}
X_1\\
X_2\\
\vdots\\
X_n
\end{pmatrix},
$$

maka variance-covariance matrix merangkum:

```text
each variable's own variance
          +
every pairwise covariance
```

Dalam bentuk matrix:

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\operatorname{Var}(X_1) & \operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2) & \cdots \\
\operatorname{Cov}(X_2,X_1) & \operatorname{Var}(X_2) & \cdots \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Mental rule:

> **Diagonal = variance. Off-diagonal = covariance.**

## Section 2 — Random Vector dan Mean Vector

Hogg, McKean & Craig menulis random vector berdimensi $n$ sebagai

$$
\mathbf X
=
(X_1,\ldots,X_n)^\top.
$$

Mean vector:

$$
\boxed{
\boldsymbol\mu
=
E[\mathbf X]
=
\begin{pmatrix}
E[X_1]\\
\vdots\\
E[X_n]
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$

Expectation pada matrix/vector diterapkan **elementwise**.

Jika $A$ dan $B$ adalah constant matrices dengan dimensi compatible:

$$
E[A\mathbf X]
=
AE[\mathbf X].
$$

Lebih umum, linearity of matrix expectation mengikuti linearity expectation scalar.

> [!TIP] Notation Check
> $\mathbf X$ adalah random vector, sedangkan $\boldsymbol\mu$ adalah constant vector.

## Section 3 — Definisi Variance-Covariance Matrix

Untuk

$$
\mathbf X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)^\top
$$

dengan finite variances, Hogg, McKean & Craig mendefinisikan:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf X)
=
E\left[
(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)
(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)^\top
\right].
}
$$

Dalam note ini kita gunakan notation silabus:

$$
\boxed{
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf X).
}
$$

Jika

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma=[\sigma_{ij}],
$$

maka:

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_{ii}
=
\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
}
$$

dan untuk $i\neq j$:

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_{ij}
=
\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
}
$$

Sehingga:

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\operatorname{Var}(X_1) &
\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2) &
\cdots &
\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_n)
\\
\operatorname{Cov}(X_2,X_1) &
\operatorname{Var}(X_2) &
\cdots &
\operatorname{Cov}(X_2,X_n)
\\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\
\operatorname{Cov}(X_n,X_1) &
\operatorname{Cov}(X_n,X_2) &
\cdots &
\operatorname{Var}(X_n)
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

---

## Section 4 — Symmetry

Karena:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)
=
\operatorname{Cov}(X_j,X_i),
$$

maka:

$$
\boxed{
\boldsymbol\Sigma^\top
=
\boldsymbol\Sigma.
}
$$

Jadi variance-covariance matrix selalu **symmetric**.

Untuk tiga variables:

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\sigma_1^2 & \sigma_{12} & \sigma_{13}\\
\sigma_{12} & \sigma_2^2 & \sigma_{23}\\
\sigma_{13} & \sigma_{23} & \sigma_3^2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Hanya elemen diagonal dan salah satu triangular half yang perlu dihitung secara unik.

> [!CHECK] Matrix Sanity Check
> Jika input covariance matrix tidak symmetric, cek apakah:
> 1. salah copy;
> 2. salah menghitung salah satu covariance;
> 3. matrix tersebut sebenarnya bukan covariance matrix.

## Section 5 — Equivalent Form

Hogg, McKean & Craig memberikan identity:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf X)
=
E[\mathbf X\mathbf X^\top]
-
\boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top.
}
$$

Ini adalah generalisasi matrix dari:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=
E[X^2]-E[X]^2.
$$

### Kenapa?

Mulai dari:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf X)
=
E[
(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)
(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)^\top
].
$$

Expand:

$$
(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)
(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)^\top
=
\mathbf X\mathbf X^\top
-\mathbf X\boldsymbol\mu^\top
-\boldsymbol\mu\mathbf X^\top
+\boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top.
$$

Ambil expectation:

$$
E[\mathbf X\mathbf X^\top]
-\boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top
-\boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top
+\boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top,
$$

sehingga:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf X)
=
E[\mathbf X\mathbf X^\top]
-
\boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top.
}
$$

## Section 6 — Contoh Dasar dari Textbook

Hogg, McKean & Craig melanjutkan contoh dengan joint PDF

$$
f(x,y)
=
e^{-y},
\qquad
0<x<y<\infty.
$$

Dari perhitungan sebelumnya diperoleh:

$$
\mu_X=1,
\qquad
\mu_Y=2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=2,
$$

dan

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=1.
$$

Untuk:

$$
\mathbf Z=
\begin{pmatrix}
X\\
Y
\end{pmatrix},
$$

mean vector:

$$
E[\mathbf Z]
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Variance-covariance matrix:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf Z)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
1&2
\end{pmatrix}.
}
$$

Interpretasi:

- $(1,1)$ = variance $X$;
- $(2,2)$ = variance $Y$;
- $(1,2)$ dan $(2,1)$ = covariance $X,Y$.

## Section 7 — Covariance Matrix dari Linear Transformation

Ini salah satu formula paling penting untuk CF2.

Jika $A$ adalah constant matrix dan:

$$
\mathbf Y=A\mathbf X,
$$

maka Hogg, McKean & Craig memberikan:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf Y)
=
\operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X)
=
A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top.
}
$$

Jika ada constant shift:

$$
\mathbf Y=A\mathbf X+\mathbf b,
$$

constant tidak menambah variance/covariance, sehingga:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X+\mathbf b)
=
A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top.
}
$$

Mental rule:

```text
linear transformation:
left multiply by A
right multiply by A^T
```

> [!WARNING] Matrix Order Trap
> Yang benar:
>
> $$
> A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top
> $$
>
> bukan
>
> $$
> A^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma A
> $$
>
> secara umum.
>
> Selalu lakukan dimension check.

## Section 8 — Variance Linear Combination sebagai Special Case

Misalkan:

$$
Y
=
a_1X_1+\cdots+a_nX_n.
$$

Tulis:

$$
\mathbf a
=
\begin{pmatrix}
a_1\\
\vdots\\
a_n
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Karena:

$$
Y=\mathbf a^\top\mathbf X,
$$

maka:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}(Y)
=
\mathbf a^\top
\boldsymbol\Sigma
\mathbf a.
}
$$

Ini adalah special case dari:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X)
=
A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top
$$

dengan $A=\mathbf a^\top$.

### Expanded Form

Miller memberikan equivalent scalar formula:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Var}\left(
\sum_{i=1}^n a_iX_i
\right)
=
\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^2\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
+
2\sum_{i<j}a_ia_j\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
}
$$

Untuk tiga variables:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(aX+bY+cZ)
&=
a^2\operatorname{Var}(X)
+b^2\operatorname{Var}(Y)
+c^2\operatorname{Var}(Z)
\\
&\quad
+2ab\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
+2ac\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)
+2bc\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Z).
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!DANGER] Cross-Term Trap
> Jangan menghapus covariance terms kecuali covariance tersebut memang $0$ atau independence/uncorrelatedness sudah justified.

## Section 9 — Independence dan Covariance Matrix

Jika random variables pairwise independent dan second moments finite:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0
\qquad(i\neq j).
$$

Maka $\boldsymbol\Sigma$ menjadi diagonal:

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\sigma_1^2&0&\cdots&0\\
0&\sigma_2^2&\cdots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&0&\cdots&\sigma_n^2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Dalam kondisi ini:

$$
\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_i a_iX_i\right)
=
\sum_i a_i^2\sigma_i^2.
$$

Namun distinction dari [[3.5 Independensi dan Korelasi]] tetap berlaku:

> **Diagonal covariance matrix tidak secara umum membuktikan mutual independence.**

Ia hanya menunjukkan pairwise covariance nol.

## Section 10 — Positive Semi-Definite Property

Hogg, McKean & Craig menunjukkan semua variance-covariance matrices adalah **positive semi-definite**.

Untuk setiap constant vector $\mathbf a$:

$$
\boxed{
\mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a
\ge0.
}
$$

Mengapa?

Karena:

$$
Y=\mathbf a^\top\mathbf X
$$

adalah random variable dan:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y)\ge0.
$$

Tetapi:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y)
=
\mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a.
$$

Maka:

$$
\mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a\ge0.
$$

### Exam Interpretation

Jika sebuah matrix diklaim sebagai covariance matrix tetapi menghasilkan:

$$
\mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a<0
$$

untuk suatu $\mathbf a$, matrix tersebut tidak dapat menjadi valid variance-covariance matrix.

> [!IMPORTANT] What PSD Means Here
> PSD adalah matrix version dari prinsip fundamental:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(\text{any linear combination})\ge0.
> $$

## Section 11 — Covariance antara Dua Linear Combinations

Miller memberikan theorem untuk:

$$
Y_1=\sum_{i=1}^n a_iX_i,
$$

dan

$$
Y_2=\sum_{i=1}^n b_iX_i.
$$

Dengan vectors $\mathbf a,\mathbf b$:

$$
\boxed{
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2)
=
\mathbf a^\top
\boldsymbol\Sigma
\mathbf b.
}
$$

Expanded scalar form:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2)
=
\sum_i a_ib_i\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
+
\sum_{i<j}
(a_ib_j+a_jb_i)
\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
$$

Jika $X_i$ independent:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2)
=
\sum_i a_ib_i\operatorname{Var}(X_i).
$$

## Section 12 — Worked Cases

### Case A — Fundamental: Susun Covariance Matrix

Diketahui:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=4,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=9,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=-1,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Z)=3.
$$

Tentukan $\boldsymbol\Sigma$ untuk

$$
\mathbf X=(X,Y,Z)^\top.
$$

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> **1. Diagonal**
>
> $$
> (4,\ 9,\ 1).
> $$
>
> **2. Off-diagonal**
>
> Gunakan covariance sesuai urutan variables.
>
> **3. Symmetry**
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> =
> \operatorname{Cov}(Y,X).
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \boldsymbol\Sigma
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 4&2&-1\\
> 2&9&3\\
> -1&3&1
> \end{pmatrix}.
> }
> $$
>
> **Sanity check:** symmetric.

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap
> Jangan menaruh standard deviation pada diagonal. Diagonal covariance matrix adalah **variance**, bukan SD.

---

### Case B — Exam-Typical: Variance dari Linear Combination

Gunakan data Miller:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=5,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Z)=2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=-2,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=-1,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Z)=1.
$$

Tentukan variance:

$$
W=3X-Y+2Z.
$$

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Coefficient vector:
>
> $$
> \mathbf a
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 3\\
> -1\\
> 2
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Covariance matrix:
>
> $$
> \boldsymbol\Sigma
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1&-2&-1\\
> -2&5&1\\
> -1&1&2
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Matrix formula:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(W)
> =
> \mathbf a^\top
> \boldsymbol\Sigma
> \mathbf a.
> $$
>
> Equivalent scalar expansion:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{Var}(W)
> &=
> 9(1)+1(5)+4(2)
> \\
> &\quad
> +2(3)(-1)(-2)
> +2(3)(2)(-1)
> +2(-1)(2)(1).
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> 9+5+8+12-12-4
> =
> \boxed{18}.
> $$
>
> Ini sama dengan hasil textbook Miller.

> [!TIP] Fast Method
> Jika hanya tiga random variables, scalar form sering lebih cepat secara mental. Jika banyak variables atau matrix sudah diberikan, matrix form lebih aman.

### Case C — Linear Transformation Matrix

Misalkan:

$$
\mathbf X
=
\begin{pmatrix}
X_1\\
X_2
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\begin{pmatrix}
4&1\\
1&9
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Definisikan:

$$
\mathbf Y
=
A\mathbf X,
\qquad
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1\\
2&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Tentukan covariance matrix $\mathbf Y$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Gunakan:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf Y)
> =
> A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top.
> $$
>
> Pertama:
>
> $$
> A\boldsymbol\Sigma
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1&1\\
> 2&-1
> \end{pmatrix}
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 4&1\\
> 1&9
> \end{pmatrix}
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 5&10\\
> 7&-7
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Kemudian:
>
> $$
> A^\top
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1&2\\
> 1&-1
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf Y)
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 5&10\\
> 7&-7
> \end{pmatrix}
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1&2\\
> 1&-1
> \end{pmatrix}
> =
> \boxed{
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 15&0\\
> 0&21
> \end{pmatrix}
> }.
> $$
>
> **Verification**
>
> - matrix symmetric;
> - diagonal nonnegative;
> - transformed components happen to be uncorrelated karena off-diagonal $0$.

> [!WARNING] Interpretation Trap
> Off-diagonal $0$ menunjukkan transformed variables uncorrelated, **bukan otomatis independent**.

### Case D — Covariance Dua Linear Combinations

Dari Miller:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=8,
\quad
\operatorname{Var}(Y)=12,
\quad
\operatorname{Var}(Z)=18,
$$

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=1,
\quad
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=-3,
\quad
\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Z)=2.
$$

Definisikan:

$$
U=X+4Y+2Z,
$$

$$
V=3X-Y-Z.
$$

Tentukan $\operatorname{Cov}(U,V)$.

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> Coefficient vectors:
>
> $$
> \mathbf a
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1\\4\\2
> \end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> \mathbf b
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 3\\-1\\-1
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Dengan:
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(U,V)
> =
> \mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf b,
> $$
>
> atau scalar expansion Miller:
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{Cov}(U,V)
> &=
> 3\operatorname{Var}(X)
> -4\operatorname{Var}(Y)
> -2\operatorname{Var}(Z)
> \\
> &\quad
> +11\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)
> +5\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)
> -6\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Z).
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> Substitusi:
>
> $$
> 3(8)-4(12)-2(18)+11(1)+5(-3)-6(2)
> $$
>
> $$
> =
> \boxed{-76}.
> $$
>
> Negative covariance valid; covariance tidak dibatasi pada $[0,1]$.

### Case E — Quick PSD Sanity Check

Misalkan candidate covariance matrix:

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&2\\
2&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Apakah matrix ini dapat menjadi valid covariance matrix?

Pilih:

$$
\mathbf a=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
-1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Hitung:

$$
\mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a.
$$

> [!SUCCESS] Pembahasan
>
> $$
> \boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1&2\\
> 2&1
> \end{pmatrix}
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1\\
> -1
> \end{pmatrix}
> =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> -1\\
> 1
> \end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> Lalu:
>
> $$
> \mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a
> =
> (1,-1)
> \begin{pmatrix}
> -1\\
> 1
> \end{pmatrix}
> =
> -2.
> $$
>
> Tetapi variance tidak dapat negatif.
>
> Jadi:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \boldsymbol\Sigma
> \text{ tidak valid sebagai covariance matrix.}
> }
> $$

## Section 13 — Dimension Checks

Matrix algebra errors sering lebih cepat dideteksi lewat dimensions.

Jika:

$$
\mathbf X:n\times1,
$$

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma:n\times n,
$$

$$
A:m\times n,
$$

maka:

$$
A\mathbf X:m\times1.
$$

Covariance matrix hasil harus:

$$
m\times m.
$$

Check:

$$
A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top
$$

memiliki dimensi:

$$
(m\times n)
(n\times n)
(n\times m)
=
m\times m.
$$

Untuk scalar linear combination:

$$
\mathbf a^\top\boldsymbol\Sigma\mathbf a:
$$

$$
(1\times n)
(n\times n)
(n\times1)
=
1\times1.
$$

> [!TIP] Dimension Check = Exam Error Detector
> Jika multiplication dimensions tidak cocok, formula/order matrix salah sebelum Anda melakukan arithmetic.

## Section 14 — Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

| Trap | Kesalahan | Mengapa Salah | Pencegahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Variance-vs-SD trap | diagonal diisi $\sigma_i$ | covariance matrix memakai $\sigma_i^2$ | diagonal = variance |
| Symmetry trap | $\sigma_{ij}\neq\sigma_{ji}$ | covariance symmetric | mirror off-diagonal |
| Correlation-matrix trap | memasukkan $\rho_{ij}$ langsung ke covariance matrix | covariance $\neq$ correlation | $\sigma_{ij}=\rho_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j$ |
| Cross-term trap | $\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)=\operatorname{Var}X+\operatorname{Var}Y$ selalu | missing $2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$ | retain covariance unless justified zero |
| Independence trap | diagonal $\Sigma$ ⇒ variables independent | zero covariance not enough generally | state only uncorrelated unless stronger assumption |
| Matrix-order trap | $A^\top\Sigma A$ for vector transform $A\mathbf X$ | dimensions/meaning wrong | use $A\Sigma A^\top$ |
| Constant-shift trap | add $\mathbf b$ to covariance | constants have zero variance/covariance | shift changes mean, not covariance |
| Sign trap | square coefficients but forget sign in cross terms | cross products retain signs | compute $2a_ia_j\sigma_{ij}$ carefully |
| PSD trap | accept negative variance of linear combination | impossible | check $\mathbf a^\top\Sigma\mathbf a\ge0$ |
| Covariance bound trap | require covariance in $[-1,1]$ | that bound belongs to correlation | covariance has scale |
| Double-count trap | include off-diagonal cross term once instead of factor 2 | symmetric pair contributes twice | use $2\sum_{i<j}$ |
| Matrix transpose trap | forget transpose in covariance transform | output may not be square/symmetric | dimension check |

## Section 15 — Formula Map

| Target | Formula | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Mean vector | $\boldsymbol\mu=E[\mathbf X]$ | componentwise means |
| Covariance matrix | $\boldsymbol\Sigma=E[(\mathbf X-\mu)(\mathbf X-\mu)^\top]$ | all second-order dependence info |
| Entry | $\Sigma_{ii}=\operatorname{Var}(X_i)$ | diagonal |
| Entry | $\Sigma_{ij}=\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)$ | off-diagonal |
| Equivalent covariance | $\Sigma=E[\mathbf X\mathbf X^\top]-\mu\mu^\top$ | matrix analogue of $E[X^2]-E[X]^2$ |
| Symmetry | $\Sigma^\top=\Sigma$ | covariance symmetry |
| Linear transform | $\operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X)=A\Sigma A^\top$ | core matrix rule |
| Shifted transform | $\operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X+\mathbf b)=A\Sigma A^\top$ | constants vanish |
| Linear-combination variance | $\operatorname{Var}(\mathbf a^\top\mathbf X)=\mathbf a^\top\Sigma\mathbf a$ | scalar quadratic form |
| Expanded variance | $\sum a_i^2\sigma_i^2+2\sum_{i<j}a_ia_j\sigma_{ij}$ | exam arithmetic form |
| Covariance two combinations | $\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf a^\top X,\mathbf b^\top X)=\mathbf a^\top\Sigma\mathbf b$ | cross quadratic/bilinear form |
| PSD | $\mathbf a^\top\Sigma\mathbf a\ge0$ | all linear-combination variances nonnegative |

## Section 16 — Correlation Matrix vs Covariance Matrix

Ini distinction yang penting walaupun silabus menargetkan covariance matrix.

Variance-covariance matrix:

$$
\boldsymbol\Sigma_{ij}
=
\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
$$

Correlation:

$$
\rho_{ij}
=
\frac{\sigma_{ij}}{\sigma_i\sigma_j}.
$$

Jika correlation matrix $R$ diberikan bersama standard deviations, covariance dapat direconstruct:

$$
\boxed{
\sigma_{ij}
=
\rho_{ij}\sigma_i\sigma_j.
}
$$

Pada diagonal:

$$
\rho_{ii}=1,
$$

sedangkan diagonal covariance matrix:

$$
\Sigma_{ii}=\sigma_i^2.
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] Quick Identification
> - diagonal semua $1$ → kemungkinan **correlation matrix**;
> - diagonal berisi variances → **covariance matrix**.

## Section 17 — Connections & Mental Model

### 17.1 Connection ke 3.5

[[3.5 Independensi dan Korelasi]] memberi building blocks:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X_i),
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
$$

Topik 3.6 hanya mengorganisasikan quantities tersebut menjadi matrix dan mengeksploitasi matrix algebra.

---

### 17.2 Connection ke Transformations

Jika random vector ditransformasi linear:

$$
\mathbf Y=A\mathbf X+\mathbf b,
$$

maka:

$$
E[\mathbf Y]
=
A\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf b,
$$

dan:

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\mathbf Y)
=
A\boldsymbol\Sigma A^\top.
$$

Ini adalah linear special case yang sangat penting sebelum [[3.8 Transformasi Variabel Acak Gabungan]].

---

### 17.3 Connection ke Sampling

Dalam random sample iid:

$$
X_1,\ldots,X_n,
$$

independence sering menghasilkan zero off-diagonal covariance antara distinct sample observations, sehingga covariance structure menjadi sederhana.

Tetapi exact sampling distribution tetap membutuhkan assumptions tambahan yang dibahas di Topik 4.

---

### 17.4 Connection ke Multivariate Normal

Hogg, McKean & Craig menggunakan $\boldsymbol\Sigma$ sebagai covariance matrix dalam parameterisasi multivariate normal:

$$
N_n(\boldsymbol\mu,\boldsymbol\Sigma).
$$

Connection ini penting untuk memahami mengapa $\boldsymbol\Sigma$ harus symmetric dan positive semi-definite.

## Section 18 — Executive Summary

> [!SUMMARY] Must Remember
>
> 1. Untuk random vector:
>
> $$
> \mathbf X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)^\top.
> $$
>
> 2. Mean vector:
>
> $$
> \boldsymbol\mu=E[\mathbf X].
> $$
>
> 3. Variance-covariance matrix:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \boldsymbol\Sigma
> =
> E[(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)^\top].
> }
> $$
>
> 4. Diagonal:
>
> $$
> \Sigma_{ii}=\operatorname{Var}(X_i).
> $$
>
> 5. Off-diagonal:
>
> $$
> \Sigma_{ij}=\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
> $$
>
> 6. Covariance matrix symmetric:
>
> $$
> \Sigma^\top=\Sigma.
> $$
>
> 7. Equivalent:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \Sigma
> =
> E[\mathbf X\mathbf X^\top]
> -
> \boldsymbol\mu\boldsymbol\mu^\top.
> }
> $$
>
> 8. Linear transformation:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X)
> =
> A\Sigma A^\top.
> }
> $$
>
> 9. Linear combination:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \operatorname{Var}(\mathbf a^\top\mathbf X)
> =
> \mathbf a^\top\Sigma\mathbf a.
> }
> $$
>
> 10. Expanded:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \operatorname{Var}
> \left(
> \sum_i a_iX_i
> \right)
> =
> \sum_i a_i^2\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
> +
> 2\sum_{i<j}a_ia_j\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
> }
> $$
>
> 11. Semua covariance matrices positive semi-definite:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> \mathbf a^\top\Sigma\mathbf a\ge0.
> }
> $$
>
> 12. Diagonal covariance matrix berarti variables uncorrelated pairwise; jangan otomatis menyimpulkan independence.
