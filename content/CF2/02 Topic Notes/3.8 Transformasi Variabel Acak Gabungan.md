# Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Pada transformasi univariat:

$$
Y=g(X),
$$

kita memetakan satu axis ke axis baru.

Pada transformasi multivariat:

$$
(Y_1,Y_2)
=
T(X_1,X_2),
$$

kita memetakan **seluruh region** di bidang $(x_1,x_2)$ menjadi region baru di bidang $(y_1,y_2)$.

Ada dua hal yang berubah:

1. **coordinates** — titik $(x_1,x_2)$ menjadi $(y_1,y_2)$;
2. **area element** — area kecil $dx_1dx_2$ tidak sama ukurannya dengan $dy_1dy_2$.

Jacobian memperbaiki perubahan skala area tersebut.

Mental picture:

```text
original support S_X
       ↓ transformation T
transformed support S_Y
       ↓ inverse mapping
x1 = w1(y1,y2)
x2 = w2(y1,y2)
       ↓
Jacobian |∂(x1,x2)/∂(y1,y2)|
       ↓
substitute into original density
       ↓
joint density of transformed variables
       ↓
marginalize if only one target variable needed
```

> [!DANGER] Main Exam Difficulty
> Source Hogg–Tanis menekankan bahwa dalam banyak soal, **mapping support** justru lebih sulit daripada menghitung inverse atau determinant Jacobian.

# Section 2 — General One-to-One Transformation Theorem

Misalkan $(X_1,X_2)$ memiliki joint PDF

$$
f_{X_1,X_2}(x_1,x_2)
$$

pada support $S$.

Definisikan:

$$
Y_1=u_1(X_1,X_2),
$$

$$
Y_2=u_2(X_1,X_2).
$$

Asumsikan transformation one-to-one dan mempunyai inverse:

$$
x_1=w_1(y_1,y_2),
$$

$$
x_2=w_2(y_1,y_2).
$$

Jacobian inverse transformation:

$$
\boxed{
J
=
\frac{\partial(x_1,x_2)}
{\partial(y_1,y_2)}
=
\begin{vmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial x_1}{\partial y_1}
&
\dfrac{\partial x_1}{\partial y_2}
\\[6pt]
\dfrac{\partial x_2}{\partial y_1}
&
\dfrac{\partial x_2}{\partial y_2}
\end{vmatrix}.
}
$$

Then:

$$
\boxed{
f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)
=
f_{X_1,X_2}
\left(
w_1(y_1,y_2),
w_2(y_1,y_2)
\right)
|J|
}
$$

untuk $(y_1,y_2)$ dalam transformed support $T=T(S)$, dan $0$ elsewhere.

> [!IMPORTANT] Absolute Jacobian
> Gunakan
>
> $$
> |J|,
> $$
>
> bukan signed determinant $J$.
>
> Density tidak boleh menjadi negatif hanya karena orientation transformation berbalik.

---

# Section 3 — Direct Jacobian vs Reciprocal Jacobian

Source theorem di atas menggunakan **inverse Jacobian**:

$$
\frac{\partial(x_1,x_2)}
{\partial(y_1,y_2)}.
$$

Jika lebih mudah menghitung forward Jacobian:

$$
K
=
\frac{\partial(y_1,y_2)}
{\partial(x_1,x_2)},
$$

maka pada regular one-to-one transformation:

$$
\boxed{
|J|
=
\frac1{|K|}.
}
$$

Tetapi jangan mencampur dua convention dalam satu calculation.

> [!WARNING] Reciprocal-Jacobian Trap
> Salah satu distractor klasik adalah menggunakan
>
> $$
> \left|
> \frac{\partial(y_1,y_2)}
> {\partial(x_1,x_2)}
> \right|
> $$
>
> langsung pada transformed density padahal theorem/source sedang memakai inverse Jacobian.

Mental rule:

> **Write the Jacobian notation explicitly before taking the determinant.**

---

# Section 4 — Support Mapping

Jika original support diberikan oleh inequalities, substitution inverse harus memenuhi semuanya.

Contoh:

$$
0<x_1<x_2<1.
$$

Transformation:

$$
Y_1=\frac{X_1}{X_2},
\qquad
Y_2=X_2.
$$

Inverse:

$$
x_1=y_1y_2,
\qquad
x_2=y_2.
$$

Substitute ke original inequalities:

$$
0<y_1y_2<y_2<1.
$$

Karena $y_2>0$:

$$
0<y_1<1,
\qquad
0<y_2<1.
$$

Jadi triangular region original berubah menjadi rectangular region.

> [!TIP] Best Support Method
> Jangan menebak transformed support dari range masing-masing variable secara terpisah.
>
> Gunakan:
>
> 1. inverse transformation;
> 2. substitute ke **seluruh** original support inequalities;
> 3. simplify simultaneously.

---

# Section 5 — Master Workflow

Untuk continuous two-variable transformation:

```text
STEP 1  Write original joint PDF and support S_X
STEP 2  Define Y1=u1(X1,X2), Y2=u2(X1,X2)
STEP 3  Check / assume one-to-one in relevant region
STEP 4  Solve inverse x1=w1(y1,y2), x2=w2(y1,y2)
STEP 5  Map support S_X → S_Y
STEP 6  Compute inverse Jacobian determinant
STEP 7  Take absolute value
STEP 8  Substitute inverse into original PDF
STEP 9  Multiply by |J|
STEP 10 Verify support + normalization if useful
STEP 11 Marginalize if only Y1 or Y2 distribution is required
```

Formula:

$$
f_{Y_1}(y_1)
=
\int
f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)\,dy_2
$$

dengan bounds dari transformed support.

# Section 6 — Worked Case A: Sum and Difference

Hogg, McKean & Craig menggunakan independent:

$$
X_1,X_2\sim U(0,1),
$$

sehingga joint PDF:

$$
f_{X_1,X_2}(x_1,x_2)=1,
\qquad
0<x_1<1,\quad0<x_2<1.
$$

Definisikan:

$$
Y_1=X_1+X_2,
$$

$$
Y_2=X_1-X_2.
$$

## 6.1 Inverse

Dari:

$$
y_1=x_1+x_2,
$$

$$
y_2=x_1-x_2,
$$

diperoleh:

$$
\boxed{
x_1=\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}
}
$$

dan:

$$
\boxed{
x_2=\frac{y_1-y_2}{2}.
}
$$

## 6.2 Jacobian

$$
J
=
\begin{vmatrix}
1/2&1/2\\
1/2&-1/2
\end{vmatrix}
=
-\frac12.
$$

Hence:

$$
|J|=\frac12.
$$

## 6.3 Transformed Joint PDF

Karena original density $=1$:

$$
\boxed{
f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)
=
\frac12
}
$$

pada transformed region.

## 6.4 Support

Original inequalities:

$$
0<\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}<1,
$$

$$
0<\frac{y_1-y_2}{2}<1.
$$

Equivalent boundaries menghasilkan diamond-shaped region:

$$
-y_1<y_2,
$$

$$
y_2<y_1,
$$

$$
y_2<2-y_1,
$$

$$
y_1-2<y_2.
$$

Untuk marginal $Y_1=X_1+X_2$:

- jika $0<y_1\le1$:
  $$
  -y_1<y_2<y_1;
  $$
- jika $1<y_1<2$:
  $$
  y_1-2<y_2<2-y_1.
  $$

Maka:

$$
f_{Y_1}(y_1)
=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle
\int_{-y_1}^{y_1}\frac12\,dy_2
=
y_1,
&
0<y_1\le1,
\\[10pt]
\displaystyle
\int_{y_1-2}^{2-y_1}\frac12\,dy_2
=
2-y_1,
&
1<y_1<2,
\\[8pt]
0,&\text{elsewhere}.
\end{cases}
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
f_{X_1+X_2}(y)
=
\begin{cases}
y,&0<y<1,\\
2-y,&1\le y<2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
}
$$

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case A
> Jacobian-nya sederhana; yang memakan waktu adalah **piecewise support**.
>
> Jangan mengintegrasikan $y_2$ pada satu range tetap untuk semua $y_1$.

# Section 7 — Worked Case B: Sum + Auxiliary Variable

Miller menyelesaikan problem yang sama dengan transformation lebih sederhana:

$$
Y=X_1+X_2,
$$

$$
Z=X_2.
$$

Original:

$$
0<x_1<1,
\qquad0<x_2<1.
$$

## 7.1 Inverse

$$
x_1=y-z,
$$

$$
x_2=z.
$$

## 7.2 Jacobian

$$
J
=
\begin{vmatrix}
1&-1\\
0&1
\end{vmatrix}
=1.
$$

Jadi transformed density:

$$
g(y,z)=1
$$

pada support mapped.

## 7.3 Support

Original inequalities menjadi:

$$
0<y-z<1,
$$

$$
0<z<1.
$$

Equivalent:

$$
z<y<z+1,
\qquad
0<z<1.
$$

Untuk marginal $Y$:

- $0<y<1$:
  $$
  0<z<y;
  $$
- $1<y<2$:
  $$
  y-1<z<1.
  $$

Maka:

$$
f_Y(y)
=
\begin{cases}
y,&0<y<1,\\
2-y,&1<y<2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

> [!TIP] Auxiliary-Variable Strategy
> Jika target hanya
>
> $$
> Y=u(X_1,X_2),
> $$
>
> pilih second transformed variable yang membuat inverse **mudah**.
>
> Common choices:
>
> $$
> Z=X_1
> $$
>
> atau
>
> $$
> Z=X_2.
> $$

---

# Section 8 — CDF Technique untuk Sum

Hogg, McKean & Craig terlebih dahulu menunjukkan CDF method untuk:

$$
Z=X_1+X_2
$$

ketika $X_1,X_2$ independent $U(0,1)$.

$$
F_Z(z)
=
P(X_1+X_2\le z).
$$

Geometry region menghasilkan:

$$
F_Z(z)
=
\begin{cases}
0,&z<0,\\[4pt]
\dfrac{z^2}{2},&0\le z<1,\\[8pt]
1-\dfrac{(2-z)^2}{2},&1\le z<2,\\[8pt]
1,&z\ge2.
\end{cases}
$$

Differentiate:

$$
f_Z(z)
=
\begin{cases}
z,&0<z<1,\\
2-z,&1<z<2,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

### Method Selection

**CDF method** sering lebih baik jika:

- hanya satu transformed variable diminta;
- event $g(X_1,X_2)\le y$ membentuk region sederhana.

**Jacobian method** sering lebih baik jika:

- dua transformed variables diminta;
- transformation mempunyai clean inverse;
- marginal distribution dapat diperoleh setelah joint transform;
- ingin menguji independence transformed variables.

# Section 9 — Worked Case C: Ratio and Scale

Hogg, Tanis & Zimmerman memberi:

$$
f_{X_1,X_2}(x_1,x_2)
=
2,
\qquad
0<x_1<x_2<1.
$$

Transformation:

$$
Y_1=\frac{X_1}{X_2},
$$

$$
Y_2=X_2.
$$

## 9.1 Inverse

$$
x_1=y_1y_2,
$$

$$
x_2=y_2.
$$

## 9.2 Jacobian

$$
J
=
\begin{vmatrix}
y_2&y_1\\
0&1
\end{vmatrix}
=
y_2.
$$

Karena mapped support memberi $y_2>0$:

$$
|J|=y_2.
$$

## 9.3 Support

Original:

$$
0<x_1<x_2<1.
$$

Substitute:

$$
0<y_1y_2<y_2<1.
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
0<y_1<1,
\qquad
0<y_2<1.
}
$$

## 9.4 Transformed Joint PDF

$$
f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)
=
2y_2,
\qquad
0<y_1<1,\;0<y_2<1.
$$

Karena expression factorizes:

$$
2y_2=(1)(2y_2),
$$

dan support rectangular, transformed variables dapat dikenali sebagai independent.

Marginal:

$$
f_{Y_1}(y_1)
=
\int_0^1 2y_2\,dy_2
=
1,
$$

sehingga:

$$
\boxed{
Y_1\sim U(0,1).
}
$$

> [!IMPORTANT] Transformation Insight
> Transformasi dapat mengubah dependent original variables menjadi transformed variables yang independent.

# Section 10 — Worked Case D: Ratio and Sum of Independent Gammas

Hogg, Tanis & Zimmerman memberi:

$$
X_1\sim\Gamma(\alpha,\theta),
$$

$$
X_2\sim\Gamma(\beta,\theta),
$$

independent, dengan common scale $\theta$.

Transformation:

$$
Y_1=\frac{X_1}{X_1+X_2},
$$

$$
Y_2=X_1+X_2.
$$

## 10.1 Inverse

$$
x_1=y_1y_2,
$$

$$
x_2=y_2-y_1y_2
=
y_2(1-y_1).
$$

## 10.2 Jacobian

$$
J
=
\begin{vmatrix}
y_2&y_1\\
-y_2&1-y_1
\end{vmatrix}
=
y_2.
$$

## 10.3 Support

Karena:

$$
x_1>0,\qquad x_2>0,
$$

maka:

$$
\boxed{
0<y_1<1,
\qquad
y_2>0.
}
$$

## 10.4 Joint PDF

Substitution memberi factorization dalam $y_1$ dan $y_2$:

$$
f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)
\propto
y_1^{\alpha-1}(1-y_1)^{\beta-1}
\;
y_2^{\alpha+\beta-1}e^{-y_2/\theta}.
$$

Setelah constants dikenali:

$$
\boxed{
Y_1\sim\operatorname{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)
}
$$

dan:

$$
\boxed{
Y_2\sim\Gamma(\alpha+\beta,\theta).
}
$$

Factorization + rectangular support menunjukkan:

$$
\boxed{
Y_1\text{ dan }Y_2\text{ independent}.
}
$$

### High-Value Pattern

$$
\boxed{
\frac{X_1}{X_1+X_2}
\quad\text{dan}\quad
X_1+X_2
}
$$

untuk independent Gammas dengan common scale menghasilkan Beta + Gamma structure.

> [!WARNING] Parameterization
> Relationship ini bergantung pada **common scale/rate convention yang compatible**. Selalu deklarasikan Gamma parameterization sebelum menggunakan hasil.

# Section 11 — Many-to-One Transformations

Hogg, McKean & Craig §2.7 memperluas transformation technique ke kasus ketika inverse mempunyai beberapa branches.

Jika untuk satu transformed point $\mathbf y$ terdapat $k$ valid inverse points:

$$
\mathbf x^{(1)},\ldots,\mathbf x^{(k)},
$$

maka density harus menjumlahkan semua contributions:

$$
\boxed{
g(\mathbf y)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{k}
f(\mathbf x^{(i)}(\mathbf y))
|J_i|.
}
$$

Ini adalah multivariate analogue dari many-to-one univariate transformation.

> [!DANGER] Branch Trap
> Jangan memakai hanya satu inverse branch jika beberapa original points memetakan ke transformed point yang sama.

---

# Section 12 — Example Many-to-One from Hogg, McKean & Craig

Original joint PDF uniform pada unit disk:

$$
f(x_1,x_2)
=
\frac1\pi,
\qquad
0<x_1^2+x_2^2<1.
$$

Transformation:

$$
Y_1=X_1^2+X_2^2,
$$

$$
Y_2=
\frac{X_1^2}
{X_1^2+X_2^2}.
$$

Mapped support:

$$
0<y_1<1,
\qquad
0<y_2<1.
$$

Untuk setiap $(y_1,y_2)$ ada empat sign combinations:

$$
x_1=\pm\sqrt{y_1y_2},
$$

$$
x_2=\pm\sqrt{y_1(1-y_2)}.
$$

Source menunjukkan absolute Jacobian tiap branch:

$$
|J_i|
=
\frac{1}
{4\sqrt{y_2(1-y_2)}}.
$$

Karena ada empat branches:

$$
g(y_1,y_2)
=
4\left(
\frac1\pi
\right)
\left(
\frac{1}
{4\sqrt{y_2(1-y_2)}}
\right).
$$

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
g(y_1,y_2)
=
\frac1{
\pi\sqrt{y_2(1-y_2)}
},
\qquad
0<y_1<1,\;0<y_2<1.
}
$$

Joint PDF does not depend on $y_1$ and factorizes on rectangular support, sehingga transformed variables independent.

### Lesson

Many-to-one problem =

```text
identify every inverse branch
      ↓
compute each |J|
      ↓
sum all contributions
```

# Section 13 — Generalization to n Random Variables

Hogg, McKean & Craig §2.7 extends theorem.

Untuk:

$$
(Y_1,\ldots,Y_n)
=
T(X_1,\ldots,X_n)
$$

one-to-one dengan inverse:

$$
x_i=w_i(y_1,\ldots,y_n),
$$

Jacobian:

$$
J
=
\det
\left[
\frac{\partial x_i}{\partial y_j}
\right].
$$

Then:

$$
\boxed{
g(y_1,\ldots,y_n)
=
f(
w_1(\mathbf y),
\ldots,
w_n(\mathbf y)
)
|J|
}
$$

pada transformed support.

Source example:

$$
0<x_1<x_2<x_3<1,
$$

with transformation:

$$
Y_1=\frac{X_1}{X_2},
\qquad
Y_2=\frac{X_2}{X_3},
\qquad
Y_3=X_3.
$$

Inverse:

$$
x_1=y_1y_2y_3,
$$

$$
x_2=y_2y_3,
$$

$$
x_3=y_3.
$$

Jacobian source:

$$
\boxed{
J=y_2y_3^2.
}
$$

Transformed support simplifies beautifully to:

$$
\boxed{
0<Y_i<1,
\qquad i=1,2,3.
}
$$

> [!NOTE] Exam Relevance
> Silabus meminta transformasi random variables gabungan secara umum. Dua-variable cases kemungkinan menjadi calculation core; n-dimensional theorem berguna untuk memahami general structure dan determinant notation.

# Section 14 — Discrete Transformations

Jacobian tidak digunakan untuk discrete random variables.

Jika:

$$
Y=g(X_1,X_2),
$$

maka:

$$
P(Y=y)
=
\sum_{(x_1,x_2):g(x_1,x_2)=y}
P(X_1=x_1,X_2=x_2).
$$

Untuk transformed vector:

$$
P(Y_1=y_1,Y_2=y_2)
=
\sum_{\mathbf x:T(\mathbf x)=\mathbf y}
P(\mathbf X=\mathbf x).
$$

Mental rule:

> **Discrete = collect masses. Continuous = map density with Jacobian.**

# Section 15 — Verification & Sanity Checks

> [!CHECK] Sanity Checks
>
> **1. Inverse check**
>
> Substitute inverse back into transformation and recover $y_1,y_2$.
>
> **2. Support check**
>
> Every mapped point must correspond to a valid original point.
>
> **3. Jacobian check**
>
> Ensure determinant is:
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial(x_1,x_2)}
> {\partial(y_1,y_2)}
> $$
>
> if using source theorem directly.
>
> **4. Absolute value**
>
> Density contribution must use $|J|$.
>
> **5. Normalization**
>
> $$
> \iint_{S_Y}
> f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)
> dy_1dy_2
> =1.
> $$
>
> **6. Marginal support**
>
> After integrating out auxiliary variable, target PDF must have correct support.
>
> **7. Known-family recognition**
>
> If transformed density factorizes into known PDFs, verify constants and support before naming distributions.
>
> **8. Independence**
>
> Factorization alone must be accompanied by product support criterion.

# Section 16 — Common Mistakes & Exam Traps

| Trap | Kesalahan | Mengapa Salah | Pencegahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Support-last trap | menghitung density sebelum transformed support | bounds bisa salah total | map support before final density |
| Forward-vs-inverse Jacobian | pakai determinant yang salah arah | reciprocal missing | write Jacobian notation |
| Signed-Jacobian trap | tidak ambil absolute value | density dapat negatif | always $|J|$ |
| Auxiliary-variable trap | memilih $Y_2$ yang membuat inverse sulit | algebra membengkak | choose $X_1$ or $X_2$ if possible |
| Piecewise-bound trap | satu marginal bound dipakai untuk seluruh support | transformed region changes geometry | sketch / split ranges |
| Branch trap | many-to-one diperlakukan one-to-one | probability mass/density hilang | enumerate inverse branches |
| Independence trap | transformed joint PDF factorizes but support not product | factorization criterion incomplete | check support |
| Distribution-recognition trap | melihat Beta/Gamma shape tetapi constant/support salah | incomplete matching | match full PDF |
| Gamma-parameter trap | common rate vs common scale tidak konsisten | ratio/sum result berubah notation | declare convention |
| Discrete-Jacobian trap | memakai derivative untuk discrete transform | no density-area scaling | aggregate PMF masses |
| Target-vs-joint trap | berhenti pada $f_{Y,Z}$ padahal soal minta $f_Y$ | auxiliary variable belum eliminated | marginalize |
| CDF/Jacobian choice trap | memaksakan Jacobian pada sum sederhana | unnecessary work | compare geometry vs inverse route |

# Section 17 — Method Selection Guide

| Problem Type | Preferred Method | Why |
|---|---|---|
| $Y=X_1+X_2$ only, simple region | CDF / convolution-like geometry | direct event region |
| $Y=X_1+X_2$, want systematic transform | add $Z=X_2$ + Jacobian | clean inverse |
| ratio $X_1/X_2$ | ratio + scale auxiliary variable | natural inverse |
| ratio $X_1/(X_1+X_2)$ | ratio + sum | canonical Beta/Gamma pattern |
| two outputs explicitly requested | Jacobian | direct joint transformed PDF |
| many-to-one squared/radial transform | branch summation | multiple inverses |
| discrete transformation | direct PMF aggregation | Jacobian irrelevant |
| linear transformation with only moments | covariance-matrix formulas from 3.6 | no need full PDF |

> [!IMPORTANT] Do Not Overuse Jacobian
> Jika soal hanya menanyakan mean/variance linear transformation, [[3.6 Matriks Variansi-Kovariansi]] mungkin lebih cepat:
>
> $$
> E[A\mathbf X+\mathbf b]
> =
> A\mu+\mathbf b,
> $$
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Cov}(A\mathbf X+\mathbf b)
> =
> A\Sigma A^\top.
> $$

# Section 18 — Formula Map

| Target | Formula | Key Condition |
|---|---|---|
| 2D transformed PDF | $f_Y(\mathbf y)=f_X(\mathbf w(\mathbf y))|J|$ | one-to-one |
| Inverse Jacobian | $J=\det[\partial x_i/\partial y_j]$ | differentiable inverse |
| Reciprocal relation | $|J_{x\leftarrow y}|=1/|J_{y\leftarrow x}|$ | regular mapping |
| Marginal target | $f_{Y_1}(y_1)=\int f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)dy_2$ | bounds from transformed support |
| Many-to-one | $\sum_i f_X(\mathbf w_i(\mathbf y))|J_i|$ | all valid branches |
| nD transform | $g(\mathbf y)=f(\mathbf w(\mathbf y))|\det D\mathbf w|$ | one-to-one regular transform |
| Discrete transform | $\sum_{\mathbf x:T(\mathbf x)=\mathbf y}p_X(\mathbf x)$ | collect masses |
| CDF approach | $F_Y(y)=P(g(X_1,X_2)\le y)$ | event region manageable |

# Section 19 — Executive Summary

> [!SUMMARY] Must Remember
>
> 1. Transformasi gabungan memetakan **joint support**, bukan hanya formula variables.
>
> 2. Untuk one-to-one continuous transformation:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> f_{Y_1,Y_2}(y_1,y_2)
> =
> f_{X_1,X_2}(w_1,w_2)
> \left|
> \frac{\partial(x_1,x_2)}
> {\partial(y_1,y_2)}
> \right|.
> }
> $$
>
> 3. Order kerja:
>
> **transformation → inverse → support → Jacobian → substitute → marginalize**.
>
> 4. Jacobian selalu gunakan **absolute determinant**.
>
> 5. Mapping support sering menjadi bagian tersulit.
>
> 6. Jika hanya satu target function, tambahkan auxiliary variable yang membuat transformation invertible.
>
> 7. Untuk sum $X_1+X_2$, CDF technique sering menjadi alternatif yang sangat efisien.
>
> 8. Banyak inverse branches ⇒ sum semua Jacobian contributions.
>
> 9. Diskrit tidak memakai Jacobian—aggregate probability masses.
>
> 10. Factorization transformed joint PDF + product support dapat menunjukkan independence.
>
> 11. Classic pattern:
>
> $$
> X_1,X_2\text{ independent Gamma with common scale}
> $$
>
> menghasilkan
>
> $$
> \frac{X_1}{X_1+X_2}
> \sim \operatorname{Beta},
> $$
>
> dan
>
> $$
> X_1+X_2
> \sim \Gamma,
> $$
>
> dengan transformed variables independent.
>
> 12. Selalu verify transformed support sebelum percaya pada PDF akhir.
