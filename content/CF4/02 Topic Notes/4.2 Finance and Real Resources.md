# 📘 4.2 — Finance and Real Resources

> [!ABSTRACT] Ringkasan Cepat
> **Topik:** Finance and Real Resources  
> **Bobot Parent Topic:** 5–15%  
> **Exam Priority:** Lower  
> **Difficulty:** Concept-Intensive  
> **Primary Skill:** Concept + Interpretation  
> **Ref:** Brigham & Ehrhardt Bab 13; Berk & DeMarzo Bab 16.1–16.7, 18.1–18.5, 22.1–22.8, 28.1–28.6, 29.1–29.4, 31.
>
> Inti materi: **financial resources adalah claims dan funding yang digunakan untuk memperoleh atau mendukung real resources. Real resources—seperti land, buildings, machines, inventory, knowledge, patents, dan investment opportunities—yang pada akhirnya menghasilkan goods/services dan operating cash flows. Finance menciptakan value bukan dengan “menciptakan uang dari uang”, tetapi dengan mengalokasikan scarce capital ke penggunaan real yang expected return-nya melebihi opportunity cost of capital.**
>
> Pada level perusahaan, keputusan yang benar adalah memilih penggunaan real resources yang meningkatkan present value of future free cash flows. Pada level negara, referensi resmi Topik 4 terutama menunjukkan peran pemerintah melalui **taxation, regulation, capital controls, legal/property-right environment, dan intervention in financial distress**. Sumber yang tersedia tidak memberikan satu teori eksplisit dan lengkap mengenai “tujuan negara”; karena itu bagian negara di note ini dibatasi pada mekanisme yang memang didukung textbook.

# Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

## 1.1 Dua Dunia yang Harus Dibedakan

Corporate finance bekerja di antara dua dunia:

### Dunia 1 — Financial Resources

Contoh:

- cash;
- debt financing;
- equity financing;
- retained funds;
- securities;
- claims milik investors;
- access to capital markets.

Financial resources **memberi kemampuan membiayai kegiatan**, tetapi tidak selalu secara langsung menghasilkan goods atau services.

### Dunia 2 — Real Resources

Contoh yang disebut atau implied oleh Brigham Chapter 13:

- land;
- buildings;
- machines;
- inventory;
- patents;
- customer relationships;
- reputation;
- operating know-how;
- knowledge base;
- growth opportunities.

Inilah resources yang dipakai untuk melakukan aktivitas ekonomi dan menghasilkan future cash flows.

### Economic Chain

```text
FINANCIAL RESOURCES
        ↓
fund investment
        ↓
REAL RESOURCES
        ↓
produce goods/services
        ↓
generate operating cash flows
        ↓
free cash flow
        ↓
supports financial claims
        ↓
value to investors
```

> [!TIP] Feynman Version
> Uang perusahaan bukan tujuan akhir.
>
> Uang digunakan untuk membeli atau mengembangkan **sesuatu yang produktif**.
>
> Sesuatu yang produktif itu menghasilkan cash flow.
>
> Cash flow itulah yang memberi nilai ekonomi kepada securities dan perusahaan.

---

## 1.2 Contoh Sederhana

Sebuah perusahaan memperoleh Rp100 miliar dari investors.

Perusahaan memiliki dua pilihan:

### Pilihan A
Menyimpan seluruh dana sebagai cash.

### Pilihan B
Menggunakan Rp100 miliar untuk membangun production facility yang diperkirakan menghasilkan cash flows bernilai sekarang Rp130 miliar.

Secara ekonomi:

\[
NPV = 130 - 100 = 30
\]

Maka Pilihan B menciptakan value Rp30 miliar.

Capital bukan menciptakan value hanya karena “ada”.

Value muncul karena capital dialokasikan ke **real investment** dengan:

\[
\boxed{NPV > 0}
\]

# Section 2 — Financial Resources vs Real Resources

## 2.1 Financial Resources

Dalam konteks finance, financial resources mewakili **purchasing power, funding capacity, atau financial claims**.

Misalnya:

```text
Investor cash
      ↓
buys corporate security
      ↓
Corporation receives financing
```

Dari sisi investor, stock atau bond merupakan **financial asset**.

Dari sisi corporation, funds yang diterima dapat digunakan untuk memperoleh atau membangun operating assets.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Security ≠ underlying productive resource.**
>
> Corporate bond adalah claim terhadap issuer.
>
> Common stock adalah ownership claim terhadap corporation.
>
> Factory, machine, inventory, patent, atau operating capability adalah real/operating resource yang menghasilkan economic output.

---

## 2.2 Real Resources dalam Brigham Chapter 13

Brigham membagi corporate assets menjadi:

```text
Corporate assets
├── Operating assets
│   ├── Assets-in-place
│   └── Growth options
└── Nonoperating assets
```

### Assets-in-Place

Termasuk:

- land;
- buildings;
- machines;
- inventory;
- patents;
- customer lists;
- reputation;
- general know-how.

Assets-in-place menghasilkan **current and expected operating cash flows**.

### Growth Options

Growth options adalah opportunities untuk melakukan future investment yang muncul dari:

- existing knowledge;
- experience;
- technology;
- brand;
- market position;
- other operating resources.

Jadi real resource tidak hanya berbentuk physical property.

Knowledge dan opportunity juga dapat memiliki economic value.

---

## 2.3 Nonoperating Financial Assets

Perusahaan juga dapat memegang:

- excess cash;
- marketable securities;
- investments in other businesses.

Brigham membedakan assets ini dari operating assets.

Kenapa?

Karena untuk sebagian besar operating companies, **value creation terutama berasal dari operating resources**, bukan dari sekadar memegang tradable financial securities.

Secara konseptual:

```text
Operating asset
→ management dapat mengubah operations
→ cash flows dapat diperbaiki
→ value dapat diciptakan

Marketable security held by firm
→ value banyak ditentukan market
→ management firm tidak mengendalikan underlying business secara langsung
```

> [!IMPORTANT] Exam Point
> Dalam Brigham Chapter 13, **value-based management fokus terutama pada operating assets**, karena management dapat memengaruhi operating performance dan future cash flows dari resources tersebut.

---

# Section 3 — Dari Real Resources ke Corporate Value

## 3.1 Free Cash Flow sebagai Bridge

Brigham mendefinisikan free cash flow sebagai cash from operations yang tersedia bagi investors setelah kebutuhan reinvestment dalam operating capital diperhitungkan.

Core logic:

\[
\boxed{
FCF
=
NOPAT
-
\text{Investment in Operating Capital}
}
\]

Di sini terlihat hubungan langsung antara finance dan real resources.

Jika perusahaan bertumbuh, perusahaan sering perlu menambah:

- inventory;
- receivables support;
- plant;
- equipment;
- operating technology;
- other productive capital.

Semua itu membutuhkan investment.

Karena itu:

> **accounting profit yang tinggi tidak otomatis berarti cash available to investors tinggi.**

---

## 3.2 Corporate Value

Brigham menggunakan:

\[
\boxed{
V_{\text{operations}}
=
\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}
\frac{FCF_t}{(1+WACC)^t}
}
\]

Artinya value operating assets ditentukan oleh:

1. berapa cash flow yang dapat dihasilkan;
2. kapan cash flow dihasilkan;
3. risk dari cash flow;
4. jumlah capital yang harus terus ditanamkan.

### Causal Chain

```text
Real resources
      ↓
Operating capability
      ↓
Sales / margins / capital needs
      ↓
NOPAT
      ↓
Reinvestment requirement
      ↓
FCF
      ↓
Discount at WACC
      ↓
Value of operations
```

---

## 3.3 Growth Tidak Otomatis Menciptakan Value

Ini salah satu poin paling penting pada Topik 4.2.

Banyak orang berpikir:

> growth = good.

Tetapi Brigham menunjukkan growth hanya menciptakan value jika return dari incremental capital cukup tinggi.

Gunakan concept:

\[
EROIC
=
\frac{\text{Expected NOPAT}}{\text{Operating Capital}}
\]

Bandingkan dengan:

\[
WACC
\]

### Case 1

\[
EROIC > WACC
\]

Interpretation:

- real resources menghasilkan return lebih tinggi daripada opportunity cost of capital;
- additional investment dapat menciptakan value;
- growth biasanya meningkatkan value.

### Case 2

\[
EROIC = WACC
\]

Interpretation:

- company secara economic “breaks even”;
- accounting profit bisa positif;
- tetapi additional capital hanya menghasilkan return yang cukup untuk compensate investors;
- growth tidak menciptakan incremental economic value.

### Case 3

\[
EROIC < WACC
\]

Interpretation:

- operating resources menghasilkan return di bawah required return;
- additional growth dapat **menghancurkan value**.

> [!DANGER] Exam Trap
> **Profitable ≠ value creating.**
>
> Perusahaan dapat menghasilkan positive accounting profit tetapi tetap destroy value jika return atas incremental real resources lebih rendah daripada cost of capital.

---

# Section 4 — Resource Allocation dan Positive-NPV Rule

## 4.1 Economic Objective Perusahaan

Dalam source resmi, corporate finance memakai benchmark:

> management should choose actions that maximize firm/shareholder value.

Dalam capital allocation language:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Accept investment if } NPV > 0
}
\]

Dengan:

\[
NPV
=
PV(\text{future project cash flows})
-
\text{initial investment}
\]

Positive NPV berarti project menghasilkan expected return yang lebih tinggi daripada return yang required investors untuk risk yang ditanggung.

---

## 4.2 Apa Artinya Secara Real Resource?

Positive-NPV rule sebenarnya adalah rule untuk resource allocation.

```text
Scarce financial capital
        ↓
candidate real investments
        ↓
evaluate expected cash flows + risk
        ↓
allocate to highest value-creating uses
```

### Positive NPV

Financial resources diubah menjadi real resources yang menciptakan economic surplus.

### Zero NPV

Firm memperoleh fair return, tetapi tidak menciptakan excess value.

### Negative NPV

Resources digunakan untuk activity yang nilainya lebih rendah daripada opportunity cost-nya.

---

# Section 5 — Real Options: Real Resources Bukan Selalu “Invest Now or Reject”

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 22 memperluas cara melihat real investment.

Traditional NPV sering dipresentasikan seolah management hanya memilih:

```text
invest now
vs
do not invest
```

Dalam real life, management sering memiliki **flexibility**.

Itulah **real options**.

## 5.1 Real Option

Real option adalah hak, bukan kewajiban, untuk membuat future business/investment decision.

Examples dalam scope Chapter 22:

- option to delay;
- option to expand;
- growth option;
- abandonment option;
- staging/sequential decisions;
- choice among related projects.

### Why It Matters

Real resources dapat memiliki value bukan hanya karena current cash flows, tetapi karena **future strategic flexibility**.

Contoh:

```text
R&D investment today
      ↓
creates knowledge
      ↓
if results good → expand
if results bad → stop
```

Initial investment dapat memiliki value karena membuka future opportunity.

---

## 5.2 Financial Option vs Real Option

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Financial option** = contractual claim traded/created in financial markets.
>
> **Real option** = flexibility embedded in real investment or operating decision.

Contoh:

- call option on stock → financial option;
- ability to delay construction → real option;
- right to abandon factory project → real option.

---

# Section 6 — Financing Frictions Dapat Merusak Real Resource Allocation

Topik 4.2 tidak berhenti pada “capital membiayai assets”.

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 16 menunjukkan bahwa financing structure dapat mengubah **investment incentives**.

Ini penting karena keputusan finance dapat mengakibatkan real resources **misallocated**.

---

## 6.1 Risk Shifting / Asset Substitution

Ketika firm memiliki banyak debt dan mendekati distress, equity holders dapat memperoleh incentive untuk memilih strategy yang lebih risky.

Mengapa?

Karena payoff equity memiliki limited downside tetapi upside besar.

Potential result:

```text
Debt outstanding
      ↓
equity payoff resembles option-like residual
      ↓
shareholders prefer extra risk
      ↓
management may choose risky project
      ↓
even if project has negative NPV
```

Jika negative-NPV project diambil:

\[
\text{Total firm value decreases}
\]

meskipun equity holders mungkin berharap mendapatkan transfer of value dari debt holders.

> [!DANGER] Exam Trap
> Decision yang menguntungkan shareholders **tidak selalu** meningkatkan total firm value jika keputusan tersebut transfers wealth dari creditors dan destroys NPV.

---

## 6.2 Debt Overhang / Underinvestment

Kebalikan risk shifting adalah **underinvestment**.

Firm mungkin memiliki positive-NPV project, tetapi equity holders menolak membiayainya karena sebagian besar gain mengalir ke existing creditors.

Textbook example logic:

```text
Positive-NPV project exists
      ↓
requires new equity contribution
      ↓
new value improves creditors' position substantially
      ↓
equity captures too little benefit
      ↓
shareholders reject project
```

Akibatnya:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Positive-NPV real investment is forgone}
}
\]

dan total firm value menjadi lebih rendah.

> [!IMPORTANT] Finance → Real Resource Link
> Debt overhang menunjukkan dengan jelas bahwa **financial claims dapat distort real investment decisions**.

---

## 6.3 Free Cash Flow Problem / Overinvestment

Berk & DeMarzo juga membahas **free cash flow hypothesis**.

Jika management memiliki excess cash setelah:

- funding all positive-NPV projects;
- meeting obligations;

management dapat memiliki incentive menggunakan cash untuk:

- empire building;
- excessive expansion;
- managerial perks;
- low-return projects.

Ini menghasilkan **overinvestment**.

```text
Excess financial resources
        ↓
weak discipline
        ↓
wasteful real investment
        ↓
negative or low NPV
        ↓
firm value ↓
```

Debt dapat mengurangi excess cash melalui required payments, tetapi excessive debt juga dapat menyebabkan underinvestment.

Jadi:

> **lebih banyak financial resources tidak otomatis lebih baik.**
>
> Yang penting adalah apakah resources dialokasikan secara efficient.

---

# Section 7 — Merger & Acquisition sebagai Reallocation of Real Resources

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 28 menunjukkan bahwa M&A dapat mengubah:

- ownership;
- control;
- combination of assets;
- operating strategy;
- resource utilization.

## 7.1 Value-Creating Acquisition

Acquisition hanya menciptakan total value jika combined business menghasilkan value lebih tinggi daripada separate businesses setelah relevant costs.

Secara sederhana:

\[
\boxed{
Synergy
=
V_{AB}
-
(V_A+V_B)
}
\]

Potential sources dapat mencakup:

- operating efficiencies;
- better asset utilization;
- improved management;
- economies of scale/scope;
- strategic combinations.

Namun acquisition premium menentukan bagaimana gains dibagi antara bidder dan target shareholders.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Reallocation of control ≠ automatic creation of real economic value.**
>
> Value hanya tercipta jika real resources dapat digunakan lebih productively atau cash flows/risk improve.

# Section 8 — Tujuan Organisasi: Value Creation sebagai Resource-Allocation Rule

Dalam source Topik 4, tujuan corporate management dirumuskan terutama dengan shareholder-value/intrinsic-value framework.

Namun interpretation-nya bukan:

> “maximize accounting profit at any cost.”

Yang diuji lebih tepat:

```text
Use financial resources
      ↓
to acquire/develop real resources
      ↓
that generate risk-adjusted cash flows
      ↓
above opportunity cost of capital
      ↓
thus increase corporate value
```

## 8.1 Empat Value Drivers dari Brigham

Brigham menghubungkan operating value dengan:

1. **growth**;
2. **operating profitability**;
3. **capital requirements**;
4. **WACC**.

Mental model:

```text
Growth ↑
does not guarantee
Value ↑

because growth may require
large real investment

and if
return on incremental capital < WACC

then
Value ↓
```

---

## 8.2 Bell Electronics Lesson

Brigham menggunakan divisions untuk menunjukkan bahwa higher profitability tidak cukup.

Satu division memiliki higher profitability tetapi juga membutuhkan sangat banyak capital.

Yang harus dilihat:

\[
\text{return generated by real resources}
\quad \text{vs} \quad
\text{cost of financial resources}
\]

Jika:

\[
EROIC > WACC
\]

additional capital creates value.

Jika:

\[
EROIC < WACC
\]

growth destroys value.

> [!TIP] One-Line Memory
> **Real resources create value only when their incremental return exceeds the cost of financing those resources.**

# Section 9 — Negara, Institutions, dan Resource Allocation

## 9.1 Apa yang Source Topik 4 Mendukung?

Silabus meminta hubungan financial-real resources dengan **tujuan negara**.

Referensi yang tersedia tidak memberi chapter makroekonomi tentang objectives of government.

Tetapi Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 31 dan Chapter 16 memberikan mekanisme yang jelas di mana negara memengaruhi capital allocation melalui:

- corporate taxation;
- cross-border tax rules;
- capital controls;
- foreign-exchange controls;
- restrictions on security ownership;
- legal/property-right environment;
- government intervention/bailouts;
- regulatory environment.

Brigham Chapter 13 juga menunjukkan regulatory environment sebagai external governance factor.

Karena itu, untuk CF4 dari sources ini, cara aman memahami sisi negara adalah:

> **negara membentuk aturan dan frictions yang memengaruhi bagaimana financial capital berpindah dan bagaimana real investments dinilai/dilakukan.**

---

## 9.2 Taxation Mengubah Resource Allocation

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 31 menjelaskan foreign projects dapat menghadapi:

- host-country tax;
- home-country tax;
- tax credits;
- repatriation rules;
- possible tax deferral.

Taxes memengaruhi:

\[
\text{after-tax project cash flows}
\]

maka memengaruhi:

\[
NPV
\]

dan akhirnya dapat memengaruhi di mana perusahaan menaruh real resources.

### Causal Chain

```text
Tax rules
    ↓
after-tax cash flows
    ↓
project NPV
    ↓
investment attractiveness
    ↓
location/allocation of real resources
```

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap
> Tax adalah **cash-flow effect**, bukan sekadar accounting presentation.

---

## 9.3 Capital Controls dan Foreign Exchange Controls

Chapter 31 menjelaskan international capital markets dapat segmented jika countries:

- regulate capital inflows;
- regulate capital outflows;
- restrict convertibility;
- restrict ownership of securities.

Result:

```text
Capital-flow barrier
      ↓
financial markets segmented
      ↓
cost of capital can differ
      ↓
project valuation differs
      ↓
real investment allocation changes
```

Jadi national rules dapat mengubah **where capital goes**.

---

## 9.4 Legal and Property-Rights Environment

Chapter 31 menyebut political, legal, social, dan cultural characteristics dapat menyebabkan **country risk premium**.

Weak property-right enforcement dapat membuat investors meminta higher return.

Secara economic logic:

\[
\text{Country risk} \uparrow
\Rightarrow
\text{Required return} \uparrow
\Rightarrow
PV(\text{investment}) \downarrow
\]

Jika cost of capital meningkat:

- fewer projects pass NPV test;
- real investment can decline;
- financing becomes more expensive.

> [!IMPORTANT] Finance–Nation Link
> Institutions yang memengaruhi investor protection dan capital access pada akhirnya dapat memengaruhi **real investment capacity**.

---

## 9.5 Government Intervention dalam Financial Distress

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 16 menggunakan financial-crisis examples untuk menunjukkan government intervention/bailouts.

Government support dapat:

- preserve financing capacity;
- prevent collapse of institutions;
- support continued lending/investment;

tetapi juga dapat alter incentives.

Jika market participants percaya losses akan ditanggung pemerintah, muncul **moral hazard**.

```text
Expected bailout
      ↓
private downside reduced
      ↓
risk-taking incentive ↑
      ↓
resource allocation may become distorted
```

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> Government intervention dapat mengurangi immediate distress tetapi juga mengubah future incentives.
>
> Source tidak menyatakan bahwa bailout selalu baik atau selalu buruk.

# Section 10 — International Resource Allocation

## 10.1 Foreign Projects

Foreign real investment menambah tiga issues utama dalam Chapter 31:

1. cash flows dalam foreign currency;
2. different interest rates/costs of capital;
3. different taxation.

Company tetap menggunakan prinsip yang sama:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Invest if project creates positive value}
}
\]

Tetapi value harus diukur consistently.

---

## 10.2 Integrated Capital Markets

Jika markets internationally integrated, value foreign project tidak bergantung pada currency yang digunakan untuk analysis.

Two valid routes:

### Route A

```text
Foreign CF
→ discount with foreign-currency cost of capital
→ foreign NPV
→ convert at spot rate
```

### Route B

```text
Foreign CF
→ convert using appropriate exchange-rate relationship
→ home-currency CF
→ discount with home-currency cost of capital
```

Dalam integrated market:

\[
\boxed{
NPV_{\text{Route A}}
=
NPV_{\text{Route B}}
}
\]

Economic meaning:

> financing currency alone tidak menciptakan real value.

---

## 10.3 Segmented Capital Markets

Jika markets segmented:

- firms dapat memiliki different access;
- cost of capital can differ across countries;
- information asymmetry can make foreign investors demand higher return;
- controls can block equalization.

Firm dengan better access di home market mungkin:

```text
raise capital where cheaper
        ↓
convert/swap financing exposure
        ↓
invest where real opportunities are most valuable
```

Ini menunjukkan bagaimana financial architecture lintas negara memengaruhi deployment of real resources.

- financial resource?

Tanyakan:

## Step 3 — Tentukan Capital Requirement

Berapa financial capital diperlukan?

Apakah growth membutuhkan additional:

- inventory;
- plant;
- working capital;
- acquisition cost;
- R&D;
- expansion cost?

## Step 4 — Bandingkan Return vs Cost of Capital

Gunakan:

\[
EROIC \quad \text{vs} \quad WACC
\]

atau:

\[
NPV
\]

## Step 5 — Cek Financial Friction

Apakah ada:

- distress;
- debt overhang;
- overinvestment;
- tax;
- information asymmetry;
- capital controls;
- segmentation?

## Step 6 — Tentukan Value Effect

```text
Does the decision:
create value?
destroy value?
or merely redistribute value?
```

# Section 12 — Exam Traps & Misconceptions

## 12.1 Financial Asset vs Real Asset

> [!BUG] **Cash/security ≠ productive operating asset.**
>
> Financial asset adalah claim atau store of financial value. Real/operating resource menghasilkan business output/cash flow.

---

## 12.2 Profit vs Value Creation

> [!BUG] **Positive profit ≠ positive economic value creation.**
>
> Jika return on capital < WACC, perusahaan dapat profitable tetapi economically destroy value.

---

## 12.3 Growth vs Value

> [!BUG] **Growth ≠ automatically good.**
>
> Growth yang memerlukan expensive operating capital dengan low return dapat menurunkan value.

---

## 12.4 More Cash vs Better Company

> [!BUG] **More free cash ≠ automatically better allocation.**
>
> Excess cash dengan weak governance dapat memicu overinvestment atau empire building.

---

## 12.5 Debt Discipline vs Debt Problem

> [!BUG] **Debt is not purely good or bad.**
>
> Debt dapat:
>
> - discipline managers;
> - reduce excess free cash;
>
> tetapi juga:
>
> - induce risk shifting;
> - cause debt overhang;
> - create distress costs.

---

## 12.6 National Policy

> [!BUG] **Government policy ≠ merely financial bookkeeping.**
>
> Tax, capital controls, property-right enforcement, regulation, dan bailout expectations dapat mengubah real investment incentives.

---

## 12.7 Foreign Interest Rate

> [!BUG] **Lower foreign nominal rate ≠ automatically lower economic cost of capital.**
>
> Currency, risk, access, tax, dan market integration harus konsisten.

---

## 12.8 M&A

> [!BUG] **Asset transfer ≠ value creation.**
>
> Acquisition menciptakan economic value hanya jika combined resources menghasilkan synergy/efficiency yang melebihi relevant costs.

---

# Section 13 — Direction-of-Effect Map

| Event | Immediate Effect | Real Resource Effect | Likely Value Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive-NPV project accepted | Capital committed | Productive assets increase | Value ↑ |
| Negative-NPV expansion | Capital committed | Resources tied to low-return use | Value ↓ |
| EROIC rises above WACC | Operating efficiency/return improves | Existing/new resources more productive | Value ↑ |
| Capital requirement rises, return unchanged | More funding required | More resources needed per unit output | Value ↓ |
| WACC rises | Financing opportunity cost rises | Fewer projects economically attractive | Value ↓ |
| Debt overhang | Equity incentive distorted | Positive-NPV investment may be forgone | Value ↓ |
| Excess free cash + weak discipline | Cash available | Wasteful investment possible | Value ↓ |
| Effective debt discipline | Required payouts rise | Less wasteful investment | Potential value ↑ |
| Severe leverage/distress | Financing constraint rises | Underinvestment / asset sales | Value ↓ |
| Stronger capital controls | Cross-border finance restricted | Foreign investment flexibility ↓ | Depends, often cost of capital ↑ |
| Higher country risk | Required return rises | Fewer investments pass NPV hurdle | Value ↓ |
| Tax burden on project rises | After-tax FCF falls | Project attractiveness ↓ | Value ↓ |
| Better asset utilization | Capital needed per sales falls | Same output with fewer resources | Value ↑ |
| Value-creating merger | Resources recombined efficiently | Synergy ↑ | Total value ↑ |

# Section 14 — Core Formulas

## 14.1 Free Cash Flow

\[
\boxed{
FCF
=
NOPAT
-
\text{Investment in Operating Capital}
}
\]

**Meaning:** cash generated by operations after funding required operating reinvestment.

---

## 14.2 Corporate Value

\[
\boxed{
V_{\text{operations}}
=
\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}
\frac{FCF_t}{(1+WACC)^t}
}
\]

**Meaning:** value of operating resources equals present value of future free cash flows.

---

## 14.3 Net Present Value

\[
\boxed{
NPV
=
PV(\text{Future Cash Flows})
-
\text{Investment}
}
\]

Decision:

\[
NPV>0
\Rightarrow
\text{value-creating investment}
\]

---

## 14.4 Expected Return on Invested Capital

Conceptually:

\[
\boxed{
EROIC
=
\frac{\text{Expected NOPAT}}{\text{Operating Capital}}
}
\]

Compare:

\[
\boxed{
EROIC \text{ vs } WACC
}
\]

- \(EROIC>WACC\) → economic value creation;
- \(EROIC=WACC\) → economic break-even;
- \(EROIC<WACC\) → value destruction.

---

## 14.5 Merger Synergy

\[
\boxed{
Synergy
=
V_{AB}
-
(V_A+V_B)
}
\]

Positive synergy means combination of real resources raises total value before considering how gains are divided.

# Section 16 — Executive Summary

### Must Remember

1. **Financial resources** adalah funding/financial claims; **real resources** adalah productive/operating assets dan capabilities.
2. Financial securities mendapatkan value karena ada underlying future cash flows dari real economic activity.
3. Brigham membagi operating assets menjadi **assets-in-place** dan **growth options**.
4. Operating assets lebih penting bagi value-based management karena management dapat memengaruhi operating performance.
5. Free cash flow menghubungkan operating performance dengan capital providers:

\[
FCF=NOPAT-\text{Investment in Operating Capital}
\]

6. Corporate value adalah PV dari expected future FCF discounted at WACC.
7. Growth tidak otomatis creates value.
8. Rule terpenting:

\[
EROIC>WACC
\Rightarrow
\text{growth creates value}
\]

9. Positive-NPV investment berarti financial capital dialokasikan ke real use yang menghasilkan economic surplus.
10. Real options menunjukkan bahwa flexibility—delay, expand, abandon—dapat menjadi bagian dari value real investment.
11. Financial structure dapat distort real investment:
    - risk shifting;
    - debt overhang;
    - underinvestment;
    - wasteful overinvestment.
12. Excess cash tidak otomatis baik jika governance lemah.
13. M&A dapat mereallocate real resources; value hanya tercipta jika combination menghasilkan synergy.
14. Negara memengaruhi resource allocation melalui taxation, regulation, capital controls, legal environment, dan intervention.
15. Capital-market segmentation dapat mengubah cost of capital dan lokasi investasi.
16. Weak property rights/country risk dapat menaikkan required return dan menurunkan project value.
17. Lower nominal foreign interest rate tidak otomatis membuat foreign funding economically cheaper.
18. Selalu pisahkan:
    - value creation;
    - value destruction;
    - wealth transfer.
