## Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Bayangkan ada dua kelompok besar:

```text
Pihak yang memiliki capital
        ↓
Investors / creditors
        ↓
FINANCIAL MARKETS
        ↓
Companies / projects / assets
        ↓
Penggunaan capital
```

Financial market memungkinkan capital dipindahkan dari pihak yang bersedia menyediakan dana menuju pihak yang memiliki investment opportunities.

Tetapi fungsinya tidak berhenti pada “mempertemukan pembeli dan penjual”. Dari referensi Topik 4, financial markets menjalankan beberapa fungsi penting:

1. **valuation** — securities dan firms memperoleh market value;
2. **financing access** — perusahaan yang memiliki akses pasar dapat menerbitkan securities untuk memperoleh dana;
3. **risk allocation** — debt dan equity membagi payoff dan risk secara berbeda;
4. **information aggregation** — market prices mencerminkan informasi dan ekspektasi investors;
5. **discipline and control** — poor management dapat menghadapi tekanan dari investors atau takeover market;
6. **international capital allocation** — capital dapat bergerak lintas currencies dan countries ketika markets terintegrasi;
7. **comparative financing advantage** — segmentation dapat menyebabkan cost of capital berbeda antar-market.

### Big Picture Topik 4.1

```text
INVESTORS
   ↓ provide capital
FINANCIAL MARKETS
   ↓ prices + financing terms
CORPORATIONS
   ↓ investment decisions
REAL PROJECTS / ASSETS
   ↓ generate cash flows
SECURITY HOLDERS
```

Dalam international setting:

```text
Domestic Market
      ↕
Exchange Rates / Capital Flows
      ↕
Foreign Market

Integrated?
→ same economic value regardless of currency/location benchmark

Segmented?
→ financing conditions and cost of capital can differ across markets
```

> [!TIP] Mental Model
> Untuk CF4, jangan melihat “financial market” hanya sebagai **lokasi perdagangan**.
>
> Pikirkan sebagai:
>
> **capital allocation + valuation + liquidity/access + information + control + international integration**.

## Section 2 — Definisi & Core Concepts

### Definisi Formal

> [!NOTE] Financial Market
> Dalam kerangka corporate finance, **financial market** adalah institutional/economic mechanism melalui mana claims terhadap future cash flows dinilai, dibiayai, dan dialihkan antar capital providers dan users of capital.

Referensi Topik 4 tidak memberikan satu definisi dictionary-style tunggal; definisi di atas merupakan sintesis langsung dari fungsi market yang digunakan sepanjang Brigham Bab 13 dan Berk & DeMarzo Bab 16, 18, 22, 28, 29, dan 31.

### Terminologi Penting

| Istilah | Makna dalam scope Topik 4 |
|---|---|
| **Capital market** | Market tempat financial claims memperoleh value dan firms/investors berinteraksi dalam financing dan investment decisions |
| **Perfect capital market** | Benchmark tanpa frictions yang relevan sehingga securities dapat diterbitkan/traded at fair value dan capital structure tertentu tidak menciptakan value hanya karena pembagian claims |
| **Market imperfection** | Friction seperti distress costs, information asymmetry, taxes, agency conflicts, financing constraints, atau market segmentation yang membuat real-world outcomes berbeda dari benchmark |
| **Market value** | Nilai yang diberikan market terhadap security atau firm berdasarkan expected future cash flows, risk, dan required return |
| **Intrinsic value** | Dalam Brigham, value berdasarkan expected future free cash flows discounted pada appropriate cost of capital |
| **Integrated capital markets** | International markets di mana valuation suatu foreign investment konsisten lintas currencies/locations setelah memperhitungkan exchange-rate relationships dan required returns |
| **Segmented capital markets** | Markets di mana barriers/frictions menyebabkan comparable risk memiliki financing cost/valuation berbeda across countries atau currencies |
| **Market for corporate control** | Mechanism di mana control atas firm dapat berpindah melalui takeover/acquisition, memberi external discipline kepada management |
| **Financial distress** | Kondisi ketika firm mengalami kesulitan memenuhi debt obligations; imperfections dan distress costs dapat memengaruhi value |
| **Information asymmetry** | Satu pihak memiliki informasi lebih baik daripada pihak lain sehingga pricing dan financing decisions dapat memberi signal |

### 2.1 Perfect Capital Market — Benchmark, Bukan Realitas

Berk & DeMarzo menggunakan **perfect capital market** sebagai benchmark untuk memahami apa yang benar-benar menciptakan atau menghancurkan value.

Dalam benchmark ini:

- securities dapat dibeli/dijual pada fair prices;
- firms dapat memperoleh financing ketika economic value assets cukup;
- tidak ada transaction/friction costs yang mengubah total pie;
- rearranging claims antara debt dan equity tidak otomatis mengubah total firm value.

Contoh Armin Industries pada Chapter 16 menunjukkan bahwa jika firm mempunyai asset value melebihi liabilities dan dapat access capital markets pada fair value, short-term lack of cash **tidak otomatis berarti default**. Firm dapat refinance atau issue equity.

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Lesson
> **Access to capital markets menghubungkan asset value dengan financing capacity.**
>
> Firm yang economically valuable dapat mengatasi temporary cash shortage apabila investors bersedia menyediakan capital dengan harga yang fair.

### 2.2 Market Imperfections — Mengapa Struktur Market Penting

Begitu assumptions perfect market dilepas, financial structure mulai memengaruhi real outcomes.

Referensi Topik 4 membahas beberapa imperfections:

- direct dan indirect costs of financial distress;
- information asymmetry;
- agency conflicts;
- managerial incentives;
- taxes;
- financing constraints;
- international market segmentation;
- differing legal/governance protections;
- takeover frictions.

Karena imperfections, dua firms dengan operating assets serupa bisa menghadapi:

- different financing costs;
- different access to capital;
- different market valuations;
- different governance pressure;
- different incentives.

---

### 2.3 Market Value, Intrinsic Value, dan Corporate Decisions

Brigham Bab 13 menghubungkan corporate value dengan expected future free cash flows:

\[
V_{\text{operations}}
=
\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}
\frac{FCF_t}{(1+WACC)^t}
\]

Konsep utama:

```text
Expected FCF ↑
→ intrinsic value ↑

Risk / WACC ↑
→ present value ↓
```

Financial markets menjadi tempat di mana investors membentuk harga berdasarkan expectations tersebut.

Tetapi:

> **market price dan intrinsic value tidak harus identik setiap saat.**

Perbedaan dapat timbul karena:

- new information;
- changing risk aversion;
- changing expected cash flows;
- information asymmetry;
- temporary mispricing.

Bagi management, tujuan yang ditekankan Brigham adalah membangun **long-term intrinsic value**, bukan memanipulasi short-term market price.

---

### 2.4 Access to Capital Markets

Akses ke market memengaruhi ability firm untuk:

- refinance debt;
- issue new securities;
- fund positive-NPV investments;
- survive temporary liquidity pressure;
- undertake acquisitions;
- invest internationally.

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 16 menekankan distinction penting:

> **cash-flow shortage ≠ economic insolvency secara otomatis.**

Jika market value assets masih cukup dan capital markets functioning, firm mungkin dapat memperoleh new financing.

Sebaliknya, ketika financial distress merusak trust:

```text
Distress
↓
Customers/suppliers/employees become cautious
↓
Cash flows deteriorate
↓
Financing becomes more difficult
↓
Firm value can fall further
```

Ini menunjukkan bahwa market access adalah bagian dari economic value, bukan sekadar convenience.

## Section 3 — How It Works

### 3.1 Financial Markets sebagai Valuation Mechanism

Corporate value berasal dari future cash flows dan required return.

Core relationship:

\[
\boxed{
Value
=
PV(\text{Expected Future Cash Flows})
}
\]

Untuk operating firm:

\[
\boxed{
V_{\text{operations}}
=
\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}
\frac{FCF_t}{(1+WACC)^t}
}
\]

Mechanism:

```text
Information about business
        ↓
Expected cash flows / risk updated
        ↓
Investors update required return
        ↓
Security prices change
        ↓
Firm financing conditions change
```

Market prices karena itu bukan hanya hasil akhir; price juga menjadi **feedback signal** kepada firm.

---

### 3.2 Financial Markets sebagai Financing Mechanism

Firm membutuhkan capital untuk real investment.

```text
Investment opportunity
      ↓
Need capital
      ↓
Issue debt/equity or obtain financing
      ↓
Investors supply funds
      ↓
Firm acquires/operates real assets
      ↓
Future cash flows
```

Jika market menilai project/future cash flows attractive relative to risk, capital dapat tersedia pada required return yang sesuai.

Jika risk tinggi atau information problems besar:

- required return meningkat;
- financing terms memburuk;
- available capital dapat turun;
- positive operating opportunities bahkan dapat sulit dibiayai.

---

### 3.3 Financial Distress dan Market Access

Dalam **perfect market**, firm dengan asset value cukup dapat refinance.

Misalkan:

- asset value = \$150 million;
- debt due = \$100 million;
- immediate cash insufficient.

Jika market percaya asset value memang \$150 million, firm dapat:

- issue new debt;
- issue new equity;
- refinance existing obligation.

Tetapi di real markets, distress menimbulkan frictions.

Potential indirect costs from Chapter 16:

- customers leave;
- suppliers tighten terms;
- employees leave;
- receivables become harder to collect;
- assets may be sold in fire sales;
- creditors incur costs;
- investment decisions can become distorted.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Economic distress** = decline in underlying asset/business value.  
> **Financial distress** = difficulty arising from financing obligations.
>
> Bankruptcy headlines tidak berarti bankruptcy process sendiri menyebabkan seluruh decline in value.

---

### 3.4 Market for Corporate Control

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 28 dan corporate-governance material menghubungkan financial markets dengan ownership/control.

Jika management performs poorly atau assets dapat digunakan more efficiently oleh another owner:

```text
Potential acquirer identifies value
        ↓
Takeover bid
        ↓
Target shareholders evaluate offer
        ↓
Control may transfer
        ↓
Assets/management strategy may change
```

Takeovers dapat menjadi external governance mechanism.

Acquirer umumnya harus membayar **acquisition premium** di atas premerger market price target.

Jika:

\[
P_{\text{offer}} > P_{\text{premerger}}
\]

maka acquisition premium:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Premium}
=
\frac{
P_{\text{offer}}-P_{\text{premerger}}
}{
P_{\text{premerger}}
}
}
\]

Economic interpretation:

- target shareholders perlu compensated untuk menyerahkan control;
- bidder berharap synergies/private benefits/strategic gains cukup besar;
- competition among bidders dapat transfer more merger gains kepada target shareholders.

> [!INFO] CF4 Connection
> Market for corporate control adalah bridge kuat dari [[4.1 Financial Markets Structure]] ke [[4.3 Agency Theory and Governance]].

## Section 4 — National vs International Financial Markets

### 4.1 Domestic Market Perspective

Pada domestic setting, firm dan investors beroperasi dalam:

- one primary currency framework;
- domestic legal/tax system;
- domestic capital-market access;
- domestic required-return environment.

Domestic security values tetap ditentukan oleh expected cash flows dan risk.

Namun international investment menambahkan dimensions baru:

- currency;
- differing risk-free rates;
- different tax systems;
- different legal/regulatory structures;
- possible capital-market segmentation.

---

### 4.2 Internationally Integrated Capital Markets

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 31 memberikan definisi core:

> Capital markets **internationally integrated** jika value foreign investment tidak bergantung pada currency—home atau foreign—yang dipakai untuk analysis.

Ini adalah **Law of One Price logic**.

Misalkan foreign project memberikan expected foreign-currency cash flow \(CF_C\).

Foreign-market valuation:

\[
PV_{\text{foreign}}
=
\frac{CF_C}{1+r_C^*}
\]

Jika spot exchange rate adalah \(S\) home currency per unit foreign currency, home investor dapat memandang value tersebut sebagai:

\[
S
\cdot
\frac{CF_C}{1+r_C^*}
\]

Alternatifnya, expected foreign cash flow dikonversi dengan forward rate \(F\), lalu didiscount dengan home-currency cost of capital:

\[
\frac{F\cdot CF_C}{1+r_H^*}
\]

Dalam integrated market benchmark, kedua approaches harus konsisten:

\[
\boxed{
S
\cdot
\frac{CF_C}{1+r_C^*}
=
\frac{F\cdot CF_C}{1+r_H^*}
}
\]

sehingga:

\[
\boxed{
F
=
S
\frac{1+r_H^*}{1+r_C^*}
}
\]

Dalam chapter, hubungan ini dikaitkan dengan covered-interest-parity logic dalam konteks valuation.

> [!IMPORTANT] Financial Meaning
> International integration berarti **currency choice tidak menciptakan free value**. Jika satu asset identik secara ekonomi, valuation routes yang berbeda harus menghasilkan value yang konsisten.

---

### 4.3 Two Valuation Routes in Integrated Markets

Jika relevant assumptions terpenuhi, foreign project dapat dinilai melalui dua routes.

#### Route A — Foreign Currency First

```text
Foreign cash flows
      ↓
Discount at foreign-currency cost of capital
      ↓
Foreign-currency NPV
      ↓
Convert at current spot rate
      ↓
Home-currency value
```

\[
NPV_H
=
S_0
\times
NPV_C
\]

#### Route B — Convert Cash Flows First

```text
Foreign cash flows
      ↓
Convert expected CF using forward rates
      ↓
Home-currency cash flows
      ↓
Discount at home-currency cost of capital
      ↓
Home-currency value
```

Jika markets integrated dan assumptions source terpenuhi:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Route A Value}
=
\text{Route B Value}
}
\]

> [!WARNING] Assumption
> Berk & DeMarzo menyatakan equivalence ini dalam framework di mana uncertainty in spot exchange rates tidak correlated dengan foreign-currency cash flows untuk simplification yang digunakan.

---

### 4.4 Internationally Segmented Capital Markets

Markets disebut **segmented** ketika frictions/barriers menyebabkan equivalent-risk capital memperoleh different pricing atau cost across markets setelah currency conversion dipertimbangkan.

Possible consequences in textbook framework:

- cost of capital berbeda antar-country/currency;
- local financing conditions dapat materially differ;
- firm dengan access ke multiple capital markets dapat memiliki comparative financing advantage;
- project valuation dapat depend on where/how financing and ownership occur.

Conceptually:

```text
Integrated markets
→ arbitrage/competition pushes equivalent risks toward consistent pricing

Segmented markets
→ barriers prevent full equalization
→ different required returns can persist
```

> [!DANGER] Definition Trap
> **Different nominal interest rates ≠ automatically segmented markets.**
>
> Rates dapat berbeda karena inflation/currency relationships. Segmentation berarti differences tetap ada **after economically comparable currency/risk adjustments**.

---

### 4.5 Why Firms May Use Foreign Capital Markets

Chapter 31 membuka dengan Starbucks Coffee Japan yang menggunakan Japanese capital markets untuk expansion.

Core lesson bukan company anecdote-nya, tetapi financing logic:

Foreign market access dapat relevan karena:

- project generates local-currency cash flows;
- local financing may better match project exposures;
- local investor base may value/understand project differently;
- market segmentation may produce different financing conditions;
- regulatory/tax structures may differ.

Namun:

> **foreign financing bukan otomatis cheaper in economic terms.**

Dalam integrated markets, apparent differences in rates harus konsisten dengan exchange-rate/currency relationships.

## Section 5 — Interpretation & Sanity Check

### Interpretation Ladder

#### Level 1 — Apa market-nya?

Tentukan konteks:

- domestic atau international?
- financing market atau control/takeover market?
- perfect-market benchmark atau imperfect market?
- integrated atau segmented?

#### Level 2 — Apa fungsi economic-nya?

Tanyakan:

- menyediakan capital?
- menghasilkan valuation?
- memindahkan ownership/control?
- menyebarkan risk?
- memberi price signal?
- memungkinkan refinancing?

#### Level 3 — Apa friction-nya?

Cari:

- distress costs;
- information asymmetry;
- agency conflict;
- tax;
- legal/regulatory barrier;
- currency risk;
- segmentation.

#### Level 4 — Apa dampaknya pada value?

Gunakan:

```text
Cash flow effect
+
Required return / financing cost effect
=
Value effect
```

#### Level 5 — Apakah hanya transfer atau value creation/destruction?

Ini sangat penting.

Contoh:

- debt holder memperoleh lebih, equity holder lebih sedikit → belum tentu total firm value berubah;
- bankruptcy transfer of control → transfer bukan otomatis economic loss;
- distress-related loss of customers → real value destruction;
- takeover premium → transfer of merger gains antara acquirer dan target owners, tidak otomatis menunjukkan total synergy amount.

---

### Sanity Checks

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check 1
> Jika dua valuation routes pada integrated international markets memberi materially different value padahal assumptions sama, ada kemungkinan:
>
> - exchange-rate convention salah;
> - discount rate salah currency;
> - forward/spot relationship salah;
> - inconsistent cash-flow currency.

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check 2
> Jika soal mengatakan firm tidak punya cash hari ini, jangan langsung simpulkan bankrupt.
>
> Pertanyaan pertama:
>
> **Apakah asset value masih melebihi claims dan apakah firm punya access to financing?**

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check 3
> Acquisition premium yang tinggi tidak otomatis berarti deal menciptakan value bagi acquirer.
>
> Acquirer hanya memperoleh value jika synergy/benefit yang diterima melebihi premium dan transaction-related costs.

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check 4
> Different interest rates antara two countries tidak otomatis menunjukkan arbitrage opportunity atau segmentation.

## Section 7 — Exam Traps & Misconceptions

### Definition Trap

> [!BUG] **Perfect market ≠ market with zero business risk.**
>
> Perfect market assumption menghilangkan relevant financial frictions, bukan uncertainty dari project cash flows.

> [!BUG] **Integrated market ≠ same nominal interest rate everywhere.**
>
> Currency and inflation differences dapat menghasilkan different nominal rates tanpa violating integration.

> [!BUG] **Financial distress ≠ economic distress.**
>
> Economic distress = underlying business/assets lose value.  
> Financial distress = financing obligations create difficulty.

> [!BUG] **Bankruptcy ≠ entire decline in firm value.**
>
> Dalam benchmark, decline in asset value dapat terjadi regardless of leverage; bankruptcy dapat menjadi consequence, bukan root cause.

---

### Financial Logic Trap

> [!BUG] **No cash today ≠ no value.**
>
> Valuable firm dengan market access dapat refinance.

> [!BUG] **Transfer between investors ≠ change in total firm value.**
>
> Selalu lihat **total value to all investors**.

> [!BUG] **Takeover premium ≠ automatic value creation.**
>
> Premium adalah amount paid above pre-deal market value; acquirer harus memperoleh sufficient synergies/benefits agar deal worthwhile.

> [!BUG] **Foreign market rate lower ≠ financing economically cheaper.**
>
> Exchange-rate relations dan currency risk/forward rates harus dipertimbangkan.

---

### Calculation Trap

#### Acquisition Premium

Salah:

\[
\frac{P_{\text{offer}}-P_{\text{pre}}}{P_{\text{offer}}}
\]

Benar:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Premium}
=
\frac{P_{\text{offer}}-P_{\text{pre}}}{P_{\text{pre}}}
}
\]

#### Currency Consistency

Jangan:

```text
Foreign-currency CF
discount with home-currency rate
without proper conversion/relationship
```

Gunakan satu consistent route.

#### Discounting

\[
PV=\frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t}
\]

Pastikan:

- cash flow currency;
- discount-rate currency;
- risk basis;

konsisten.

---

### Interpretation Trap

> [!BUG] Market price naik setelah announcement tidak membuktikan keputusan selalu menciptakan long-run value.

> [!BUG] Capital-market segmentation dapat memberi apparent financing advantage, tetapi benefit harus dievaluasi setelah all cash-flow, currency, tax, and risk effects.

> [!BUG] Lower WACC meningkatkan value hanya jika cash-flow assumptions dan risk classification tetap appropriate.

---

### Red Flags

| Keyword pada soal | Pikirkan |
|---|---|
| perfect capital market | benchmark tanpa relevant frictions |
| fair-value issuance | refinancing possible if asset value supports claims |
| cannot make payment | default risk; jangan langsung samakan dengan economic insolvency |
| indirect distress costs | customers, suppliers, employees, fire sales |
| takeover / tender / acquisition | market for corporate control |
| acquisition premium | offer price vs premerger market price |

| barriers to capital flows | segmentation |
| same asset, different valuation routes | Law of One Price consistency |
| management incentives | link ke agency/governance |

## Section 8 — Executive Summary

### Must Remember

1. Financial markets dalam Topik 4 harus dipahami sebagai **mechanism of valuation, capital allocation, financing access, information, and control**.
2. **Perfect capital market** adalah benchmark untuk memisahkan real economic effects dari mere transfer antar-security holders.
3. Firm yang kekurangan cash belum tentu insolvent jika asset value cukup dan firm dapat access capital markets.
4. Real markets memiliki imperfections: distress costs, information asymmetry, incentives, taxes, legal barriers, dan segmentation.
5. Brigham menghubungkan firm value dengan expected FCF dan WACC:

\[
V_{\text{operations}}
=
\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}
\frac{FCF_t}{(1+WACC)^t}
\]

6. Financial markets menyalurkan information ke price; price changes memengaruhi financing conditions dan memberi feedback kepada management.
7. **Market for corporate control** memungkinkan ownership/control berpindah melalui takeover dan dapat menjadi external governance mechanism.
8. Acquisition premium:

\[
\text{Premium}
=
\frac{P_{\text{offer}}-P_{\text{pre}}}{P_{\text{pre}}}
\]

9. International capital markets **integrated** jika equivalent foreign investment mempunyai consistent value regardless of currency used for analysis.
10. Dalam integrated-market benchmark:

\[
S
\frac{CF_C}{1+r_C^*}
=
\frac{F CF_C}{1+r_H^*}
\]

11. **Segmented markets** memungkinkan persistent differences in cost of capital/valuation because barriers prevent full pricing equalization.
12. Different nominal rates antar-country **tidak otomatis** berarti segmentation.
13. Foreign financing tidak otomatis murah hanya karena stated interest rate lebih rendah.
14. Selalu bedakan:
    - economic distress vs financial distress;
    - investor wealth transfer vs total value destruction;
    - market price vs intrinsic value;
    - integrated vs segmented markets.

| Concept | Core Meaning | What Changes? | Main Exam Trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perfect capital market | Frictionless benchmark | Claims can be rearranged without automatic value creation | Menganggap no risk |
| Imperfect market | Real-world frictions matter | Financing decisions can affect value | Menganggap all effects are taxes only |
| Market value | Price/value assigned by market | Changes with expectations/risk | Menyamakan selalu dengan intrinsic value |
| Intrinsic value | PV of expected economic cash flows | Changes with FCF and WACC | Fokus hanya short-term price |
| Integrated international markets | Consistent economic valuation across currencies | Currency route should not alter value | Same nominal rates required |
| Segmented markets | Barriers allow persistent pricing/capital-cost differences | Market access may create advantage | Any rate difference = segmentation |
| Market for corporate control | Takeovers transfer control | Governance/ownership can change | Premium = synergy |
| Financial distress | Financing obligations create difficulty | Can destroy value through frictions | Sama dengan economic distress |

## Section 10 — Exam Pattern Map

| Narasi / Clue | Keyword | Konsep | Langkah Mental | Jebakan | Shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Firm tidak punya cash untuk debt payment tetapi assets valuable | “can issue new securities” | Capital-market access | Compare asset value vs liabilities → financing access | Cash shortage = insolvency | **Value first, cash second** |
| Business value jatuh sebelum bankruptcy | “economic decline” | Economic vs financial distress | Pisahkan operating-value loss dari financing consequence | Bankruptcy caused all loss | **Cause vs consequence** |
| Supplier/customer meninggalkan distressed firm | “indirect cost” | Financial distress cost | Identify incremental effect on total firm value | Transfer antar-creditors | **External operating damage = real cost** |
| Buyer offers above premerger price | “premium” | Market for corporate control | Compute offer minus pre-price over pre-price | Denominator offer price | **Premium uses old price** |
| Management underperforms and takeover threat emerges | “hostile takeover” | Governance via control market | Poor performance → external bidder → possible control transfer | Pure financing event | **Control = governance** |
| Foreign project valued in two currencies | “same value” | International integration | Ensure consistent CF currency + discount rate | Mix currencies | **One currency per route** |
| Country A and B have different rates | “segmented?” | Integrated vs segmented | Ask whether differences remain after currency/risk relationships | Rate difference alone | **Difference ≠ segmentation** |
| Firm can finance cheaper in one market because barriers persist | “comparative advantage” | Market segmentation | Identify barriers + persistent capital-cost gap | Assume arbitrage instantly eliminates | **Barrier permits persistence** |
| FCF forecast rises | “intrinsic value” | Corporate valuation | Higher expected FCF → higher value ceteris paribus | Current accounting earnings only | **FCF drives value** |
| Required return rises | “WACC” | Valuation | Higher discount rate → lower PV | Higher WACC = higher value | **Discount rate up → value down** |
