# 📘 4.3 — Agency Theory and Governance

> [!ABSTRACT] Ringkasan Cepat
> **Topik:** Agency Theory and Governance  
> **Bobot Parent Topic:** 5–15%  
> **Exam Priority:** Lower  
> **Difficulty:** Concept-Intensive  
> **Primary Skill:** Concept + Interpretation  
> **Ref:** Brigham & Ehrhardt Bab 13; Berk & DeMarzo Bab 16.1–16.7, 18.1–18.5, 22.1–22.8, 28.1–28.6, 29.1–29.4, 31.
>
> Inti materi: **agency problem muncul ketika orang yang membuat keputusan tidak menanggung seluruh manfaat maupun seluruh biaya dari keputusan tersebut.** Dalam corporation, separation of ownership and control membuat shareholders menyerahkan keputusan kepada managers. Karena managers memiliki kepentingan sendiri, tindakan mereka dapat menyimpang dari kepentingan investors.
>
> **Corporate governance** adalah sistem controls, regulations, incentives, monitoring, dan rights yang digunakan untuk mengurangi konflik tersebut. Tidak ada satu governance mechanism yang selalu optimal: board oversight, compensation, managerial ownership, debt discipline, shareholder action, takeover threat, analysts, lenders, auditors, regulation, dan legal systems masing-masing memiliki benefits sekaligus costs.
>
> CF4 juga perlu memahami agency conflicts **shareholders vs creditors**, terutama **risk shifting**, **debt overhang**, dan distortions terhadap investment decisions. Jadi governance bukan hanya isu “manager jahat vs shareholder”; lebih luas, ini adalah persoalan **siapa yang memiliki decision rights, siapa yang menerima payoff, siapa yang menanggung risk, dan bagaimana incentives dibuat agar keputusan mendekati value-maximizing behavior**.

# Section 1 — Intuisi: Mengapa Agency Problem Ada?

Bayangkan pemilik perusahaan menjalankan bisnisnya sendiri.

```text
Owner
↓
makes decision
↓
receives benefit
↓
bears cost
```

Jika owner menggunakan uang perusahaan secara boros, ia sendiri yang kehilangan value.

Sekarang ubah menjadi large corporation:

```text
Thousands of shareholders
        ↓
elect board
        ↓
board hires managers
        ↓
managers control daily decisions
```

Managers mungkin:

- hanya memiliki sedikit saham;
- menerima salary dan perks;
- memiliki career concerns;
- menghindari risiko;
- ingin memperbesar ukuran firm;
- ingin mempertahankan jabatan;
- memiliki information lebih banyak daripada investors.

Maka decision-maker dan economic owner tidak lagi orang yang sama.

Di sinilah agency problem muncul.

---

## 1.1 Separation of Ownership and Control

Berk & DeMarzo menekankan bahwa separation of ownership and control justru merupakan salah satu alasan corporation sangat berhasil.

Keuntungannya:

- investors tidak harus menjalankan firm sendiri;
- ownership dapat tersebar;
- investors dapat diversify;
- professional managers dapat menjalankan operasi.

Tetapi konsekuensinya:

```text
Owners supply capital
        ↓
Managers control decisions
        ↓
Managers may pursue own interests
        ↓
Agency conflict
```

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Separation of ownership and control bukan murni kelemahan.**
>
> Separation memberi keuntungan besar berupa specialization dan diversification.
>
> Agency problem adalah **trade-off** dari struktur tersebut.

---

# Section 2 — Definisi Agency Theory

## 2.1 Principal dan Agent

Dalam agency relationship:

- **principal** memberi authority atau resources;
- **agent** membuat keputusan atau bertindak atas nama principal.

Dalam corporation klasik:

| Principal | Agent |
|---|---|
| Shareholders | Managers |
| Shareholders | Board of directors |
| Creditors | Firm / management |
| Investors | Financial intermediaries / agents tertentu |
| Citizens | Public officials — secara konseptual sesuai learning outcome silabus, tetapi detail government-agency model tidak dikembangkan secara formal dalam textbook Topik 4 |

### Core Problem

Agency conflict muncul ketika:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Agent's private objective}
\neq
\text{Principal's objective}
}
\]

dan agent tidak menanggung seluruh economic consequences dari tindakannya.

---

## 2.2 Agency Cost

Berk & DeMarzo menggunakan **agency costs** sebagai economic costs akibat conflict of interest.

Secara konseptual agency costs dapat muncul dari:

1. **value-destroying actions** oleh agent;
2. **monitoring costs** untuk mengawasi agent;
3. **incentive/compensation costs** untuk menyelaraskan kepentingan;
4. **constraints** yang mencegah agent mengambil tindakan tertentu tetapi juga dapat mengurangi flexibility.

Tidak semua governance cost buruk.

Jika governance menghabiskan Rp1 tetapi mencegah value destruction Rp5, governance menciptakan net benefit.

---

# Section 3 — Manager–Shareholder Agency Conflict

## 3.1 Mengapa Kepentingan Bisa Berbeda?

Shareholders dalam framework textbook menginginkan peningkatan **long-term intrinsic value**.

Managers dapat memiliki objectives lain:

- salary;
- job security;
- prestige;
- private benefits;
- lower personal risk;
- larger organization;
- easier workload;
- short-term performance appearance.

Karena itu:

```text
Shareholder objective
= maximize value

Manager objective
= value + personal utility
```

Jika personal utility memengaruhi keputusan secara material, agency cost dapat muncul.

---

## 3.2 Common Managerial Behaviors that Can Reduce Value

Brigham Chapter 13 menekankan beberapa bentuk behavior yang dapat merusak intrinsic value.

### 1. Excessive Perquisites

Contoh:

- luxurious offices;
- corporate jets;
- unnecessary benefits;
- company resources untuk private consumption.

Economic problem:

\[
\text{Private benefit to manager}
<
\text{Cost borne by shareholders}
\]

Manager menikmati benefit, shareholders menanggung sebagian besar cost.

---

### 2. Empire Building

Managers dapat ingin menjalankan perusahaan yang lebih besar karena:

- prestige;
- compensation;
- influence;
- organizational power.

Maka mereka dapat melakukan:

- unnecessary acquisitions;
- excessive expansion;
- negative-NPV projects.

> [!DANGER] Exam Trap
> **Growth ≠ value creation.**
>
> Jika expansion hanya memperbesar firm tetapi tidak meningkatkan intrinsic value, decision tersebut adalah agency problem.

---

### 3. Managerial Entrenchment

Manager disebut **entrenched** ketika probability untuk diganti rendah.

Akibatnya, disciplinary pressure melemah.

```text
Low removal threat
↓
less accountability
↓
more room for self-serving actions
```

Entrenchment dapat diperkuat oleh:

- captured board;
- voting power;
- staggered board;
- anti-takeover defenses;
- managerial ownership yang terlalu besar.

---

### 4. Excessive Risk Avoidance

Shareholders dapat diversify, tetapi managers biasanya memiliki:

- human capital tied to firm;
- career tied to firm;
- compensation tied to firm.

Karena itu managers dapat menjadi lebih risk-averse daripada diversified shareholders.

Mereka mungkin menolak positive-NPV risky project karena takut terhadap personal downside.

---

### 5. Short-Termism / Earnings Management

Manager dapat mencoba meningkatkan appearance jangka pendek:

- delaying bad news;
- managing earnings;
- cutting useful long-term expenditure;
- selecting actions that boost current metrics.

Brigham menekankan bahwa jika investors meragukan quality of information, mereka dapat menggunakan higher required return, sehingga intrinsic value turun.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Current stock price maximization ≠ intrinsic value maximization.**
>
> Governance harus mendorong long-term value, bukan sekadar short-term reported performance.

---

### 6. Insufficient Effort / Shirking

Jika manager tidak memperoleh full benefit dari additional effort, incentive untuk bekerja optimal dapat lebih rendah dibanding owner-manager.

Agency conflict dapat berbentuk sangat sederhana:

```text
Effort cost borne personally by manager
Benefit largely accrues to shareholders
↓
manager may exert too little effort
```

---

# Section 4 — Corporate Governance

## 4.1 Definisi

Brigham mendefinisikan **corporate governance** sebagai:

> set of laws, rules, and procedures yang memengaruhi operations perusahaan dan decisions yang dibuat oleh managers.

Berk & DeMarzo menekankan governance sebagai system of controls, regulations, and incentives yang menangani conflicts of interest.

### Feynman Version

Corporate governance adalah sistem yang menjawab:

- Siapa boleh mengambil keputusan?
- Siapa mengawasi?
- Siapa dapat memberhentikan manager?
- Bagaimana manager dibayar?
- Apa hak shareholders?
- Apa hak creditors?
- Bagaimana fraud dicegah?
- Siapa dapat mengambil alih control?
- Bagaimana regulation dan law memaksa accountability?

---

## 4.2 Carrots and Sticks

Brigham menyederhanakan governance menjadi dua kategori.

### Carrot — Incentives

Contoh:

- performance-based compensation;
- stock ownership;
- stock options;
- bonuses.

Tujuan:

\[
\text{Manager payoff}
\uparrow
\quad \text{when shareholder value} \uparrow
\]

---

### Stick — Threat of Discipline

Contoh:

- dismissal by board;
- shareholder pressure;
- proxy fight;
- hostile takeover;
- legal/regulatory penalties.

Tujuan:

```text
Poor decision / misconduct
↓
personal consequence for manager
```

> [!TIP] Exam Shortcut
> Governance mechanism hampir selalu bekerja melalui salah satu dari dua channels:
>
> **align payoff** atau **increase accountability**.

---

# Section 5 — Internal Governance Mechanisms

Brigham membagi internal governance provisions menjadi lima area:

1. board monitoring and discipline;
2. charter provisions and bylaws affecting takeovers;
3. compensation plans;
4. capital structure choices;
5. accounting control systems.

---

# Section 6 — Board of Directors

## 6.1 Role of the Board

Shareholders elect directors untuk bertindak sebagai agents mereka.

Board bertugas antara lain:

- appoint senior management;
- monitor management;
- evaluate performance;
- set executive compensation;
- replace managers bila diperlukan;
- approve major corporate decisions.

Ideal chain:

```text
Shareholders
↓ elect
Board
↓ monitors
CEO / senior managers
↓ run
Company
```

---

## 6.2 Board Capture

Berk & DeMarzo menjelaskan board dapat menjadi **captured** oleh CEO.

Artinya board kehilangan effective independence dan terlalu aligned dengan management.

Possible reasons:

- CEO influences nomination;
- directors depend on CEO;
- personal/professional ties;
- compensation/prestige from board seat;
- directors reluctant to challenge CEO.

Jika board captured:

```text
Board should monitor CEO
        ↓
but board depends on CEO
        ↓
monitoring weakens
        ↓
agency problem worsens
```

---

## 6.3 Inside vs Outside Directors

### Inside Director

Director yang juga employee/manager perusahaan.

Possible advantage:

- deep firm knowledge.

Possible weakness:

- may be less independent from CEO.

### Outside / Independent Director

Director yang bukan member of management.

Potential benefit:

- more independent oversight;
- less direct dependence on CEO.

Tetapi label “outside” saja tidak menjamin independence jika director memiliki strong personal/business ties dengan management.

---

## 6.4 Board Structure

Brigham membahas factors seperti:

- cumulative vs noncumulative voting;
- staggered/classified boards;
- insider vs outsider composition;
- CEO influence.

### Cumulative Voting

Shareholder dapat concentrate votes pada candidate tertentu.

Potential effect:

- minority/dissident shareholders lebih mudah memperoleh representation.

### Noncumulative Voting

Votes tidak dapat dikonsentrasikan dengan cara yang sama.

Management controlling majority dapat lebih mudah menguasai seluruh board.

### Staggered Board

Hanya sebagian directors dipilih setiap tahun.

Effect:

- harder to replace board quickly;
- can provide continuity;
- but can also reduce takeover/shareholder discipline.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> Governance provision dapat memiliki **benefit dan cost sekaligus**.
>
> Staggered board dapat memberi stability tetapi juga memperkuat entrenchment.

---

# Section 7 — Executive Compensation

## 7.1 Tujuan Compensation Design

Compensation digunakan untuk menghubungkan manager wealth dengan firm performance.

Common components:

- salary;
- bonus;
- shares;
- stock options;
- long-term incentive plans.

Jika compensation hanya fixed salary:

```text
Performance ↑
Manager payoff changes little
```

Incentive alignment relatif lemah.

Jika compensation performance-sensitive:

```text
Firm value ↑
Manager payoff ↑
```

Alignment meningkat.

---

## 7.2 Stock Ownership

Manager yang memiliki shares ikut menanggung gain/loss shareholders.

Benefit:

- stronger alignment.

Cost:

- manager bears more firm-specific risk;
- large ownership stake dapat membuat manager harder to remove.

Berk & DeMarzo menekankan hubungan managerial ownership dan governance **tidak monotonic**.

> [!DANGER] Exam Trap
> **More managerial ownership ≠ always better governance.**
>
> Ownership dapat meningkatkan alignment tetapi juga meningkatkan entrenchment.

---

## 7.3 Stock Options

Stock option memberi manager upside ketika stock price naik.

Potential benefit:

- strong incentive untuk meningkatkan stock value.

Potential problems:

- encourages excessive risk-taking;
- manager may focus on stock-price metric;
- options can reward broad market increases unrelated to manager effort;
- timing/manipulation issues dapat muncul;
- manager dapat benefit from volatility;
- option design dapat create incentives for reporting manipulation.

Brigham juga menyoroti historical problems seperti **backdating** dan compensation arrangements yang tidak selalu align dengan genuine performance.

---

## 7.4 Risk–Incentive Trade-Off

Berk & DeMarzo memberi principle penting:

Semakin compensation manager sensitive terhadap firm performance:

\[
\text{Alignment} \uparrow
\]

tetapi juga:

\[
\text{Manager risk exposure} \uparrow
\]

Karena manager tidak fully diversified atas human capital dan job risk, terlalu banyak performance sensitivity juga costly.

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Trade-Off
> Governance bukan sekadar membuat manager menanggung 100% risk.
>
> Sistem optimal harus menyeimbangkan:
>
> **incentive alignment vs risk burden**.

---

# Section 8 — Shareholder Monitoring and Direct Action

## 8.1 Shareholder Voice

Shareholders dapat mengajukan resolutions pada annual meeting.

Potential proposals:

- remove anti-takeover provision;
- change compensation policy;
- alter board structure;
- improve governance practices.

Resolusi tidak selalu memperoleh majority support, tetapi dapat memberi reputational dan political pressure kepada board.

---

## 8.2 Proxy Contest / Proxy Fight

Jika shareholders tidak puas dengan board:

```text
Dissident shareholders
↓
seek proxy votes
↓
support alternative director slate
↓
attempt to replace board
```

Proxy contest adalah mechanism untuk mengubah control tanpa membeli seluruh firm.

---

## 8.3 Say-on-Pay

Berk & DeMarzo membahas advisory shareholder voting atas executive compensation.

Tujuan:

- memberikan shareholder voice;
- pressure board compensation committee;
- improve accountability.

Namun advisory vote tidak selalu equivalent dengan direct legal control atas compensation.

---

## 8.4 Blockholders

**Blockholder** adalah shareholder yang memegang large ownership stake.

Benefit:

- monitoring lebih worthwhile;
- incentive untuk collect information dan intervene lebih kuat.

Small diversified shareholder menghadapi free-rider problem:

```text
Monitoring cost borne individually
Benefits shared by all shareholders
↓
small investors may not monitor
```

Large blockholder memiliki lebih besar economic stake sehingga monitoring lebih rational.

Potential cost:

- large shareholder dapat memiliki private interests sendiri;
- minority shareholders dapat dirugikan jika controlling owner expropriates value.

---

# Section 9 — External Monitoring

Brigham dan Berk & DeMarzo menunjukkan governance tidak hanya datang dari board.

## 9.1 Security Analysts

Analysts:

- evaluate company information;
- compare performance;
- question management;
- disseminate assessments.

Potential governance role:

```text
Better external scrutiny
↓
harder to hide poor performance
↓
management discipline ↑
```

---

## 9.2 Lenders and Creditors

Creditors memiliki economic interest agar borrower:

- tidak mengambil excessive risk;
- menjaga asset value;
- memenuhi covenants;
- maintain financial discipline.

Mereka dapat monitor melalui:

- covenants;
- reporting requirements;
- restrictions;
- pricing/refinancing terms.

Ini adalah governance dari **creditor perspective**, bukan shareholder perspective.

---

## 9.3 Auditors and Accounting Controls

Reliable accounting systems mengurangi ability management untuk menyembunyikan actions.

Governance requires:

- internal controls;
- credible financial reporting;
- auditor independence;
- reliable measurement.

Accounting control systems merupakan salah satu lima internal governance areas dalam Brigham.

---

## 9.4 Whistle-Blowers

Whistle-blower adalah insider yang mengungkap misconduct atau fraud.

Governance role:

- information that outsiders cannot observe becomes available;
- probability of detection rises;
- expected cost of misconduct rises.

---

## 9.5 Media, Product-Market Competition, Litigation

Brigham juga menyebut external environmental governance factors:

- regulatory environment;
- block ownership;
- product-market competition;
- media;
- litigation.

Competition dapat discipline management karena poorly run firms lose customers/profits.

Media dan litigation dapat expose misconduct.

Namun media attention sendiri tidak selalu beneficial; Brigham mencatat “superstar CEO” effect dapat justru contribute to excessive compensation atau distraction.

---

# Section 10 — Capital Structure as Governance

Debt bukan hanya financing source.

Debt juga mengubah managerial incentives.

## 10.1 Debt as Discipline

Required debt payments mengurangi cash yang bebas digunakan management.

Ini dapat mengurangi:

- excessive perquisites;
- empire building;
- wasteful investments.

Mechanism:

```text
More debt
↓
more mandatory cash payments
↓
less discretionary free cash
↓
less room for waste
```

---

## 10.2 But Debt Creates New Agency Conflicts

Semakin leverage tinggi, conflicts antara shareholders dan creditors menjadi lebih penting.

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 16 menekankan beberapa distortions.

### A. Risk Shifting / Asset Substitution

Shareholders dapat prefer high-risk project karena limited liability.

Contoh logic:

```text
If project succeeds:
equity gets much of upside

If project fails:
creditors absorb much of downside
```

Maka equity dapat memilih project yang:

- higher risk;
- bahkan lower total NPV;

jika redistribution dari creditors ke shareholders cukup besar.

> [!DANGER] Exam Trap
> **Good for equity ≠ good for firm.**

---

### B. Debt Overhang / Underinvestment

Firm dapat menolak positive-NPV project karena sebagian besar gain memperbaiki position creditors.

```text
New investment requires equity funding
↓
project increases total firm value
↓
much benefit accrues to debt holders
↓
equity gain too small
↓
shareholders reject project
```

Result:

\[
\boxed{\text{Positive-NPV project forgone}}
\]

---

### C. Cashing Out / Excess Payout

Distressed shareholders dapat memiliki incentive untuk distribute cash rather than preserve asset value for creditors.

Ini adalah bentuk wealth transfer.

---

## 10.3 Agency Costs of Debt

Agency costs of debt adalah value losses dan contracting/monitoring costs yang muncul karena conflicts antara debt holders dan equity holders.

Possible responses:

- debt covenants;
- collateral;
- restrictions on additional borrowing;
- restrictions on payouts;
- monitoring;
- shorter maturity;
- pricing higher interest rates.

---

# Section 11 — Market for Corporate Control

Jika internal governance gagal, external buyer dapat mencoba mengambil control.

## 11.1 Hostile Takeover as Discipline

Logic:

```text
Poor management
↓
firm value below potential
↓
outside bidder sees opportunity
↓
takeover attempt
↓
management may be replaced
```

Karena itu takeover threat adalah governance “stick”.

---

## 11.2 Friendly vs Hostile Takeover

### Friendly Takeover

Target board supports transaction.

### Hostile Takeover

Target board opposes transaction, sehingga bidder harus appeal directly kepada shareholders.

Methods dapat melibatkan:

- tender offer;
- proxy fight.

---

## 11.3 Takeover Defenses

Berk & DeMarzo Chapter 28 membahas:

- poison pill;
- staggered board;
- white knight;
- costly management replacement;
- capital-structure changes.

### Poison Pill

Rights diberikan kepada target shareholders—kecuali acquirer—untuk membeli shares dengan deep discount ketika trigger terjadi.

Effect:

- dilutes acquirer;
- makes takeover expensive.

Potential benefit:

- strengthens target bargaining power;
- may help negotiate higher takeover premium.

Potential cost:

- management becomes harder to replace;
- entrenchment can increase.

> [!WARNING] Key Governance Trade-Off
> **Takeover defense ≠ automatically bad.**
>
> Defense dapat:
>
> - protect bargaining power,
>
> tetapi juga:
>
> - shield ineffective managers.

---

# Section 12 — Regulation and Legal Systems

## 12.1 Why Regulation Exists

Corporate governance cannot rely only on voluntary internal mechanisms because:

- managers control information;
- investors are dispersed;
- fraud can be difficult to detect;
- auditors may face conflicts;
- insiders may exploit informational advantage.

Regulation attempts to establish minimum standards.

---

## 12.2 Regulation Has Costs

Berk & DeMarzo emphasizes an essential trade-off:

```text
More regulation
↓
potentially less fraud / stronger protection

but

More regulation
↓
compliance cost / reduced flexibility
```

Thus:

> **Optimal governance is not maximum regulation.**

The goal is a balance where benefits from reduced agency problems exceed implementation costs.

---

## 12.3 Auditor Independence

Governance reforms often emphasize independent auditing because auditor conflicts can weaken monitoring.

If auditor depends economically on management:

```text
Auditor independence ↓
↓
reporting credibility ↓
↓
agency problem ↑
```

Therefore rules may address:

- audit committee independence;
- non-audit services;
- auditor rotation;
- internal-control requirements.

---

## 12.4 Insider Trading

Berk & DeMarzo discusses insider-trading regulation as another governance mechanism.

Economic tension:

- insider trading can incorporate information into prices;
- but unrestricted insider trading can allow insiders to profit at expense of uninformed investors and weaken fairness/confidence.

CF4 focus should be conceptual:

> regulation attempts to limit exploitation of private information while recognizing that restrictions also have costs.

---

# Section 13 — Corporate Governance Around the World

Berk & DeMarzo emphasizes governance systems differ by country.

Differences can involve:

- shareholder legal protection;
- board structure;
- employee participation;
- concentrated vs dispersed ownership;
- cross-holdings;
- dual-class shares;
- family control;
- pyramidal structures.

---

## 13.1 Pyramidal Control

A controlling family can control a chain of companies through layered ownership.

Simplified:

```text
Family
↓ controls 51% of Company A
Company A
↓ controls 51% of Company B
Company B
↓ controls 51% of Company C
```

Effective cash-flow ownership in C can be much smaller than effective control.

If each layer is 51%:

\[
0.51^3 \approx 13.3\%
\]

Yet control can remain substantial.

Potential agency problem:

- controller has strong control rights;
- but relatively low cash-flow exposure;
- incentive for **tunneling** or extraction of private benefits increases.

---

## 13.2 Dual-Class Shares

Different share classes may have different voting rights.

A founder/family may retain control while holding relatively smaller economic claim.

Potential benefit:

- protects long-term vision from short-term market pressure.

Potential cost:

- weakens outside shareholder discipline;
- increases risk of entrenchment/expropriation.

---

## 13.3 Stakeholders Beyond Shareholders

Governance around the world may formally include:

- employees;
- banks;
- other corporations.

For example:

- employee board representation;
- bank representatives;
- cross-shareholdings.

This means governance structures can differ because legal systems assign decision rights differently.

# Section 14 — Government and Public Decision-Making

Silabus CF4 explicitly states that individual/group interests can influence decisions in **government as well as private companies**.

The Topik 4 textbook sources primarily develop agency theory in corporate settings, not a complete public-choice framework.

However, the general agency logic supported by the syllabus is:

```text
Public / citizens
      ↓ delegate authority
Government officials / institutions
      ↓ make decisions
Potential divergence of interests
      ↓
Need for accountability, rules, oversight
```

> [!NOTE] Source Boundary
> The supplied official textbook chapters do **not** provide a full formal treatment of government agency theory or public-choice economics.
>
> Therefore, for CF4:
>
> - understand that self/group interest can distort public decisions;
> - transfer the principal–agent intuition carefully;
> - do not add unsupported detailed theories of electoral incentives, bureaucracy, lobbying, or rent-seeking unless such concepts appear in a given exam question/source.

# Section 15 — Duties, Accountability, and Conflicts of Interest

## 15.1 Fiduciary Logic

Brigham stresses that managers are hired to act in shareholders' interests.

If management deliberately misleads investors for personal benefit, this violates that role.

In mergers, Berk & DeMarzo also discusses heightened director duties when control changes.

CF4 takeaway:

> Decision-makers who control other people's capital have obligations that limit pure self-interest.

---

## 15.2 Conflict of Interest

A **conflict of interest** exists when a decision-maker's personal incentives can interfere with objective action on behalf of the principal.

Examples:

- CEO influences board nominees who later evaluate CEO;
- auditor earns significant non-audit fees from audit client;
- manager evaluates acquisition that increases own prestige/pay;
- controlling shareholder arranges related-party transaction benefiting itself;
- equity holders choose risk that harms creditors.

---

## 15.3 Prevention / Mitigation

Use this mental map:

```text
Conflict of interest
      ↓
Can incentives be aligned?
      ├─ compensation
      ├─ ownership
      └─ contractual design

Can behavior be monitored?
      ├─ board
      ├─ analysts
      ├─ lenders
      ├─ auditors
      └─ internal controls

Can decision-maker be replaced?
      ├─ board dismissal
      ├─ proxy contest
      └─ takeover

Can misconduct be punished?
      ├─ regulation
      ├─ litigation
      └─ legal penalties
```

# Section 18 — Exam Traps & Misconceptions

## Definition Traps

> [!BUG] **Agency problem ≠ fraud only.**
>
> Agency problem also includes legal but value-reducing behavior such as shirking, empire building, excess perks, or excessive risk avoidance.

> [!BUG] **Corporate governance ≠ board of directors only.**
>
> Governance includes board, compensation, ownership, capital structure, controls, shareholders, creditors, analysts, regulation, litigation, takeover market, and other mechanisms.

> [!BUG] **Outside director ≠ automatically independent.**
>
> Personal/business relationships can weaken independence.

---

## Incentive Traps

> [!BUG] **More stock/options ≠ always better.**
>
> Incentives can increase risk-taking, manipulation, or entrenchment.

> [!BUG] **More debt ≠ always better discipline.**
>
> Debt can reduce waste but also create distress and debt-equity conflicts.

> [!BUG] **Managerial ownership ↑ ≠ firm value must ↑.**
>
> Alignment and entrenchment move in opposite directions.

---

## Takeover Traps

> [!BUG] **Poison pill ≠ purely shareholder-destroying.**
>
> It can increase bargaining power and premium, but also entrench management.

> [!BUG] **Hostile takeover ≠ automatically bad.**
>
> It can replace poor management and discipline firms.

> [!BUG] **Staggered board ≠ purely good or bad.**
>
> Stability vs reduced replacement pressure.

---

## Stakeholder Trap

> [!BUG] **Shareholder–manager conflict is not the only agency conflict.**
>
> Also remember:
>
> - shareholders vs creditors;
> - controlling vs minority shareholders;
> - management vs broader investors.

---

## Regulation Trap

> [!BUG] **More regulation ≠ automatically better governance.**
>
> Governance must balance benefits against compliance and flexibility costs.

# Section 20 — Mini Scenarios

## Scenario 1 — CEO Wants a Large Acquisition

A CEO wants to acquire another company mainly because larger firms confer more prestige and pay.

### Diagnosis

```text
Decision-maker: CEO
Private benefit: prestige + compensation
Cost borne by: shareholders
```

Agency problem:

> **manager–shareholder conflict / empire building**

Governance response:

- independent board review;
- compensation design;
- shareholder vote if required;
- market discipline.

---

## Scenario 2 — Distressed Firm Chooses Riskier Project

Debt is large. Equity receives little if safe project succeeds moderately, but much more if risky project succeeds.

### Diagnosis

> **risk shifting / asset substitution**

Potential winner:

- shareholders.

Potential loser:

- creditors.

Total value may fall if risky project has lower NPV.

---

## Scenario 3 — Good Project Rejected

A positive-NPV project requires new equity funding, but most gain would increase value of existing debt.

### Diagnosis

> **debt overhang / underinvestment**

Important:

> Shareholders may rationally reject a project that is good for the firm as a whole.

---

## Scenario 4 — Manager Owns More Shares

Initial effect:

- stronger alignment.

But if ownership becomes large:

- voting power increases;
- removal becomes harder;
- entrenchment may rise.

Conclusion:

> **non-monotonic governance effect.**

---

## Scenario 5 — Poison Pill

Board adopts poison pill.

Do not immediately answer “bad”.

Ask:

1. Does it protect bargaining position and increase premium?
2. Or does it mainly protect management from replacement?

Governance interpretation depends on motive and outcome.

# Section 22 — Executive Summary

### Must Remember

1. **Agency relationship** terjadi ketika principal mendelegasikan authority kepada agent.
2. **Agency conflict** muncul ketika interests principal dan agent tidak sepenuhnya aligned.
3. Corporate form memperoleh benefit besar dari separation of ownership and control, tetapi separation ini menciptakan agency problem.
4. Agency problem tidak hanya fraud; perks, shirking, empire building, excessive risk aversion, short-termism, dan entrenchment juga termasuk.
5. **Corporate governance** adalah system of controls, rules, incentives, monitoring, and accountability untuk mengurangi conflicts of interest.
6. Brigham merangkum governance melalui:
   - **carrot** = compensation/incentives;
   - **stick** = removal threat.
7. Lima internal governance areas Brigham:
   1. board monitoring;
   2. charter/bylaw provisions;
   3. compensation;
   4. capital structure;
   5. accounting controls.
8. Board adalah agent shareholders, tetapi board dapat captured oleh CEO.
9. Outside directors biasanya meningkatkan independence, tetapi label outside tidak menjamin genuine independence.
10. Managerial ownership dapat improve alignment tetapi juga increase entrenchment.
11. Stock options meningkatkan performance sensitivity tetapi dapat mendorong excessive risk atau manipulation.
12. Debt dapat discipline management dengan mengurangi free cash flow.
13. Debt juga menciptakan shareholder–creditor conflicts:
   - risk shifting;
   - debt overhang;
   - underinvestment.
14. Creditors menggunakan covenants dan monitoring untuk mengurangi agency costs of debt.
15. Shareholders dapat bertindak melalui resolutions, proxy contests, voting, dan activism.
16. Blockholders memiliki stronger monitoring incentives, tetapi controlling shareholders juga dapat exploit minorities.
17. Analysts, lenders, auditors, whistle-blowers, media, competition, litigation, dan regulators adalah external governance mechanisms.
18. Hostile takeover dapat menjadi external discipline terhadap poor management.
19. Poison pills dan staggered boards dapat meningkatkan bargaining power/stability tetapi juga entrench management.
20. Regulation memiliki trade-off antara fraud/investor protection dan compliance/flexibility costs.
21. Governance structures berbeda antar-country karena legal protection, ownership concentration, employee participation, cross-holdings, dual-class shares, dan pyramidal control.
22. **Good governance is a trade-off system—not the maximum possible amount of monitoring, ownership, debt, takeover pressure, or regulation.**
