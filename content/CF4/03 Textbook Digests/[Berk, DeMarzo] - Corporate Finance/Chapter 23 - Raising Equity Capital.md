# Berk & DeMarzo — Chapter 23: Raising Equity Capital

> [!ABSTRACT] Chapter in One View
> Chapter 23 mengikuti **life cycle equity financing** perusahaan: founder financing → **angel investors** → **venture capital (VC)** / strategic investors → private financing rounds → **initial public offering (IPO)** → **seasoned equity offering (SEO)**.
>
> Private firms menghadapi illiquidity dan information asymmetry. Angels dan VC menyediakan capital sekaligus monitoring, expertise, contacts, dan governance. VC funds biasanya dikelola general partners menggunakan committed capital dari limited partners dan memperoleh management fee serta **carried interest**.
>
> Young private companies sering menerbitkan **convertible preferred stock** kepada outside investors. Instrument ini memberi downside protection melalui priority terhadap common stock, tetapi tetap memberi upside melalui conversion menjadi common bila firm berhasil.
>
> Valuation setiap funding round dapat dilihat melalui **pre-money** dan **post-money valuation**:
>
> $$
> V_{post}=V_{pre}+\text{New Capital}
> $$
>
> Going public memberi liquidity dan access to much larger capital pools, tetapi ownership menjadi lebih dispersed dan firm menanggung disclosure, compliance, governance, serta flotation costs.
>
> IPO dapat terdiri dari **primary shares**—new shares yang membawa proceeds ke company—dan **secondary shares**—existing shares yang dijual current owners. Underwriter dapat menggunakan **best efforts**, **firm commitment**, atau auction mechanism.
>
> IPO process mencakup registration/prospectus, due diligence, road show, book-building, final pricing, allocation, underwriting spread, **greenshoe provision**, dan insider **lockup**.
>
> Chapter membahas IPO puzzles: **underpricing**, **hot issue markets**, high issuance costs, dan **long-run underperformance**. Underpricing memberi first-day gains kepada allocated investors tetapi meninggalkan value di meja bagi existing owners. **Winner's curse** menjelaskan mengapa average first-day IPO return bukan free lunch: attractive IPOs heavily rationed, sedangkan poor deals cenderung fully allocated.
>
> Setelah public, firm dapat issue additional equity melalui **SEO**, baik sebagai **cash offer** maupun **rights offer**. Rights offering melindungi existing shareholders dari wealth transfer akibat issue price yang rendah karena rights sendiri mempunyai value.
>
> SEO announcements sering diikuti price decline dalam evidence textbook, konsisten dengan **adverse selection**: market dapat menafsirkan keputusan management menjual equity sebagai signal bahwa stock tidak undervalued, bahkan mungkin overvalued.
>
> Untuk CF4, Chapter 23 adalah source yang sangat langsung bagi [[2.5 Capital Raising Methods]] dan penting untuk [[2.1 Equity Instruments]].

## 1. Why This Chapter Exists

Perusahaan tidak langsung lahir sebagai public corporation. Financing berkembang sesuai maturity perusahaan:

```text
Founder capital
      ↓
Angel / private investors
      ↓
Venture capital / strategic investors
      ↓
Private financing rounds
      ↓
IPO
      ↓
Public equity markets
      ↓
SEO / rights offering
```

Setiap tahap memecahkan problem berbeda. Early-stage firm sangat risky dan opaque sehingga public investors sulit menilai. Ketika firm berkembang, capital need meningkat dan early investors mulai membutuhkan liquidity. IPO membuka public market. Setelah public, company dapat kembali raise equity melalui seasoned offerings.

## 3. 23.1 Equity Financing for Private Companies

### Angel Investors

**Angel investors** adalah individual investors yang menanamkan personal capital pada young private firms. Selain money, mereka dapat memberi industry knowledge, contacts, managerial advice, dan credibility untuk subsequent rounds.

Concentrated ownership membuat direct monitoring jauh lebih feasible daripada pada dispersed public ownership.

### Venture Capital Firms

**Venture capital firm** berinvestasi pada young private companies menggunakan capital dari outside investors.

Typical structure:

```text
Limited Partners
      ↓ committed capital
VC Fund
      ↓ managed by
General Partner
      ↓
Portfolio Companies
      ↓
Monitoring + board involvement + advice
      ↓
Exit via acquisition or IPO
```

VC bukan passive stock investor. General partners memilih investments, negotiate terms, monitor management, dan sering mengambil board seats.

### VC Compensation

Dua komponen utama:

- **management fee** atas committed capital;
- **carried interest** = share of fund profits yang diterima VC manager.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> Gross return portfolio tidak sama dengan net return limited partners karena fees dan carried interest mengurangi investor proceeds.

### Corporate / Strategic Investors

Established corporations juga dapat membeli equity young private firms. Selain financial return, mereka dapat mengejar strategic objectives seperti technology access, collaboration, supply relationships, atau future acquisition opportunities.

Textbook memberi contoh Daimler investing in Tesla sebagai strategic collaboration.

### Convertible Preferred Stock

Ketika young company pertama kali menjual equity kepada outside investors, instrument yang umum adalah **convertible preferred stock**.

Young-company preferred biasanya:

- senior terhadap common stock dalam downside/liquidation;
- tidak selalu membayar regular cash dividend;
- dapat dikonversi menjadi common stock.

Economic logic:

```text
Bad outcome
→ investor wants seniority

Good outcome
→ investor wants common upside

Convertible preferred
→ combines both
```

Ini berbeda dari mature-company preferred stock yang textbook gambarkan dapat memiliki preferential dividend, liquidation seniority, dan special voting rights.

### Financing Rounds

Private firms sering raise capital sequentially: Series A, B, C, D, dan seterusnya. Jika prospects membaik, later investors dapat bersedia membayar higher price per share. RealNetworks dipakai textbook untuk menunjukkan progressive funding rounds dan meningkatnya amount of capital raised as firm matured.

### Pre-Money and Post-Money Valuation

Jika sebelum round ada \(N_{old}\) shares dan new round price adalah \(P\):

\[
\boxed{V_{pre}=N_{old}P}
\]

Jika firm menerbitkan \(N_{new}\) shares:

\[
\text{New Capital}=N_{new}P
\]

Post-money valuation:

\[
\boxed{V_{post}=V_{pre}+N_{new}P}
\]

atau:

\[
\boxed{V_{post}=(N_{old}+N_{new})P}
\]

New investor ownership:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Ownership}_{new}
=
\frac{N_{new}}{N_{old}+N_{new}}
}
\]

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Ownership dilution ≠ automatic wealth destruction.** Existing investors memiliki percentage yang lebih kecil, tetapi company menerima cash sehingga post-money pie lebih besar.

### Exit Strategies

Private-company stock biasanya illiquid. Investors merealisasikan return terutama melalui:

1. **acquisition / merger**;
2. **IPO**.

Acquisition memungkinkan buyer membeli outstanding stock sehingga investors cash out. IPO menciptakan publicly tradable stock dan liquidity.

## 4. 23.2 The Initial Public Offering

### Definition

An **initial public offering (IPO)** adalah first sale of stock by a private company to public investors.

### Advantages of Going Public

Textbook menekankan dua core benefits:

1. **greater liquidity** — early investors dapat diversify dan eventually sell;
2. **better access to capital** — public markets menyediakan much larger pools of funding dan memungkinkan subsequent offerings.

Public market price juga membuat stock lebih useful untuk employee compensation dan acquisition currency.

### Disadvantages

Going public juga:

- disperses ownership;
- can weaken direct shareholder monitoring;
- requires more disclosure;
- adds compliance/governance costs;
- reduces privacy.

`[TEXTBOOK CONTEXT]` Specific U.S. regulatory references dalam chapter dibaca sebagai institutional context, bukan current-law summary.

## 5. Primary vs Secondary Offering

### Primary Offering

**Primary shares** adalah new shares issued by company.

```text
Investor cash
→ Company
```

Firm memperoleh new capital.

### Secondary Offering

**Secondary shares** adalah existing shares sold by current shareholders.

```text
Investor cash
→ Selling shareholder
```

Company tidak memperoleh new funds dari portion ini.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> IPO dapat berisi campuran primary dan secondary shares. Jangan menganggap seluruh gross proceeds otomatis masuk ke issuer.

## 6. Underwriting Methods

### Best-Efforts IPO

Underwriter hanya berusaha menjual shares dan tidak menjamin entire issue terjual. Placement risk lebih banyak berada pada issuer. Beberapa offerings memiliki all-or-none clause.

### Firm Commitment IPO

Underwriter membeli issue dari issuer lalu resells ke public.

```text
Issuer
→ Underwriter
→ Public Investors
```

Issuer mendapat greater certainty of proceeds, tetapi underwriter menanggung lebih banyak distribution/market risk.

Large deals dapat memakai underwriting syndicate.

### Auction IPO

Investors submit bids specifying desired quantity and price. Offering price ditentukan dari aggregate demand sesuai auction mechanism. Textbook memperlakukannya sebagai alternative to traditional book-building.

## 7. IPO Process

Textbook's basic sequence:

```text
Select underwriter
      ↓
Due diligence + registration
      ↓
Prospectus
      ↓
Road show
      ↓
Book-building
      ↓
Set final offer price
      ↓
Allocate shares
      ↓
Public trading begins
```

### Prospectus

Offering disclosure menjelaskan business, management, financial condition, risks, use of proceeds, dan offering terms.

### Road Show

Management dan underwriter present company kepada prospective investors untuk:

- market the issue;
- answer questions;
- gauge demand.

### Book-Building

Underwriter collects indications of investor demand, desired quantities, dan price sensitivity. Information tersebut membantu pricing dan allocation.

### Underwriting Spread

Investors membayar offer price tetapi issuer menerima less than full amount karena underwriting fee/spread.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Spread per Share}
=
P_{offer}-P_{issuer}
}
\]

Percentage:

\[
\boxed{
s=
\frac{P_{offer}-P_{issuer}}{P_{offer}}
}
\]

Total underwriting fee:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Fee}
=
N P_{offer}s
}
\]

### Greenshoe Provision

**Greenshoe** memberi underwriter option untuk sell additional shares beyond initial offering size.

Economic uses:

- accommodate strong demand;
- support post-issue stabilization.

If demand strong, extra shares can be exercised. If demand weak, underwriter can cover short position by buying shares in market.

### Lockup

Insiders sering sepakat tidak menjual shares selama specified post-IPO period. Tujuannya menghindari immediate insider selling pressure dan reassure new investors.

## 8. 23.3 IPO Puzzles

### IPO Underpricing

IPO is **underpriced** if first trading value exceeds offer price.

\[
\boxed{
R_{initial}
=
\frac{P_1-P_0}{P_0}
}
\]

Example:

\[
P_0=20,\quad P_1=25
\]

\[
R_{initial}=25\%
\]

Allocated investors gain, tetapi existing owners could have raised more money at the higher value.

Conceptually:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Money Left on Table}
=
N(P_1-P_0)
}
\]

for the relevant underpriced shares.

Thus underpricing is an **indirect flotation cost**.

### Winner's Curse

Why not simply buy every IPO?

Because good IPOs are oversubscribed and rationed, while weak offerings are easier to obtain.

Textbook Example 23.4:

- 80% deals oversubscribed 16:1 and rise 20%;
- 20% deals not oversubscribed and fall 5%.

Average first-day return:

\[
0.8(20\%)+0.2(-5\%)=15\%
\]

But investor requesting 2,000 shares receives only:

\[
2000/16=125
\]

shares in successful deals, while may receive all requested shares in poor deals.

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Lesson
> Average IPO underpricing is not a freely attainable investor return because **allocation depends on demand and deal quality**.

### Hot Issue Markets

IPO activity and first-day returns cluster over time. Periods with high issuance and strong initial returns are called **hot issue markets**. Detailed historical cycles are compressed; textbook lesson is that IPO conditions vary substantially through time.

### Costs of IPOs

Going public involves:

```text
Underwriting spread
+ legal/accounting/registration costs
+ management time
+ underpricing
```

Fixed costs can make small IPOs especially expensive in percentage terms.

### Long-Run Underperformance

The textbook reviews evidence that IPO issuers historically underperformed broad benchmarks over later years in some samples.

Interpretation is not uniquely resolved. Possible explanations include mispricing, issuance timing, issuer characteristics, and changes in risk after firms exercise growth options.

`[TEXTBOOK CONTEXT]` Exact historical return figures are evidence from textbook samples, not universal future laws.

## 9. 23.4 The Seasoned Equity Offering

### Definition

A **seasoned equity offering (SEO)** is a later equity issue by a firm whose stock already trades publicly.

Main difference from IPO:

> market price already exists.

SEO may contain both primary and secondary shares.

### Cash Offer

In a **cash offer**, new shares are offered broadly to investors.

If issue price is far below fair market value, new investors can receive value at the expense of old shareholders.

### Rights Offer

In a **rights offer**, new shares are offered to existing shareholders.

Existing holders receive rights proportional to ownership, allowing them to preserve their ownership share or capture the economic value of the discount.

### Why Rights Protect Existing Shareholders

Textbook example:

- firm has $100 cash;
- 50 shares;
- share price = $2;
- issues 50 shares at $1.

After issue:

\[
V=100+50=150
\]

Total shares:

\[
100
\]

Ex-issue share price:

\[
P=150/100=1.50
\]

In a cash offer, new buyers pay $1 for a share worth $1.50, gaining $0.50 per share at old shareholders' expense.

In a rights offer, old shareholders receive the right to buy at $1. The value of that right offsets the fall from $2 to $1.50.

> [!IMPORTANT] Core Lesson
> Rights offering can raise equity at a discounted subscription price without transferring value away from existing shareholders, because **the discount itself is allocated to existing owners through the rights**.

### SEO Announcement Effect and Adverse Selection

Textbook reports that SEO announcements are often followed by stock-price decline.

An adverse-selection explanation:

```text
Management knows more than market
      ↓
Management chooses to sell equity now
      ↓
Investors infer shares are unlikely to be undervalued
      ↓
Possibly overvalued
      ↓
Price declines
```

This is an **information effect**, not mechanical dilution.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> “More shares outstanding” is not enough to explain value destruction. If shares are sold at fair value, firm also receives cash. The negative announcement effect is linked to what issuance may reveal about management's private information.

### Post-SEO Performance

Like IPOs, SEO issuers show long-run underperformance in some textbook samples.

One explanation linked to Chapter 22:

- before investment, firm holds risky growth options;
- after financing and investment, growth options are exercised;
- projects become assets in place;
- beta may fall;
- lower subsequent average returns may reflect lower risk rather than pure mispricing.

## Chapter Synthesis

Chapter 23 explains how equity financing evolves as a firm matures.

Early companies cannot easily sell claims to anonymous public investors because information asymmetry is severe and shares are illiquid. Financing therefore starts with investors who can monitor directly—angels, VC funds, and strategic investors.

Young companies commonly use **convertible preferred stock** because it matches the state-contingent needs of outside investors:

```text
Failure
→ priority matters

Success
→ conversion upside matters
```

Successive financing rounds generate new valuations. **Pre-money** describes firm equity before capital injection, while **post-money** adds the new funds and determines post-round ownership.

IPO then solves two major problems simultaneously:

```text
Private investors need liquidity
+
Company needs more capital
      ↓
IPO
```

Public status comes with trade-offs: more liquidity and capital versus dispersed ownership, disclosure, governance, and issuance costs.

Investment banks play a key economic role through due diligence, marketing, information gathering, pricing, allocation, and sometimes underwriting risk. IPO underpricing shows that pricing new equity under asymmetric information is costly. Yet investors cannot simply earn the average first-day return because good issues are rationed—the winner's curse.

Once company is public, equity financing continues through SEO. Rights offerings reveal a central principle: **a low subscription price is not automatically a gift to outsiders if existing shareholders receive the rights**.

Finally, Chapter 23 returns to information asymmetry. An SEO can lower stock price not because share count mechanically dilutes value, but because management's willingness to issue equity can reveal information about valuation.

The financing life cycle is:

```text
Founder
  ↓
Angel / VC / Strategic Capital
  ↓
Convertible Preferred
  ↓
Private Funding Rounds
  ↓
IPO
  ↓
Public Equity
  ↓
SEO / Rights Offering
```
