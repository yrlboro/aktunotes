# Robinson — Chapter 3: Financial Reporting Standards

> [!ABSTRACT] Chapter in One View
> Chapter ini menjelaskan **framework yang membuat financial reporting dapat dipercaya dan dibandingkan**, bukan sekadar rules untuk satu account tertentu. Financial reporting standards diperlukan karena penyusunan laporan membutuhkan **policy choices, estimates, dan judgment**; tanpa common framework, perusahaan dapat merepresentasikan economic events yang mirip secara sangat berbeda. Chapter membedakan **standard-setting bodies**, yang pada umumnya mengembangkan rules, dari **regulatory authorities**, yang memiliki legal authority untuk mengawasi dan menegakkan reporting requirements. Global convergence menuju IFRS meningkatkan potensi comparability lintas negara, tetapi full convergence tetap terhambat oleh perbedaan institutional, regulatory, business, cultural, political, application, dan enforcement environments. Pusat chapter adalah **IASB Conceptual Framework**, yang menempatkan decision-useful financial information sebagai objective, dengan **relevance** dan **faithful representation** sebagai fundamental qualitative characteristics serta **comparability, verifiability, timeliness, dan understandability** sebagai enhancing characteristics. Framework juga menjelaskan financial statement elements, recognition, measurement, accrual accounting, going concern, dan cost-benefit constraint. IAS 1 kemudian menerjemahkan framework tersebut ke general presentation requirements: complete set of financial statements, fair presentation, going concern, accrual basis, materiality, no offsetting, consistency, annual reporting, dan comparative information. Robinson menegaskan bahwa framework yang efektif harus **transparent, comprehensive, dan consistent**. Bagi analyst, consequence akhirnya adalah: selalu ketahui **reporting framework yang dipakai, accounting policies yang dipilih, perbedaan standards yang belum converged, dan perubahan standards yang dapat mengubah historical comparability maupun future forecasts**.

## 1. Why This Chapter Exists

Chapter 1 menunjukkan bagaimana analyst menggunakan financial reports. Chapter 2 menunjukkan bagaimana transactions menjadi accounts dan financial statements. Chapter 3 naik satu tingkat lagi dan bertanya:

> **Siapa menentukan bagaimana transaksi seharusnya dilaporkan, berdasarkan konsep apa, dan mengapa analyst harus peduli?**

Bayangkan dua perusahaan melakukan economic transaction yang identik tetapi masing-masing bebas menentukan kapan revenue diakui, apakah expenditure menjadi asset atau expense, bagaimana asset diukur, dan disclosure apa yang diberikan. Jika setiap perusahaan bebas sepenuhnya, comparison menjadi lemah.

Karena financial reporting membutuhkan judgment, common standards berfungsi untuk **mengarahkan dan membatasi judgment**, bukan menghapusnya.

```text
Economic reality
↓
Accounting judgment is unavoidable
↓
Conceptual framework
↓
Financial reporting standards
↓
Company accounting policies & estimates
↓
Financial statements + disclosures
↓
Regulatory oversight / enforcement
↓
Analyst interpretation
```

Robinson menekankan bahwa understanding atas underlying framework lebih luas dan lebih berguna daripada menghafal isolated accounting rules. Ketika transaction baru belum secara eksplisit diatur, conceptual logic membantu analyst memahami bagaimana transaction tersebut seharusnya direpresentasikan dan apa valuation implications-nya.

## 3. Introduction

Financial reporting standards menetapkan principles yang digunakan dalam preparation of financial reports dan memengaruhi **jenis serta jumlah information** yang diberikan kepada users. Standards langsung memengaruhi reported assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses, presentation, comparability, dan valuation inputs.

Chapter ini karena itu fokus pada architecture financial reporting:

```text
Objective
↓
Conceptual framework
↓
Standard setting
↓
Regulation / enforcement
↓
Accounting standards
↓
Company application
↓
Financial reports
```

## 4. The Objective of Financial Reporting

### 4.1 General-Purpose Financial Reporting

Objective framework adalah menyediakan financial information tentang reporting entity yang berguna bagi existing dan potential investors, lenders, serta other creditors dalam decisions tentang penyediaan resources kepada entity.

Decisions tersebut mencakup membeli, menjual, atau menahan equity/debt instruments serta menyediakan atau menyelesaikan loans dan forms of credit lainnya.

Financial reporting bukan dirancang untuk memberi satu angka “true value” perusahaan. Ia menyediakan **information useful for decision making**.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Financial reporting ≠ valuation.** Financial reports menyediakan inputs; investor atau analyst tetap melakukan valuation dan credit assessment.

### 4.2 Why Standards Are Necessary

Financial reporting memerlukan accounting policy choices, estimates, assumptions, dan management judgment. Dua reasonable preparers dapat menghasilkan estimates berbeda.

```text
Same economic event
↓
Different judgments
↓
Different recognition / measurement
↓
Lower consistency
↓
Lower comparability
↓
Harder investment / credit decisions
```

Standards menciptakan common boundaries sehingga judgment lebih disciplined dan understandable.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Standards reduce variability in judgment; they do not eliminate judgment.**

## 5. Standard-Setting Bodies and Regulatory Authorities

### 5.1 Standard-Setting Bodies

Secara umum, standard-setting bodies mengembangkan financial reporting standards, meninjau existing standards, menjalankan due process, meminta input constituents, dan menerbitkan authoritative guidance.

Contoh utama:

- **International Accounting Standards Board (IASB)** → develops IFRS;
- **Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB)** → develops US GAAP dalam konteks Amerika Serikat.

### 5.2 Regulatory Authorities

Regulators biasanya memiliki legal authority, menentukan siapa harus file, mengawasi securities markets, enforce disclosure/reporting requirements, dan dapat memberikan sanctions.

Contoh textbook: **US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)**.

```text
Standard setter
→ develops accounting standards

Regulator
→ legal oversight + enforcement
```

Textbook memberi nuance: regulator dapat mengandalkan private-sector body untuk standard development, tetapi legal authority tetap berada pada regulator dalam jurisdiction tersebut.

### 5.3 Desirable Attributes of Standard Setters

Standard-setting process yang credible membutuhkan independence, professional competence, adequate resources, due process, stakeholder input, dan resistance terhadap undue political atau industry pressure.

Accounting rules dapat mengubah reported earnings, capital ratios, compensation, contractual outcomes, dan market perceptions. Karena itu, lobbying pressure nyata dan independence penting.

### 5.4 IASB, FASB, SEC, IOSCO

- **IASB**: international private-sector standard setter untuk IFRS.
- **FASB**: private-sector standard setter untuk US GAAP.
- **SEC**: securities regulator AS dengan legal authority terhadap public-company reporting.
- **IOSCO**: international organization of securities regulators yang mendukung cooperation, high-quality cross-border regulation, dan high-quality international reporting standards.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **IASB/FASB ≠ SEC/IOSCO.** Yang pertama berfokus pada standard-setting; yang kedua berfokus pada regulatory architecture dan market oversight.

## 6. Convergence of Global Financial Reporting Standards

### 6.1 Why Convergence Is Attractive

Cross-border capital markets membuat investor perlu membandingkan perusahaan dari banyak jurisdiction. Common global reporting language berpotensi meningkatkan comparability, menurunkan analysis cost, dan memudahkan cross-border investment.

### 6.2 Adoption vs Convergence

**Adoption** berarti jurisdiction menggunakan IFRS. **Convergence** berarti local standards secara bertahap dipertemukan dengan IFRS atau standards lain sehingga differences berkurang.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Convergence ≠ immediate identical standards.**

### 6.3 IASB–FASB Convergence

`[TEXTBOOK CONTEXT]` Robinson membahas **Norwalk Agreement (2002)** sebagai milestone formal IASB–FASB untuk meningkatkan compatibility existing standards dan mengoordinasikan future work. Note ini mempertahankan historical status dalam textbook dan tidak memperbaruinya dengan post-source developments.

### 6.4 Barriers to Full Convergence

Barriers utama:

- institutional differences;
- regulatory differences;
- business differences;
- cultural differences;
- political dan lobbying pressure;
- application differences;
- enforcement differences.

Salah satu lesson terpenting chapter adalah:

> **Common written standards tidak menjamin common reporting outcomes.**

Jika application dan enforcement berbeda, comparability tetap dapat lemah.

Conceptual Framework membantu standard setters, preparers, auditors, dan users/analysts. Untuk analyst, framework menjelaskan **mengapa accounting standards mengambil bentuk tertentu**.

## 8. Qualitative Characteristics of Useful Financial Information

### 8.1 Fundamental Characteristics

Dua fundamental characteristics:

1. **Relevance**
2. **Faithful Representation**

### 8.2 Relevance

Information relevant jika capable of making a difference dalam user decision. Relevance berhubungan dengan predictive value, confirmatory value, dan materiality.

#### Materiality

Materiality adalah entity-specific aspect of relevance. Information material jika omission atau misstatement dapat influence decisions.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Materiality is contextual, not an absolute amount.**

### 8.3 Faithful Representation

Information harus faithfully represent economic phenomenon. Framework mengaitkannya dengan representation yang complete, neutral, dan free from error dalam sense framework.

“Free from error” bukan berarti every estimate perfectly certain. Estimates dapat tetap faithfully represented jika process dan assumptions tepat dan uncertainty dijelaskan.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Faithful representation ≠ perfect certainty.**

### 8.4 Enhancing Characteristics

- **Comparability** — melihat similarities dan differences antar-period atau antar-company.
- **Verifiability** — knowledgeable independent observers dapat mencapai reasonable consensus.
- **Timeliness** — information tersedia sebelum kehilangan decision usefulness.
- **Understandability** — information classified dan presented clearly.

Comparability bukan uniformity. Different economics tidak boleh dipaksa terlihat sama hanya untuk consistency.

## 9. Constraint: Cost versus Benefit

Preparing, auditing, disseminating, dan analyzing financial information memiliki cost. Framework mengakui bahwa benefits of information harus justify costs. Ini menjelaskan mengapa reporting framework tidak meminta every possible piece of information.

## 10. Elements of Financial Statements

### 10.1 Financial Position Elements

**Assets** adalah resources controlled by enterprise as result of past events from which future economic benefits are expected to flow.

**Liabilities** adalah present obligations arising from past events whose settlement is expected to result in an outflow of resources embodying economic benefits.

**Equity** adalah residual interest:

$$
Equity = Assets - Liabilities
$$

### 10.2 Performance Elements

**Income** adalah increases in economic benefits yang meningkatkan equity selain owner contributions. Income mencakup revenues dan gains.

**Expenses** adalah decreases in economic benefits yang menurunkan equity selain distributions to owners. Expenses mencakup normal expenses dan losses.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Income is broader than revenue. Expenses include losses.**

## 11. Underlying Assumptions

### 11.1 Accrual Basis

Transactions direfleksikan pada period ketika economic effects terjadi, bukan semata saat cash berpindah.

Karena itu:

> **Profit ≠ cash flow.**

### 11.2 Going Concern

Financial statements biasanya mengasumsikan entity akan continue operating for the foreseeable future. Assumption ini memengaruhi measurement dan presentation.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Going concern ≠ guarantee that the company will survive forever.**

## 12. Recognition and Measurement

Recognition berarti memasukkan item ke financial statements ketika memenuhi relevant criteria. Dalam framework source, recognition terkait probability of future economic benefit dan ability to measure cost/value with reliability.

```text
Economic phenomenon
↓
Meets element definition?
↓
Meets recognition criteria?
├── Yes → Recognize in statements
└── No → May require disclosure / no recognition
```

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Economically important ≠ automatically recognized on the balance sheet.**

Measurement bases yang dibahas meliputi historical cost, current cost, realizable/settlement value, present value, dan fair value.

### Historical Cost

Transaction-grounded dan sering verifiable, tetapi dapat kurang current.

### Current Cost

Amount needed currently untuk memperoleh equivalent asset atau settle liability.

### Realizable / Settlement Value

Amount obtainable from disposal atau expected settlement amount.

### Present Value

Future cash flows didiskontokan ke current value.

### Fair Value

`[TEXTBOOK CONTEXT]` Chapter menggunakan definition fair value yang berlaku pada konteks source dan menyebut joint IASB/FASB fair-value project. Note tidak memperbaruinya dengan definitions sesudah textbook.

Measurement basis penting karena dapat mengubah reported assets, liabilities, income, equity, dan ratios.

## 13. General Requirements for Financial Statements — IAS 1

IAS 1 menjelaskan required statements, general features, serta structure dan content.

### Figure/Table — IASB General Requirements

| Required Financial Statements | General Features | Structure & Content |
|---|---|---|
| Statement of financial position | Fair presentation | Classified balance sheet |
| Statement of comprehensive income | Going concern | Minimum information on face |
| Statement of changes in equity | Frequency of reporting | Comparative information |
| Statement of cash flows | Comparative information | Minimum note disclosures |
| Earliest comparative statement in certain cases | Consistency of presentation | |
| Notes | Accrual basis | |
| | Materiality & aggregation | |
| | No offsetting | |

### 13.1 Complete Set

Complete set mencakup statement of financial position, statement of comprehensive income, statement of changes in equity, statement of cash flows, notes, dan dalam certain circumstances additional comparative statement of financial position.

### 13.2 Fair Presentation

Statements harus fairly present financial position, financial performance, dan cash flows.

### 13.3 Going Concern

Management menilai ability entity untuk continue as going concern. Significant uncertainty perlu disclosure jika relevant.

### 13.4 Frequency and Comparative Information

Complete financial statements disajikan setidaknya annually dan menyertakan prior-period comparative information.

### 13.5 Consistency of Presentation

Presentation dan classification seharusnya consistent across periods kecuali change justified atau required.

> [!WARNING] Important Distinction
> **Consistency does not mean accounting can never change.**

### 13.6 Accrual Basis

Selain cash flow information, statements disusun menggunakan accrual accounting.

### 13.7 Materiality and Aggregation

Material classes disajikan separately; immaterial items dapat aggregated appropriately.

### 13.8 No Offsetting

Assets dan liabilities atau income dan expenses umumnya tidak di-offset kecuali required/permitted by standard. Netting dapat menyembunyikan gross exposure.

## 14. Characteristics of an Effective Financial Reporting Framework

Robinson menyebut tiga characteristics utama:

1. **Transparency**
2. **Comprehensiveness**
3. **Consistency**

### Transparency

Reports harus memberi clear representation of underlying economics.

### Comprehensiveness

Framework harus cukup lengkap untuk broad range of transactions dan mengurangi gaps atau structuring opportunities.

### Consistency

Similar transactions seharusnya memperoleh similar treatment across firms dan periods bila economics-nya comparable.

Barriers terhadap coherent framework dapat berasal dari valuation differences, alternative measurement bases, political pressure, industry-specific practices, legacy standards, legal requirements, dan enforcement differences.

## 15. IFRS and US GAAP

Chapter memberi high-level comparison, bukan exhaustive list semua differences.

IASB dan FASB telah mengurangi sejumlah framework differences melalui convergence, tetapi differences tetap ada pada definitions, recognition, measurement, presentation, terminology, dan specific standards.

### Example — Syngenta IFRS vs US GAAP Reconciliation

`[TEXTBOOK EXAMPLE]`

Reported 2006 net income:

$$
\text{IFRS Net Income} = \$634\text{ million}
$$

Setelah US GAAP adjustments:

$$
\text{US GAAP Net Income} = \$504\text{ million}
$$

Difference:

$$
634 - 504 = \$130\text{ million}
$$

Significant reconciliation areas termasuk acquisition accounting, pension provisions, put options, taxes, environmental remediation, restructuring, impairment, dan inventory items.

**Meaning:** underlying company sama, tetapi reporting framework berbeda menghasilkan materially different reported income. Analyst perlu mengidentifikasi reconciliation items yang material dan memahami economic effect-nya.

## 16. Monitoring Developments in Financial Reporting Standards

Standards berubah. Analyst tidak boleh memperlakukan accounting rules sebagai static background.

Changes dapat memengaruhi:

- assets;
- liabilities;
- equity;
- earnings;
- cash-flow classifications;
- leverage/profitability ratios;
- covenant metrics;
- forecasts.

### 16.1 Significant Accounting Policies

Notes menjelaskan standards used, significant accounting policies, choices, changes, dan expected effects of newly issued standards bila disclosed.

Accounting-policy disclosure adalah map tentang bagaimana economic events diubah menjadi reported numbers.

### Example — General Electric New Consolidation Rules

`[TEXTBOOK EXAMPLE]`

Robinson menunjukkan disclosure GE yang memperkirakan akibat new consolidation requirements:

- total assets naik sekitar $32 billion;
- total liabilities naik sekitar $34 billion;
- equity turun sekitar $2 billion.

Analyst dapat menggunakan disclosure tersebut untuk adjust expected balance sheet, leverage ratios, dan forecasts sebelum formal adoption.

```text
New / proposed reporting standard
↓
Which companies / transactions affected?
↓
Recognition / measurement / presentation change?
↓
Historical statements comparable?
↓
Impact on A, L, E, income, cash-flow classification?
↓
Impact on ratios / covenants?
↓
Impact on forecasts / valuation?
```

## Chapter Synthesis

Jika seluruh chapter dipahami sebagai satu cerita, ceritanya adalah ini:

**Financial reporting tidak pernah sepenuhnya mechanical. Economic reality kompleks dan harus diterjemahkan melalui recognition, measurement, presentation, estimates, dan disclosure. Karena translation tersebut membutuhkan judgment, markets membutuhkan standards. Standards dibangun di atas conceptual framework yang menjelaskan objective reporting dan qualitative characteristics informasi yang useful. Standard setters mengembangkan rules, regulators mengawasi dan enforce reporting dalam jurisdiction.**

Globalization menciptakan kebutuhan financial reporting language yang lebih common. Namun **common standards alone are insufficient**: institutional differences, politics, application, dan enforcement tetap dapat menghasilkan differences in practice.

Conceptual hierarchy-nya:

```text
Why report?
→ Decision usefulness

What makes information useful?
→ Relevance + faithful representation
→ enhanced by comparability, verifiability, timeliness, understandability

What is reported?
→ Assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses

On what assumptions?
→ Accrual basis + going concern

Within what constraint?
→ Cost versus benefit

How presented?
→ IAS 1 general requirements
```

Bagi analyst, jangan hanya bertanya **“berapa reported profit?”** tetapi juga **under which standards, with which policies, using what measurement, under what assumptions, and are peer numbers genuinely comparable?**

## Quick Reading Review

- Financial reporting bertujuan menyediakan **decision-useful information** bagi investors, lenders, dan creditors.
- Standards dibutuhkan karena reporting mengandung **policy choices, estimates, dan judgment**.
- **Standard setter develops standards; regulator enforces**.
- IASB → IFRS; FASB → US GAAP; SEC → US securities regulator; IOSCO → international regulator cooperation.
- Convergence meningkatkan comparability tetapi common standards tidak menjamin common application.
- Fundamental qualitative characteristics: **relevance + faithful representation**.
- Enhancing characteristics: **comparability + verifiability + timeliness + understandability**.
- Materiality adalah entity-specific aspect of relevance.
- Financial statement elements: **assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses**.
- **Accrual basis** membuat recognition timing berbeda dari cash timing.
- **Going concern** adalah assumption, bukan guarantee.
- Measurement basis dapat mengubah reported A/L/E/income dan ratios.
- IAS 1 menekankan fair presentation, annual reporting, comparatives, consistency, materiality, accrual basis, dan no offsetting.
- Effective framework harus **transparent, comprehensive, consistent**.
- Same company dapat menghasilkan materially different income di bawah different GAAP.
- Analyst harus membaca significant accounting policies dan memonitor new standards.
