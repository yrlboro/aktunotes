# CF4 — Stage 14: Reconstruction Paths

> [!ABSTRACT] Tujuan
> Stage 14 menjawab satu pertanyaan praktis:
>
> **“Kalau saya lupa concept/formula ini di exam, apa jalur paling pendek dan aman untuk membangunnya kembali?”**
>
> Reconstruction diprioritaskan untuk mengurangi ketergantungan pada formula dump dan mengatasi notation/source collision.

## 1. Reconstruction Principle

Gunakan urutan:

```text
Primitive idea
→ economic/accounting meaning
→ relationship
→ formula / treatment
→ sanity check
```

Bukan:

```text
formula fragment
→ guess what it means
```

## 2. Reconstruction Path Matrix

| ID | Concept / Formula | Retrieval Policy | Primitive Anchor | Shortest Safe Reconstruction | Rebuilt Result | Sanity Check / Trap | Source Area |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Accounting Equation | MEMORIZE / RECONSTRUCT BACKSTOP | Assets = Liabilities + Equity | Ask: who financed the assets? Creditors + owners. | Assets are resources; liabilities and equity are financing claims. | If transaction set does not balance, treatment is wrong. | 1.4–1.5 |
| R02 | Revenue Recognition | RECONSTRUCT | Revenue = earned income, not cash received | Ask: has performance/earning occurred? | If earned: recognize revenue; cash may be now or later. | Do not use cash timing as recognition rule. | 1.3–1.5 |
| R03 | Expense Recognition | RECONSTRUCT | Expense = incurred/consumed economic benefit, not cash paid | Ask: was benefit consumed/incurred? | If incurred: recognize expense; cash may be now or later. | Prepayment can be asset; AP payment may settle old expense. | 1.3–1.5 |
| R04 | Retained Earnings | MEMORIZE / RECONSTRUCT BACKSTOP | Ending RE = Beginning RE + NI − Dividends | Start accumulated earnings; add current profit; subtract owner distributions. | RE tracks accumulated undistributed earnings. | RE ≠ cash. | 1.4–1.5 |
| R05 | Customer Advance | RECONSTRUCT | Cash first, revenue later | Cash received before earning → obligation exists. | Cash ↑, Unearned Revenue ↑; later liability ↓, revenue ↑. | Advance ≠ revenue until earned. | 1.5 |
| R06 | Prepaid Expense | RECONSTRUCT | Asset first, expense later | Cash paid before consumption → future benefit remains. | Cash ↓, Prepaid Asset ↑; later asset ↓, expense ↑. | Payment ≠ expense automatically. | 1.5 |
| R07 | Accrued Expense | RECONSTRUCT | Expense first, cash later | Expense incurred before payment → obligation exists. | Expense ↑, Liability ↑; later cash ↓, liability ↓. | Later payment does not create new expense. | 1.5 |
| R08 | Credit Sale / AR | RECONSTRUCT | Revenue now, cash later | Earned but uncollected → receivable. | AR ↑, Revenue ↑; collection later Cash ↑, AR ↓. | Collection ≠ new revenue. | 1.5 |
| R09 | Depreciation | RECONSTRUCT | Accounting expense, noncash in period | Asset cost allocated over use; cash purchase occurred earlier. | Expense ↑, accumulated depreciation ↑, NI ↓; no current cash outflow. | Depreciation ≠ CapEx. | 1.5 / 3.3 |
| R10 | Consolidation | RECONSTRUCT | Control → one economic group → eliminate internal effects | Ask: does parent control subsidiary? | If yes: combine group external balances, eliminate intercompany, identify NCI. | Do not simply add statements. | 1.4 |
| R11 | Current Ratio | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | Liquidity = near-term resources relative to near-term obligations. | Put current resources on top, current obligations below. | Higher ≠ automatically better. | 1.6 |
| R12 | Quick Ratio | RECONSTRUCT | Most liquid current assets / current liabilities | Start from current ratio and remove inventory/prepaids. | Cash + marketable securities/short-term investments + receivables over current liabilities. | Use source quick-asset definition. | 1.6 |
| R13 | AR Turnover | RECONSTRUCT | Credit-sales flow / receivable stock | Turnover asks: how much flow passes through average stock? | Net Credit Sales / Average Net AR. | Do not use total sales if credit sales are provided. | 1.6 |
| R14 | Inventory Turnover | RECONSTRUCT | COGS flow / inventory stock | Inventory is consumed through cost of goods sold. | COGS / Average Inventory. | Sales numerator is a common trap. | 1.6 |
| R15 | ROA | RECONSTRUCT | Profit / asset base | Return on assets asks earnings generated by assets. | Net Income / Average Total Assets under Weygandt convention. | Average vs ending assets is source-sensitive. | 1.6 |
| R16 | ROE | RECONSTRUCT | Common earnings / common equity base | Ask: what earnings belong to common shareholders? | (Net Income − Preferred Dividends) / Average Common Equity. | Do not mix total equity if preferred exists. | 1.6 |
| R17 | Debt-to-Assets | RECONSTRUCT | Creditor financing / total assets | Ask how much of assets are financed by creditor claims. | Weygandt: Total Liabilities / Total Assets. | Debt definition is source-sensitive. | 1.6 |
| R18 | Debt vs Equity | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Debt = contractual claim; Equity = residual claim | Ask: fixed contractual payment or residual ownership? | Debt has contractual obligations/priority; equity absorbs residual outcome. | Preferred needs separate treatment. | 2.1–2.2 |
| R19 | Secured vs Senior | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Secured = collateral; Senior = priority | Ask two separate questions: what collateral? what ranking? | Do not collapse claim dimensions. | Secured ≠ senior. | 2.2 |
| R20 | Trade Credit Cost | RECONSTRUCT | Lost discount / funds effectively retained | If discount d is forgone, you pay d more relative to 1−d retained financing. | Periodic cost = d/(1−d); annualize over extra-credit period. | Discount percent ≠ annual cost. | 2.3 |
| R21 | Long Call Payoff | RECONSTRUCT | Right to buy at K | If market S_T > K, exercise; else walk away. | max(S_T−K,0). | Payoff ≠ profit. | 2.4 |
| R22 | Long Put Payoff | RECONSTRUCT | Right to sell at K | If K > S_T, exercise; else walk away. | max(K−S_T,0). | Payoff ≠ profit. | 2.4 |
| R23 | Option Profit | RECONSTRUCT | Payoff minus premium for long position | Start with contractual payoff, then include initial option price. | Long profit = payoff − premium. | Short side reverses economics. | 2.4 |
| R24 | Forward Payoff | RECONSTRUCT | Obligation to transact at K | Long receives asset worth S_T and pays K. | Long payoff = S_T−K; short = K−S_T. | No max function. | 5.2 |
| R25 | Primary vs Secondary Shares | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Who gets the cash? | If issuer receives proceeds → primary; if selling owner receives proceeds → secondary. | Use cash destination as routing rule. | Secondary sale ≠ company financing. | 2.5 |
| R26 | Pre-/Post-Money Valuation | RECONSTRUCT | Post = Pre + New Cash | Value old ownership first, then add new investment. | V_pre = old shares × price; V_post = V_pre + new capital. | Dilution ≠ automatic wealth loss. | 2.5 |
| R27 | Underwriting Spread | RECONSTRUCT | Public offer price minus issuer-effective proceeds price | Spread measures direct underwriter compensation. | (P_offer − P_issuer)/P_offer. | Spread ≠ underpricing. | 2.5 |
| R28 | After-Tax Cost of Debt | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Debt cost × (1−T) | Interest deduction saves T of each interest dollar. | r_D(1−T). | Do not tax-adjust equity cost. | 3.2–3.3 |
| R29 | WACC | MEMORIZE / RECONSTRUCT BACKSTOP | Weighted required return of capital providers | List debt, preferred, equity; apply after-tax adjustment to debt; weight each component. | w_D r_D(1−T)+w_P r_P+w_E r_E. | Project risk must match before using as project rate. | 3.3 |
| R30 | NOPAT | RECONSTRUCT | Operating profit after tax before financing effects | Start from EBIT, tax operating profit directly. | NOPAT = EBIT(1−T). | NOPAT ≠ net income. | 3.3 / 4.2 |
| R31 | FCF | RECONSTRUCT | After-tax operating profit minus operating reinvestment | Ask: what operating cash remains after capital required for operations? | FCF = NOPAT − Net Investment in Operating Capital. | FCF ≠ NI. | 3.3 / 4.2 |
| R32 | ΔNWC | RECONSTRUCT | More working capital tied up = cash use | Compare current NWC to prior period. | ΔNWC = NWC_t − NWC_{t−1}; increase reduces FCF. | Use operating NWC in project context. | 3.3 |
| R33 | Depreciation Tax Shield | RECONSTRUCT | Deduction × tax rate | Depreciation is noncash but reduces taxable income if deductible. | DTS = T × Depreciation. | Depreciation ≠ cash inflow; tax shield is the cash effect. | 3.3 |
| R34 | NPV | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | PV of all incremental cash flows | Discount every project CF to same date and sum. | NPV = Σ CF_t/(1+r)^t. | Use risk-appropriate r. | 3.4 |
| R35 | IRR | RECONSTRUCT | Rate that makes NPV zero | Take NPV equation and solve for r when NPV = 0. | 0 = Σ CF_t/(1+IRR)^t. | Nonnormal CF can create multiple IRRs. | 3.4 |
| R36 | MIRR | RECONSTRUCT | Connect PV of costs to terminal value of inflows | Move negative CFs to t0, positive CFs to terminal date, solve one compound rate. | MIRR = [TV(inflows)/PV(costs)]^(1/n) − 1. | MIRR ≠ IRR. | 3.4 |
| R37 | Payback | RECONSTRUCT | When cumulative cash inflow recovers initial outlay | Cumulate project cash flows until zero net unrecovered amount. | No need for a special formula unless partial year interpolation. | Regular payback ignores TVM. | 3.4 |
| R38 | ARR | RECONSTRUCT | Accounting income / average investment | It is an accounting-return metric, not discounted cash-flow metric. | ARR = Expected Annual Net Income / Average Investment. | ARR ≠ IRR. | 3.4 |
| R39 | PI — Brigham | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | PV inflows per initial investment | Start from standard initial-outlay project. | PV future inflows / initial investment. | Threshold >1 belongs to this convention. | 3.4 |
| R40 | PI — Berk & DeMarzo | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | NPV per scarce resource | Ask: how much value per constrained resource unit? | NPV / resource consumed. | Do not import Brigham threshold. | 3.4 |
| R41 | Value of Operations | RECONSTRUCT | PV of future operating FCF | Firm operations are worth the discounted cash they can generate. | Σ FCF_t/(1+WACC)^t. | Match FCF and discount-rate risk. | 4.2 |
| R42 | Horizon Value | RECONSTRUCT | Growing perpetuity starting after forecast horizon | At time N, value all later FCFs using growing perpetuity. | FCF_{N+1}/(WACC−g). | This value is at time N, not time 0. | 4.2 |
| R43 | Agency Problem | RECONSTRUCT | Principal-agent incentives diverge | Ask: who delegates? who controls decision? what incentive differs? | Conflict → distorted behavior → agency cost → governance response. | Agency problem ≠ agency cost. | 4.3 |
| R44 | Governance Mechanism | RECONSTRUCT | Mechanism must target a specific conflict | Identify agency conflict first, then ask what monitoring/incentive/control reduces it. | Board, covenants, compensation, takeover discipline, etc. | Governance has implementation/flexibility cost. | 4.3 |
| R45 | Total Return | RECONSTRUCT | Income + price change over beginning value | Investor earns distributions plus change in market value. | (Dividend + P_end − P_begin)/P_begin. | Dividend yield alone ≠ total return. | 5.4 |
| R46 | Arithmetic Average Return | RECONSTRUCT | Simple mean of periodic returns | Add periodic returns and divide by number of periods. | ΣR_t/T. | Arithmetic mean ≠ CAGR. | 5.4 |
| R47 | CAGR | RECONSTRUCT | Constant compound rate that links initial to final wealth | Multiply gross returns or use FV/PV, then take root. | [Π(1+R_t)]^(1/T)−1. | CAGR ≠ arithmetic expected return. | 5.4 |
| R48 | Beta | RECONSTRUCT | Market-sensitivity slope | Ask: how much does asset co-move with market relative to market variance? | Cov(R_i,R_M)/Var(R_M). | Beta ≠ volatility. | 5.4 |
| R49 | CAPM | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Risk-free base + beta-scaled market premium | Start with risk-free; add compensation only for systematic exposure. | r_i = r_f + β_i(E[R_M]−r_f). | Use market risk premium, not market return. | 5.4 |
| R50 | Nominal–Real Relation | RECONSTRUCT | Gross nominal growth = gross real growth × inflation growth | Multiply purchasing-power growth by price-level growth. | 1+r_n = (1+r_r)(1+π). | r_n−π is approximation only. | 5.3–5.4 |
| R51 | Bond Price | RECONSTRUCT | PV of contractual coupons + principal | Discount each promised bond cash flow at market yield. | Price = PV(coupons)+PV(face). | Coupon rate ≠ yield. | 5.3 |
| R52 | Gordon Growth Model | RECONSTRUCT | Growing perpetuity of dividends | Value constant-growth dividends as perpetuity beginning with D1. | P0 = D1/(r−g). | Use D1, and require r>g. | 5.3–5.4 |
| R53 | Market Rate Increase Interpretation | RECONSTRUCT | Higher discount rate lowers PV of unchanged cash flows | Existing fixed bond CF unchanged while market required yield rises. | Bond price falls; issuer borrowing cost may rise. | Perspective matters: new lender may like higher yield. | 5.3 |
| R54 | Economic Growth Interpretation | RECONSTRUCT | Two-channel analysis | Ask separately: expected CF effect? required-return effect? | If channels reinforce, direction clearer; if oppose, context-dependent. | Growth ≠ guaranteed positive market return. | 5.3 |
| R55 | Good News / Market Return | RECONSTRUCT | Price reacts to surprise vs prior expectation | Ask: was news better than already priced in? also what happened to discount rate? | Return depends on revision, not headline label. | Good news ≠ positive return. | 5.3–5.4 |
| R56 | Required vs Expected vs Realized Return | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Label time perspective first | Required = demanded; expected = anticipated; realized = actual outcome. | Keep ex ante and ex post separate. | Same numeric value does not make same concept. | 5.4 |
| R57 | Volatility vs Beta | MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP | Total dispersion vs systematic sensitivity | Standard deviation asks how spread out returns are; beta asks market co-movement. | Use volatility for total risk; beta for CAPM systematic risk. | Volatility ↑ does not mechanically imply CAPM required return ↑. | 5.4 |

## 3. Reconstruction Families

### 3.1 Accounting Timing Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
Earned / Incurred?
vs
Paid / Received?
```

From this alone you can rebuild:

- credit revenue;
- AR collection;
- accrued expense;
- AP payment;
- customer advance;
- prepaid expense;
- depreciation cash-vs-profit treatment.

### 3.2 Ratio Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
Flow
÷
Stock
```

Then ask:

```text
which flow?
which stock?
average or ending?
```

This reconstructs:

- AR turnover;
- inventory turnover;
- asset turnover;
- ROA;
- ROE.

### 3.3 Claim / Security Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
What rights does the holder have?
```

Then:

```text
fixed or residual cash flow?
priority?
collateral?
maturity?
optionality?
```

This reconstructs debt/equity/preferred/secured/senior distinctions.

### 3.4 Capital-Budgeting Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
Value
=
PV of incremental cash flow
```

From this:

```text
NPV
→ sum discounted CF

IRR
→ rate making NPV zero

WACC
→ weighted required return

FCF
→ operating cash after reinvestment
```

### 3.5 Option Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
Call = right to buy
Put = right to sell
```

Then compare market price with strike.

No need to memorize four independent payoff formulas.

### 3.6 Return Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
What kind of return?
```

Then label:

```text
historical / expected / required / realized
nominal / real
arithmetic / compound
total / excess
```

This prevents formula selection errors.

### 3.7 Market-Economic Family

Primitive anchor:

```text
Price
=
PV(expected cash flow)
```

Then ask:

```text
cash-flow expectation changed?
discount rate changed?
prior expectation?
```

This reconstructs most macro-direction answers.

## 5. What Should NOT Be Reconstructed Under Time Pressure

Some items are safer to recall directly because reconstruction adds ambiguity:

- terminology distinctions such as secured vs senior;
- primary vs secondary shares;
- required vs expected vs realized return;
- Brigham PI vs Berk PI;
- exact ratio source conventions if explicitly supplied;
- claimant priority labels.

These remain **MEMORIZE / BACKSTOP** items.

## 6. Reconstruction Failure Signals

Stop reconstruction and return to the canonical dictionary/formula when:

1. two source conventions are competing;
2. you cannot identify the quantity being asked;
3. the same label has two different definitions;
4. your reconstructed formula changes units;
5. your result violates a basic sign/direction check.

Examples:

```text
PI
→ source qualifier required

ROA
→ average vs ending convention required

Debt ratio
→ debt definition required
```

## 7. Unit-Based Reconstruction Check

Use dimensions:

### Ratio

```text
currency / currency
→ dimensionless
```

### Turnover

```text
currency per period
÷
currency
→ times per period
```

### Collection Period

```text
days
```

### NPV

```text
currency
```

### IRR / WACC / CAPM

```text
rate
```

### Beta

```text
dimensionless
```

If units do not match the requested quantity, reconstruction is probably wrong.

## 8. Sign-Based Reconstruction Check

### Project FCF

```text
CapEx ↑ → FCF ↓
ΔNWC > 0 → FCF ↓
NWC release → FCF ↑
```

### Bond

```text
yield ↑ → price ↓
```

### Option

```text
long call payoff ≥ 0
long put payoff ≥ 0
```

### Forward

```text
long + short payoff = 0
```

### Present Value

```text
discount rate ↑ → PV ↓
```

## 9. Perspective-Based Reconstruction Check

When confused by sign:

```text
who receives?
who pays?
who owns the right?
who bears the obligation?
```

Examples:

- interest = issuer cost / lender income;
- dividend = company cash outflow / shareholder cash receipt;
- call payoff = holder benefit / writer obligation;
- higher secured priority = creditor benefit / issuer flexibility cost.
