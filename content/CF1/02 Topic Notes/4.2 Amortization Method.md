# 📘 4.2 — Amortization Method

> [!ABSTRACT] Ringkasan Cepat
> **Topik:** Amortization Method  
> **Bobot Parent Topic:** 5–15%  
> **Exam Priority:** Medium  
> **Difficulty:** Calculation-Intensive  
> **Primary Skill:** Calculation  
> **Ref:** Vaaler Bab 5 §5.2; Kellison Bab 5 §§5.2–5.3  
>
> **Inti:** Pada amortized loan, setiap payment membayar **interest yang telah accrued terlebih dahulu**, lalu sisanya mengurangi principal. Hampir semua soal dapat dipecahkan dari tiga hubungan:
>
> $$
> B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t,
> $$
>
> $$
> I_t=iB_{t-1},
> $$
>
> $$
> P_t=R_t-I_t.
> $$

## Section 1 — Intuisi & Big Picture

Pada **amortization method**, utang benar-benar berkurang setiap kali borrower membayar principal. Namun payment tidak langsung seluruhnya mengurangi loan balance. Urutannya selalu:

```text
Outstanding balance setelah payment sebelumnya
        ↓ accrue interest
Balance tepat sebelum payment berikutnya
        ↓ payment masuk
Interest due dibayar
        ↓
Sisanya = principal repayment
        ↓
Outstanding balance baru
```

Jika $B_{t-1}$ adalah balance **immediately after** payment ke-$(t-1)$, maka satu payment period kemudian loan tumbuh menjadi

$$
B_{t-1}(1+i).
$$

Setelah borrower membayar $R_t$:

$$
\boxed{
B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t
}
$$

Inilah recursion paling fundamental untuk amortization.

Dari sudut pembagian payment:

$$
I_t=iB_{t-1}
$$

adalah interest portion, sedangkan

$$
P_t=R_t-I_t
$$

adalah principal portion. Karena principal portion mengurangi debt,

$$
B_t=B_{t-1}-P_t.
$$

Dua cara melihatnya—**accrue lalu subtract payment** atau **subtract principal portion**—harus menghasilkan balance yang sama.

> [!TIP] Exam Mental Model
> Jika bingung, jangan mulai dari formula annuity. Tulis dulu:
>
> **balance after previous payment → interest → total payment → principal → new balance**.
>
> Setelah itu baru tentukan apakah metode prospective, retrospective, atau recursion paling cepat.

## Section 2 — Definisi & Core Concepts

### 2.1 Notation

Dalam note ini:

| Simbol | Makna |
|---|---|
| $L$ | original loan amount |
| $R$ | level payment per period |
| $R_t$ | payment ke-$t$ jika payment tidak selalu level |
| $i$ | effective interest rate **per payment period** |
| $v$ | discount factor, $v=(1+i)^{-1}$ |
| $n$ | total number of scheduled payment periods |
| $B_t$ | outstanding balance immediately **after** payment ke-$t$ |
| $I_t$ | interest portion payment ke-$t$ |
| $P_t$ | principal portion payment ke-$t$ |

> [!IMPORTANT] Notation Mapping
> Kellison menggunakan notation prospective/retrospective dengan superscript pada balance dalam pembahasan textbook. Untuk study note ini digunakan satu notation $B_t$ karena secara matematis kedua metode harus menghasilkan **balance yang sama** pada valuation date yang sama.

### 2.2 Initial Equation of Value — Fully Amortized Level Loan

Jika loan sebesar $L$ dilunasi dengan $n$ level end-of-period payments sebesar $R$ pada effective rate $i$ per payment period:

| Waktu | 0 | 1 | 2 | $\cdots$ | $n$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Loan / Payment | $+L$ | $-R$ | $-R$ | $\cdots$ | $-R$ |

Dari lender perspective tanda berlawanan, tetapi equation of value sama secara magnitudo:

$$
\boxed{
L=R\,a_{\overline{n}|i}
}
$$

sehingga

$$
\boxed{
R=\frac{L}{a_{\overline{n}|i}}.
}
$$

Ini adalah starting equation untuk standard fully amortized loan.

### 2.3 Payment Decomposition

Untuk setiap payment:

$$
\boxed{
R_t=I_t+P_t
}
$$

dengan

$$
\boxed{
I_t=iB_{t-1}
}
$$

dan

$$
\boxed{
P_t=R_t-iB_{t-1}.
}
$$

Jika payment level $R_t=R$:

$$
P_t=R-iB_{t-1}.
$$

### 2.4 Balance Recursion

Dari satu payment date ke payment date berikutnya:

$$
\boxed{
B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t.
}
$$

Untuk level payments:

$$
\boxed{
B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R.
}
$$

Dengan initial condition:

$$
B_0=L.
$$

Untuk fully amortized loan:

$$
B_n=0.
$$

---

### 2.5 Outstanding Balance — Prospective Method

**Prospective method** melihat **future payments yang belum dibayar**.

Prinsipnya:

$$
\boxed{
B_t=PV_t(\text{future required payments})
}
$$

Untuk fully amortized loan dengan $n$ total level payments dan balance diukur immediately after payment ke-$t$, masih ada $n-t$ payments:

$$
\boxed{
B_t=R\,a_{\overline{n-t}|i}.
}
$$

Contoh bentuk:

$$
B_5=R\,a_{\overline{n-5}|i}.
$$

Interpretasi: setelah payment ke-5 selesai, debt yang tersisa setara dengan PV pada waktu 5 dari seluruh payments ke-6 sampai ke-$n$.

> [!TIP] Kapan Prospective Cepat?
> Gunakan prospective jika **future payments sederhana/known**: level payments, jumlah remaining payments diketahui, atau final payment diketahui.

---

### 2.6 Outstanding Balance — Retrospective Method

**Retrospective method** melihat **original loan dan payments yang sudah terjadi**.

Prinsipnya:

$$
\boxed{
B_t
=
AV_t(\text{original loan})
-
AV_t(\text{past payments})
}
$$

Untuk level payments $R$:

$$
\boxed{
B_t
=
L(1+i)^t
-
R\,s_{\overline{t}|i}.
}
$$

Karena $L=R a_{\overline{n}|i}$, formula ini equivalent dengan prospective formula.

Kellison menunjukkan secara algebra bahwa prospective dan retrospective balance harus sama karena keduanya hanyalah dua decomposition dari equation of value yang sama.

> [!TIP] Kapan Retrospective Cepat?
> Gunakan retrospective jika **past cash flows lebih sederhana** daripada future payments, atau original loan dan jumlah payments yang telah dibuat langsung tersedia.

---

### 2.7 Prospective = Retrospective

Untuk standard level loan:

$$
R\,a_{\overline{n-t}|i}
=
L(1+i)^t-R\,s_{\overline{t}|i}.
$$

Ini bukan dua definition yang bersaing. Keduanya adalah dua cara menghitung **quantity yang sama**.

> [!CHECK] Fundamental Identity
> Jika dua metode menghasilkan nilai berbeda, hampir pasti ada:
>
> - off-by-one timing error;
> - rate-frequency mismatch;
> - payment date yang salah;
> - atau salah membaca balance before vs after payment.

---

### 2.8 Interest Portion pada Payment ke-$t$

Karena balance sebelum period ke-$t$ adalah $B_{t-1}$:

$$
I_t=iB_{t-1}.
$$

Untuk standard level loan:

$$
B_{t-1}=R\,a_{\overline{n-t+1}|i}.
$$

Maka:

$$
I_t
=
iR\,a_{\overline{n-t+1}|i}.
$$

Gunakan identity

$$
i\,a_{\overline{m}|}=1-v^m.
$$

Sehingga:

$$
\boxed{
I_t
=
R\left(1-v^{n-t+1}\right).
}
$$

---

### 2.9 Principal Portion pada Payment ke-$t$

Karena

$$
P_t=R-I_t,
$$

maka:

$$
P_t
=
R-R\left(1-v^{n-t+1}\right)
$$

dan:

$$
\boxed{
P_t=R\,v^{n-t+1}.
}
$$

Formula ini sangat powerful untuk standard level-payment fully amortized loan.

Perhatikan exponent:

$$
\boxed{
n-t+1
}
$$

bukan $n-t$.

Sanity checks:

- untuk payment pertama, $t=1$:

$$
P_1=Rv^n;
$$

- untuk payment terakhir, $t=n$:

$$
P_n=Rv.
$$

Karena $v<1$, principal portion meningkat seiring $t$ bertambah.

---

### 2.10 Geometric Growth of Principal Portions

Untuk consecutive payments:

$$
P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}
$$

dan

$$
P_{t+1}=Rv^{n-t}.
$$

Maka:

$$
\boxed{
P_{t+1}=(1+i)P_t.
}
$$

Jadi principal portions membentuk **geometric progression** dengan ratio $1+i$.

Ini ditekankan baik pada struktur amortization schedule Kellison maupun Vaaler.

Sebaliknya, interest portion turun karena outstanding balance turun.

> [!IMPORTANT] High-Value Shortcut
> Jika soal memberi principal portion payment tertentu dan meminta principal portion beberapa payment sesudah/sebelumnya, sering tidak perlu mencari $L$ atau $R$:
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> P_{t+k}=P_t(1+i)^k.
> }
> $$

Untuk bergerak mundur:

$$
P_{t-k}=P_t v^k.
$$

---

### 2.11 Change in Outstanding Balance = Principal Repaid

Karena

$$
B_t=B_{t-1}-P_t,
$$

maka:

$$
\boxed{
P_t=B_{t-1}-B_t.
}
$$

Untuk beberapa payments dari $a+1$ sampai $b$:

$$
\boxed{
\sum_{t=a+1}^{b}P_t=B_a-B_b.
}
$$

Ini sering lebih cepat daripada menghitung principal satu per satu.

---

### 2.12 Interest Paid over a Block of Payments

Total payments pada interval = total interest + total principal.

Maka untuk payments dari $a+1$ sampai $b$:

$$
\sum_{t=a+1}^{b} I_t
=
\sum_{t=a+1}^{b}R_t-(B_a-B_b).
$$

Untuk level payments $R$:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Interest over block}
=
(b-a)R-(B_a-B_b).
}
$$

Vaaler secara eksplisit menggunakan observation ini dalam mortgage example:

> interest paid over a period = total payments over the period − decrease in outstanding balance.

Untuk seluruh fully amortized loan:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Total Interest}=nR-L.
}
$$

---

### 2.13 Irregular Final Payment

Jika currency rounding atau contractual structure membuat final payment tidak sama dengan regular payment, jangan memaksakan $R$.

Jika setelah payment ke-$n-1$ balance adalah $B_{n-1}$, exact payoff satu period kemudian adalah:

$$
\boxed{
R_n=B_{n-1}(1+i).
}
$$

Jika $R_n<R$, final payment merupakan **drop payment**.

Vaaler menunjukkan bahwa small rounding differences dapat muncul ketika scheduled payments dibulatkan ke cents. Prinsip ekonominya tetap: final payment disesuaikan agar final balance menjadi zero.

## Section 3 — How It Works

### 3.1 Universal Amortization Workflow

```text
1. Tentukan payment period
        ↓
2. Konversi rate ke effective rate per payment period
        ↓
3. Tentukan timing: balance before atau after payment?
        ↓
4. Jika perlu, cari level payment dari L = R a_n
        ↓
5. Pilih metode balance:
   prospective / retrospective / recursion
        ↓
6. Interest = rate × beginning balance
        ↓
7. Principal = payment − interest
        ↓
8. New balance = old balance − principal
        ↓
9. Verify maturity balance = 0
```

### 3.2 Memilih Metode Balance

| Informasi Soal | Metode Biasanya Paling Cepat |
|---|---|
| Future remaining level payments diketahui | Prospective |
| Original loan + past payments diketahui | Retrospective |
| Diminta satu baris setelah baris balance yang diketahui | Recursion |
| Diminta principal payment ke-$t$ pada level loan | Direct $P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}$ |
| Diberi principal pada satu payment dan diminta payment lain | Geometric principal relationship |
| Diminta total interest beberapa periods | Payments minus balance decrease |

### 3.3 Amortization Schedule Mechanics

Standard table:

| Period | Payment | Interest | Principal | Outstanding Balance |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $0$ | — | — | — | $L$ |
| $1$ | $R$ | $iB_0$ | $R-iB_0$ | $B_1$ |
| $2$ | $R$ | $iB_1$ | $R-iB_1$ | $B_2$ |
| $\vdots$ | $\vdots$ | $\vdots$ | $\vdots$ | $\vdots$ |
| $n$ | final payment | interest due | remaining principal | $0$ |

Setiap row mengikuti:

$$
I_t=iB_{t-1},
$$

$$
P_t=R_t-I_t,
$$

$$
B_t=B_{t-1}-P_t.
$$

atau langsung:

$$
B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t.
$$

## Section 4 — Worked Examples & Exam Cases

### Case A — Basic Amortization: Interest, Principal, dan Balance

[TEXTBOOK EXAMPLE — adapted from Vaaler Example 5.2.1]

Sebuah loan sebesar $2{,}000$ dikenai compound interest $5\%$ per tahun. Borrower membayar $800$ pada akhir tahun pertama dan $1{,}000$ pada akhir tahun ketiga. Tentukan principal portion masing-masing payment dan balance setelah payment kedua.

#### Payment 1 — akhir tahun 1

Interest:

$$
I_1=2{,}000(0.05)=100.
$$

Principal:

$$
P_1=800-100=700.
$$

Balance setelah payment:

$$
B_1=2{,}000-700=1{,}300.
$$

#### Payment 2 — akhir tahun 3

Ada **dua tahun** antara payment pertama dan kedua.

Balance $1{,}300$ tumbuh menjadi:

$$
1{,}300(1.05)^2.
$$

Interest accrued selama dua tahun:

$$
I
=
1{,}300\left[(1.05)^2-1\right]
=
133.25.
$$

Principal pada payment $1{,}000$:

$$
P
=
1{,}000-133.25
=
866.75.
$$

Balance baru:

$$
B
=
1{,}300-866.75
=
433.25.
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
P_1=700,\quad
P_2=866.75,\quad
B=433.25.
}
$$

> [!WARNING] Exam Trap — Case A
> Formula $I_t=iB_{t-1}$ hanya langsung berlaku jika interval antara balance dan payment adalah **satu interest period**. Di sini payment kedua berjarak dua tahun, sehingga interest harus memakai
>
> $$
> B\left[(1+i)^2-1\right].
> $$

### Case B — Constructing an Amortization Schedule

[TEXTBOOK EXAMPLE — adapted from Vaaler Example 5.2.4]

Loan:

$$
L=20{,}000.
$$

Annual effective **discount rate**:

$$
d=4.8\%.
$$

Karena schedule membutuhkan effective interest rate:

$$
i=\frac{d}{1-d}
=
\frac{0.048}{0.952}
\approx 0.0504201681.
$$

Payments:

- tahun 1–3: $5{,}645.75$;
- tahun 4: $5{,}645.72$.

#### Tahun 1

Interest:

$$
I_1=20{,}000i\approx1{,}008.40.
$$

Principal:

$$
P_1=5{,}645.75-1{,}008.40=4{,}637.35.
$$

Balance:

$$
B_1=20{,}000-4{,}637.35=15{,}362.65.
$$

#### Shortcut principal progression

Untuk regular payments sebelum final adjustment:

$$
P_2\approx P_1(1+i)
\approx4{,}871.16,
$$

$$
P_3\approx P_2(1+i)
\approx5{,}116.77.
$$

| Year | Payment | Interest | Principal | Balance |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | — | — | — | $20{,}000.00$ |
| 1 | $5{,}645.75$ | $1{,}008.40$ | $4{,}637.35$ | $15{,}362.65$ |
| 2 | $5{,}645.75$ | $774.59$ | $4{,}871.16$ | $10{,}491.49$ |
| 3 | $5{,}645.75$ | $528.98$ | $5{,}116.77$ | $5{,}374.72$ |
| 4 | $5{,}645.72$ | $271.00$ | $5{,}374.72$ | $0.00$ |

### Case C — Outstanding Balance: Prospective vs Retrospective

Misalkan:

$$
L=100{,}000,
\qquad
i=6\%,
\qquad
n=10,
$$

dan loan dilunasi dengan level annual payments.

Payment:

$$
R=\frac{100{,}000}{a_{\overline{10}|0.06}}.
$$

Misalkan kita ingin balance immediately after payment ke-4.

#### Prospective

Masih tersisa $6$ payments:

$$
\boxed{
B_4
=
R\,a_{\overline{6}|0.06}.
}
$$

#### Retrospective

Original loan accumulated 4 years, dikurangi accumulated value 4 payments pertama:

$$
\boxed{
B_4
=
100{,}000(1.06)^4
-
R\,s_{\overline{4}|0.06}.
}
$$

Kedua formula harus memberi result yang sama.

> [!TIP] Exam Speed
> Jika $R$ sudah diketahui dan remaining payments langsung terlihat, prospective hampir selalu lebih pendek.

### Case D — Principal Portion Tanpa Membuat Schedule

Loan fully amortized oleh $n=9$ annual level payments sebesar $R=1{,}360.49$ pada $i=8\%$.

Cari principal portion payment pertama.

Gunakan direct formula:

$$
P_1
=
Rv^9.
$$

Dengan

$$
v=(1.08)^{-1},
$$

maka:

$$
P_1
=
1{,}360.49(1.08)^{-9}
\approx680.6.
$$

Jadi:

$$
\boxed{
P_1\approx681.
}
$$

> [!WARNING] Formula Trap
> Payment pertama menggunakan exponent $n$, karena:
>
> $$
> P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}.
> $$
>
> Untuk $t=1$:
>
> $$
> n-1+1=n.
> $$

### Case E — Principal Portions dari Geometric Progression

Suppose principal portion payment ke-3 adalah $5{,}000$ dan rate per payment period $i=4\%$.

Principal portion payment ke-7:

$$
P_7=P_3(1.04)^{7-3}.
$$

Maka:

$$
\boxed{
P_7=5{,}000(1.04)^4.
}
$$

Tidak perlu mengetahui:

- original loan;
- payment amount;
- total term.

> [!TIP] Shortcut Recognition
> Jika soal memberi **dua principal portions pada payment numbers berbeda**, pikirkan:
>
> $$
> P_{t+k}=P_t(1+i)^k.
> $$

### Case F — Interest selama Beberapa Payments

Misalkan payments ke-56 sampai ke-67 masing-masing sebesar $R$. Balance sebelum payment 56 adalah $B_{55}$ dan balance setelah payment 67 adalah $B_{67}$.

Ada 12 payments. Total principal repaid:

$$
B_{55}-B_{67}.
$$

Maka total interest:

$$
\boxed{
12R-(B_{55}-B_{67}).
}
$$

Ini adalah metode yang digunakan Vaaler untuk menghitung mortgage interest selama satu calendar year tanpa menjumlahkan 12 interest portions satu per satu.

### Case G — Final Drop Payment

Misalkan setelah payment ke-11 balance adalah

$$
B_{11}=900
$$

dan effective rate per period adalah $5\%$.

Jika loan dilunasi satu period kemudian:

$$
R_{12}
=
900(1.05)
=
945.
$$

Jika regular payment adalah $1{,}000$, maka:

$$
945<1{,}000
$$

sehingga $945$ adalah **drop payment**.

Sanity check:

$$
900(1.05)-945=0.
$$

## Section 5 — Verification & Sanity Check

> [!CHECK] Sanity Check
> Untuk standard positive-rate fully amortized level loan:
>
> 1. **Balance harus turun menuju nol**
>
> $$
> B_0>B_1>\cdots>B_n=0
> $$
>
> selama regular payment cukup besar untuk amortize loan.
>
> 2. **Interest portion biasanya turun**
>
> Karena
>
> $$
> I_t=iB_{t-1},
> $$
>
> dan balance turun.
>
> 3. **Principal portion naik**
>
> Karena
>
> $$
> P_{t+1}=(1+i)P_t.
> $$
>
> 4. **Payment decomposition harus reconcile**
>
> $$
> R_t=I_t+P_t.
> $$
>
> 5. **Balance change harus sama dengan principal**
>
> $$
> B_{t-1}-B_t=P_t.
> $$
>
> 6. **Prospective dan retrospective harus sama**
>
> $$
> PV_t(\text{future payments})
> =
> AV_t(L)-AV_t(\text{past payments}).
> $$
>
> 7. **Total principal seluruh loan = original loan**
>
> $$
> \sum_{t=1}^{n}P_t=L.
> $$
>
> 8. **Total interest seluruh loan**
>
> $$
> \sum_{t=1}^{n}I_t=nR-L
> $$
>
> untuk $n$ identical payments tanpa additional cash flows.

### Metode Alternatif

#### Jika diminta $B_t$

**Prospective**

$$
B_t=R a_{\overline{n-t}|}.
$$

Paling cepat jika remaining payments sederhana.

**Retrospective**

$$
B_t=L(1+i)^t-Rs_{\overline{t}|}.
$$

Paling cepat jika original loan dan past payments lebih mudah.

**Recursion**

$$
B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R.
$$

Paling cepat jika balance sebelumnya diketahui.

#### Jika diminta $P_t$

Cara 1:

$$
P_t=R-iB_{t-1}.
$$

Cara 2, standard level loan:

$$
P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}.
$$

Cara 3, jika principal payment lain diketahui:

$$
P_t=P_k(1+i)^{t-k}.
$$

Pilih cara dengan unknown paling sedikit.

## Section 6 — Connections & Mental Model

### Connection ke [[2.1 Annuity-Immediate and Annuity-Due]]

Standard amortized loan dengan end-of-period level payments adalah annuity-immediate dari lender perspective:

$$
L=R a_{\overline{n}|}.
$$

Jadi amortization bukan teori terpisah; ia adalah annuity valuation ditambah **tracking balance dan decomposition payment**.

### Connection ke [[1.2 Effective, Nominal, and Force of Interest]]

Semua amortization formulas menganggap $i$ adalah rate yang compatible dengan payment period.

Jika annual nominal rate convertible monthly adalah $i^{(12)}$, maka monthly rate:

$$
i_m=\frac{i^{(12)}}{12}.
$$

Tetapi jika diberikan annual effective rate $i_{\text{ann}}$, monthly effective equivalent rate adalah:

$$
i_m=(1+i_{\text{ann}})^{1/12}-1.
$$

Jangan membagi annual effective rate dengan 12.

### Connection ke [[4.1 Loan Terminology]]

Topik 4.1 memberi bahasa:

- payment;
- interest;
- principal;
- balance;
- drop/balloon.

Topik 4.2 membuat semuanya computational.

### Connection ke [[4.3 Sinking Fund Method]]

Amortization:

```text
Payment → interest to lender + direct principal reduction
```

Sinking fund:

```text
Interest to lender
+
deposit to separate fund
```

Jangan memakai amortization balance recursion pada fund account tanpa menyesuaikan struktur.

### Connection ke [[5.2 Book Value, Premium and Discount Amortization]]

Bond book value recursion memiliki bentuk matematis sangat mirip:

$$
\text{new value}
=
\text{old value}(1+\text{yield})
-
\text{cash payment}.
$$

Karena itu pemahaman loan amortization sangat membantu bond amortization.

## Section 7 — Exam Traps & Misconceptions

### Timing Trap

> [!BUG] Timing Trap
> Jika $B_t$ didefinisikan **immediately after payment ke-$t$**, maka interest pada payment ke-$(t+1)$ adalah
>
> $$
> I_{t+1}=iB_t.
> $$
>
> Bukan $iB_{t+1}$.

### Before vs After Payment

> [!BUG] Timing Trap
> Jika $B_{t-1}$ adalah after-payment balance:
>
> **immediately before payment $t$:**
>
> $$
> B_{t-1}(1+i).
> $$
>
> **immediately after payment $t$:**
>
> $$
> B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t.
> $$

### Rate & Frequency Trap

> [!BUG] Rate & Frequency Trap
> Monthly payment tidak boleh langsung memakai annual effective rate.
>
> Jika annual effective $i$:
>
> $$
> i_{\text{monthly}}=(1+i)^{1/12}-1.
> $$
>
> Jika nominal convertible monthly $i^{(12)}$:
>
> $$
> i_{\text{monthly}}=\frac{i^{(12)}}{12}.
> $$

### Formula Trap — Principal Exponent

> [!BUG] Formula Trap
> **Salah:**
>
> $$
> P_t=Rv^{n-t}
> $$
>
> **Benar:**
>
> $$
> \boxed{
> P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}.
> }
> $$

Cara cek: pada final payment $t=n$ harus memperoleh

$$
P_n=Rv,
$$

bukan $R$.

### Prospective Off-by-One

> [!BUG] Formula Trap
> Setelah payment ke-$t$, jumlah remaining payments adalah
>
> $$
> n-t.
> $$
>
> Maka:
>
> $$
> B_t=R a_{\overline{n-t}|}.
> $$

### Interest = Rate × Payment

> [!BUG] Concept Trap
> Interest portion bukan:
>
> $$
> iR.
> $$
>
> Interest dihitung dari **outstanding balance**:
>
> $$
> I_t=iB_{t-1}.
> $$

### Menghitung Seluruh Schedule Padahal Tidak Perlu

> [!BUG] Calculation Trap
> Jika hanya diminta satu principal payment pada standard level loan, membuat $n$ baris schedule biasanya membuang waktu.
>
> Gunakan:
>
> $$
> P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}.
> $$

### Rounding Too Early

> [!BUG] Calculation Trap
> Vaaler menunjukkan schedule yang final payment-nya berbeda beberapa cents akibat rounding.
>
> Simpan precision intermediate, lalu round di akhir atau sesuai contractual cash amount yang diberikan.

### Negative Amortization

> [!IMPORTANT] Important Distinction
> Jika suatu payment lebih kecil daripada interest accrued, maka:
>
> $$
> P_t=R_t-I_t<0.
> $$
>
> Balance justru **naik**.
>
> Jadi “principal portion selalu positif” bukan mathematical identity. Ia positif pada standard fully amortizing structure dengan sufficiently large payment.

## Section 8 — Formula Sheet

### Core Formula Map

| Quantity | Formula | Kapan Dipakai |
|---|---|---|
| Level loan equation | $L=R a_{\overline{n}|i}$ | fully amortized, level end-of-period payments |
| Level payment | $R=L/a_{\overline{n}|i}$ | $L,n,i$ known |
| Balance recursion | $B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t$ | one-step roll-forward |
| Interest portion | $I_t=iB_{t-1}$ | one payment period after $B_{t-1}$ |
| Principal portion | $P_t=R_t-I_t$ | general amortized payment |
| Balance via principal | $B_t=B_{t-1}-P_t$ | after payment |
| Prospective balance | $B_t=R a_{\overline{n-t}|i}$ | level loan |
| Retrospective balance | $B_t=L(1+i)^t-Rs_{\overline{t}|i}$ | level loan |
| Direct interest portion | $I_t=R(1-v^{n-t+1})$ | standard level fully amortized loan |
| Direct principal portion | $P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}$ | standard level fully amortized loan |
| Principal progression | $P_{t+1}=(1+i)P_t$ | consecutive standard payments |
| $k$-step principal | $P_{t+k}=P_t(1+i)^k$ | principal another period |
| Principal over block | $B_a-B_b$ | payments $a+1$ through $b$ |
| Interest over block | $\sum R_t-(B_a-B_b)$ | block of payments |
| Total interest | $nR-L$ | identical payments, no other cash flows |
| Exact final payoff | $B_{n-1}(1+i)$ | one period after penultimate payment |

### Three Methods for Outstanding Balance

$$
\boxed{
\text{Prospective: }
B_t=PV_t(\text{future payments})
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{Retrospective: }
B_t=AV_t(L)-AV_t(\text{past payments})
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{Recursion: }
B_t=B_{t-1}(1+i)-R_t
}
$$

### Standard Level Loan Shortcuts

$$
\boxed{
I_t=R(1-v^{n-t+1})
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
P_t=Rv^{n-t+1}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
P_{t+1}=(1+i)P_t
}
$$

## Section 10 — Exam Pattern Map

[TEXTBOOK-BASED EXPECTATION]

| Narasi Soal | Keyword | Konsep | Formula / Rule | Langkah Cepat | Trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cari payment dari loan amount | “repaid by $n$ equal payments” | Loan as annuity | $L=Ra_{\overline{n}|}$ | samakan rate period → solve $R$ | frequency mismatch |
| Balance setelah payment ke-$t$ | “outstanding balance after” | Prospective | $B_t=Ra_{\overline{n-t}|}$ | hitung remaining payments | pakai $n-t+1$ |
| Balance dari original loan dan past payments | “after $t$ payments” | Retrospective | $B_t=L(1+i)^t-Rs_{\overline{t}|}$ | accumulate loan minus past payments | salah focal date |
| Interest payment ke-$t$ | “interest in the $t$th payment” | Beginning balance | $I_t=iB_{t-1}$ | cari $B_{t-1}$ dulu | $i\times R$ |
| Principal payment ke-$t$ | “principal repaid” | Payment decomposition | $P_t=R-I_t$ | atau direct $Rv^{n-t+1}$ | exponent off-by-one |
| Principal payment lain diketahui | “principal in payment $k$” | Geometric progression | $P_{t+k}=P_t(1+i)^k$ | ratio langsung | membuat full schedule |
| Total principal beberapa payments | “principal repaid during…” | Balance decrease | $B_a-B_b$ | subtract balances | menjumlahkan semua row |
| Total interest beberapa payments | “interest paid during…” | Payment minus principal | total payments $-(B_a-B_b)$ | count payments → subtract balance decrease | salah endpoint balance |
| Final slightly smaller payment | “final reduced payment” | Exact payoff | $B(1+i)$ | accrue residual once | memakai regular $R$ |
| Payment < interest due | “small initial payments” | Negative amortization | $P_t<0$ | compare $R_t$ vs $I_t$ | menganggap balance selalu turun |
| Rate quoted as discount | “effective discount rate” | Rate conversion | $i=d/(1-d)$ | convert before schedule | gunakan $d$ sebagai $i$ |
| Monthly payments + annual effective rate | “monthly” | Frequency alignment | $(1+i_a)^{1/12}-1$ | convert first | divide by 12 |
